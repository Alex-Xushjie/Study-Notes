# Reading Guide

[← All 32 chapters](README.md#browse-by-chapter) · [Find a word](indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Book-style reading edition

Open [the reading edition](reader/index.html) in a web browser. It lists all chapters and includes word search.

## Choose a chapter

Open the [dictionary home](README.md#browse-by-chapter). All 32 chapters are listed there, grouped into eight parts. Select any chapter title to open it.

## Read a chapter

Press **Ctrl+Shift+V** in VS Code to switch to Markdown preview. Each chapter page links to its topic pages and lists its words.

On a page with a numbered picture, match the numbers to the word list immediately below the picture, then read the examples. Some abstract topics are currently text-first while their illustrations are being discussed.

## Look up a word

Select a word to open its detailed entry. To find a term across the dictionary, open the [English index](indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md) or use VS Code search.

## What are the folders for?

- `entries` holds the individual word pages. Open them by selecting words on chapter pages.
- `assets` holds images used by the pages.
- `templates` holds material for writing new entries.

You can read from the chapter links without opening these folders yourself.
