# subnetting

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 07

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The division of an address block into smaller network prefixes.

## Usage and distinctions

Subnet planning depends on address family, prefix size, and operational requirements.

## Examples

> The team is subnetting the allocated address block.

> Check the number and size of the required subnets.
