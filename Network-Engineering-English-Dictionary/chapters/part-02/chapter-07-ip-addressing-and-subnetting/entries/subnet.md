# subnet

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 07

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A division of an IP address space identified by a network prefix.

## Usage and distinctions

A subnet and a VLAN are different concepts, though a design may associate an IP subnet with a VLAN.

## Examples

> The servers are in a separate subnet.

> Confirm the subnet used by the management interface.
