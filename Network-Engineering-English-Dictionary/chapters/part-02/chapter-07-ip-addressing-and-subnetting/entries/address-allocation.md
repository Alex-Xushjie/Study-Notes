# address allocation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 07

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The assignment of an address block to an organization, network, or purpose.

## Usage and distinctions

Allocation of a block and assignment of an individual address are related but distinct actions.

## Examples

> The provider confirmed the address allocation.

> Document the allocation and its intended use.
