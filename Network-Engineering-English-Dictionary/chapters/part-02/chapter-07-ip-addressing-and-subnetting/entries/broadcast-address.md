# IPv4 directed broadcast address

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 07

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An IPv4 address with all host bits set to one for a subnet.

## Usage and distinctions

Directed broadcasts are subject to device policy and may be disabled. IPv6 does not use broadcast addresses.

## Examples

> 192.0.2.255 is the directed broadcast address of 192.0.2.0/24.

> Do not assign the broadcast address as an ordinary host address.
