# multicast source

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A sender that transmits traffic addressed to a multicast group.

## Usage and distinctions

A source can send to a group without being a receiver of that group.

## Examples

> The multicast source sends the market-data feed.

> Confirm the source address and group address.
