# IGMP version 2

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A version of IGMP that adds mechanisms including explicit leave messages and group-specific queries.

## Usage and distinctions

IGMPv2 does not provide the source filtering introduced by IGMPv3.

## Examples

> The receiver subnet is operating with IGMPv2.
