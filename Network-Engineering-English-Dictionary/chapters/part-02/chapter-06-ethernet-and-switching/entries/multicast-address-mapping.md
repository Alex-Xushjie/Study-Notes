# IPv4 multicast-to-Ethernet mapping

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The mapping of IPv4 multicast group addresses to Ethernet multicast MAC addresses.

## Usage and distinctions

The mapping is not one-to-one; multiple IPv4 groups can share a mapped Ethernet MAC address.

## Examples

> The switch receives a frame using the mapped multicast MAC.

> Check for MAC-address aliasing between the groups.
