[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A version of IGMP that supports reporting multicast group membership with source filtering.

## Usage and distinctions

Source-filtering behavior depends on the reported mode and source list.
