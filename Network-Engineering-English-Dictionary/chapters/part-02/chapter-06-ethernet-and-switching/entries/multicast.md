# multicast

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Traffic addressed to a group of interested receivers.

## Usage and distinctions

Multicast delivery behavior depends on Layer 2 and Layer 3 mechanisms and configuration.

## Examples

> The application sends multicast traffic to a group.

> Check multicast membership and forwarding state.
