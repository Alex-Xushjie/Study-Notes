# source MAC address

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The link-layer address identifying the sender of an Ethernet frame.

## Usage and distinctions

The source address is used by learning bridges to associate addresses with ingress ports in the relevant VLAN.

## Examples

> The switch learns the source MAC address on the incoming port.

> Check the source MAC address in the captured frame.
