# MAC address aging

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The removal of a dynamic MAC address entry after it has not been refreshed for a configured period.

## Usage and distinctions

Aging behavior depends on platform and configuration; static entries may behave differently.

## Examples

> The dynamic MAC address entry aged out.

> Check the aging time and whether the host is sending traffic.
