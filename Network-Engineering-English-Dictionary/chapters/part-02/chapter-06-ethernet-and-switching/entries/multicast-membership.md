# multicast group membership

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The state indicating that a host or interface has requested traffic for a multicast group.

## Usage and distinctions

Membership behavior depends on the IP version, protocol version, and source-filtering mode.

## Examples

> The switch observes multicast group membership on the port.

> Check membership on the receiver-facing interface.
