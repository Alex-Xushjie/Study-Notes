# trunk port

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A switch port configured to carry traffic for multiple VLANs.

## Usage and distinctions

In 802.1Q networks tags distinguish VLAN traffic, with handling of native or untagged traffic depending on configuration.

## Examples

> The trunk port carries VLANs 10 and 20 between switches.

> Verify the allowed VLAN list on both ends of the trunk.
