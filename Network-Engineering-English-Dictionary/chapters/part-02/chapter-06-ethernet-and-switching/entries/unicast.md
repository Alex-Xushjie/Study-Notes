# unicast

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Traffic addressed to one intended network endpoint.

## Usage and distinctions

A unicast address identifies a single intended endpoint; network delivery conditions still matter.

## Examples

> The switch forwards known unicast traffic toward one port.

> Check whether the destination MAC address is unicast.
