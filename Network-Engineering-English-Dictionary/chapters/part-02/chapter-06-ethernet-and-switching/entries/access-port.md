# access port

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A switch port configured to carry traffic for an access VLAN, usually without VLAN tags toward the attached device.

## Usage and distinctions

Port behavior varies by configuration, including features such as voice VLANs; do not assume every frame is always untagged.

## Examples

> The workstation is connected to an access port in VLAN 10.

> Check the access VLAN on the port.
