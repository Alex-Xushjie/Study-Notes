# virtual LAN

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical segmentation of a Layer 2 network into separate broadcast domains.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: VLAN. VLAN IDs and forwarding scope must be considered within the particular network design.

## Examples

> The user ports are assigned to VLAN 10.

> Verify that the VLAN exists on both switches.
