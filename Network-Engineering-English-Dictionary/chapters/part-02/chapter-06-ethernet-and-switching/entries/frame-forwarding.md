# frame forwarding

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The process of selecting an output interface or interfaces for a received frame.

## Usage and distinctions

Forwarding also depends on port state, VLAN membership, policy, and platform behavior.

## Examples

> The switch forwards the frame according to its destination address.

> Check the VLAN and MAC table when investigating frame forwarding.
