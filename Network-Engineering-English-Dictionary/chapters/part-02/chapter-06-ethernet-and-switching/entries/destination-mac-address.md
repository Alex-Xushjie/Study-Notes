# destination MAC address

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The link-layer address identifying the intended receiver or group for an Ethernet frame.

## Usage and distinctions

Destination MAC addresses are link-layer addresses, not IP destination addresses.

## Examples

> The switch looks up the destination MAC address.

> The destination MAC address may be unicast, multicast, or broadcast.
