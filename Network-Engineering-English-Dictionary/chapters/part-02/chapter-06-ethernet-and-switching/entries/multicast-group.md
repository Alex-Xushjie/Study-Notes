# multicast group

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical group identified by a multicast address to which traffic can be sent.

## Usage and distinctions

A group address identifies a delivery group; membership and forwarding depend on the relevant protocols.

## Examples

> The receiver joins the multicast group.

> Check the group address used by the application.
