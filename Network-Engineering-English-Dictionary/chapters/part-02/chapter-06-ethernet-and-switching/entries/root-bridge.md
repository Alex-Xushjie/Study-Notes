# root bridge

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The bridge selected as the reference point for a spanning-tree topology.

## Usage and distinctions

Root selection depends on bridge identifiers and the spanning-tree variant or instance.

## Examples

> The distribution switch is the root bridge for the VLAN.

> Verify the root bridge after the maintenance.
