# unknown unicast

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A unicast frame whose destination MAC address is not present in the relevant forwarding table.

## Usage and distinctions

Flooding is subject to device behavior and configuration; it is not the same as a broadcast destination address.

## Examples

> The switch floods the unknown unicast frame within the VLAN.

> Check why the destination MAC address has not been learned.
