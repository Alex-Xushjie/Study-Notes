# Link Aggregation Control Protocol

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A protocol used by devices to negotiate and maintain an aggregated link.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: LACP. Both ends must have compatible aggregation and interface settings.

## Examples

> LACP is enabled on both ends of the port channel.

> Check the LACP neighbor and member states.
