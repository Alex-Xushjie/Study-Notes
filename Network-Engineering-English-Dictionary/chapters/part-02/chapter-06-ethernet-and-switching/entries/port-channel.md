# port channel

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical interface aggregating multiple physical links between compatible devices.

## Usage and distinctions

Vendor terminology varies; traffic distribution depends on the selected hashing method and configuration.
