# Ethernet

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A family of networking technologies that defines data-link and physical-layer specifications.

## Usage and distinctions

Ethernet includes many physical media and data rates; it is not synonymous with copper cable.

## Examples

> Ethernet connects the hosts to the access switch.

> Verify that the interfaces support the required Ethernet rate.
