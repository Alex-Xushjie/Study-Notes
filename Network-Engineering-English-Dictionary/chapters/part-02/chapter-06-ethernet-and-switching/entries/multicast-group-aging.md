# multicast membership aging

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The expiration of learned multicast membership or forwarding state after it is not refreshed.

## Usage and distinctions

Aging timers and behavior depend on the protocol version and switch implementation.
