# multicast packet replication

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The creation of packet copies for multiple outgoing multicast paths.

## Usage and distinctions

Replication occurs where forwarding paths diverge, subject to protocol state and platform behavior.

## Examples

> The router replicates the packet at a branch point.
