# unknown multicast

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Multicast traffic for which a switch lacks a matching forwarding entry in the relevant context.

## Usage and distinctions

Forwarding or filtering behavior is platform- and configuration-specific.

## Examples
