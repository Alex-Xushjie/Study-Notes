# broadcast

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Traffic addressed for delivery to all recipients within a defined broadcast domain.

## Usage and distinctions

Ethernet broadcast uses the all-ones destination MAC address; routers normally do not forward Layer 2 broadcasts as broadcasts.

## Examples

> An ARP request is commonly sent as a local Ethernet broadcast.

> Excessive broadcast traffic can affect a broadcast domain.
