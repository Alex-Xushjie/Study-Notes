# multicast MAC aliasing

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The situation in which different multicast IP groups map to the same Ethernet multicast MAC address.

## Usage and distinctions

Aliasing follows from the IPv4 multicast-to-Ethernet mapping and does not mean the IP groups are identical.

## Examples

> The two multicast groups share a mapped Ethernet MAC address.

> Consider MAC aliasing when investigating Layer 2 forwarding.
