# native VLAN

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The VLAN associated with untagged traffic on a trunk in common 802.1Q implementations.

## Usage and distinctions

Terminology and handling can vary by platform and configuration; verify the device documentation.

## Examples

> The native VLAN must be configured consistently across the trunk.

> Check for an unexpected native VLAN mismatch.
