# Internet Group Management Protocol

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A protocol used by IPv4 hosts and multicast routers to communicate group membership information.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: IGMP. IPv6 uses Multicast Listener Discovery rather than IGMP.

## Examples

> The receiver sends an IGMP membership report.

> Check the IGMP version on the receiver subnet.
