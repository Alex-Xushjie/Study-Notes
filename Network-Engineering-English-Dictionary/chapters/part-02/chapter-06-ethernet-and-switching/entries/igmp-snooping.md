# IGMP snooping

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 06

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A switch function that observes IGMP messages to control multicast forwarding within a VLAN.

## Usage and distinctions

Snooping behavior and handling of unknown multicast depend on platform and configuration.
