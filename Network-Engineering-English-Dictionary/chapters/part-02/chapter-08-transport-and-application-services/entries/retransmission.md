# TCP retransmission

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 08

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The sending of TCP data again when it has not been acknowledged as expected.

## Usage and distinctions

A retransmission is a symptom; it does not on its own identify where or why the loss occurred.

## Examples

> The capture shows repeated TCP retransmissions.

> Investigate packet loss, delay, and endpoint behavior.
