# TCP three-way handshake

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 08

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The exchange of SYN, SYN-ACK, and ACK used to synchronize TCP connection state.

## Usage and distinctions

The handshake establishes TCP connection state; it does not prove that the application response will succeed.
