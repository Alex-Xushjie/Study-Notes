# TCP acknowledgment

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 08

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Information indicating the next sequence number expected by a TCP receiver.

## Usage and distinctions

TCP acknowledgments refer to sequence numbers in the byte stream; ACK is also the name of a TCP flag.

## Examples

> The receiver sends an acknowledgment for the received data.

> Check whether acknowledgments are arriving.
