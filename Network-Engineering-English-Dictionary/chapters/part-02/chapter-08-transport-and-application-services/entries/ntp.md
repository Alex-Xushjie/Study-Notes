# Network Time Protocol

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 08

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A protocol used to synchronize clocks between systems over a network.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: NTP. Correct time supports troubleshooting, but timestamps also require a known time zone or UTC reference.

## Examples

> The router synchronizes its clock with an NTP server.

> Check the time source before correlating logs.
