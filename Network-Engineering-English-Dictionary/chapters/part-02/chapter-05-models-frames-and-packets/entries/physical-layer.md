# physical layer

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 05

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The layer concerned with transmitting signals over a physical medium.

## Usage and distinctions

The OSI physical layer is Layer 1. Exact signaling and media depend on the applicable standard.

## Examples

> A damaged cable can affect the physical layer.

> Check the physical layer before investigating higher-layer symptoms.
