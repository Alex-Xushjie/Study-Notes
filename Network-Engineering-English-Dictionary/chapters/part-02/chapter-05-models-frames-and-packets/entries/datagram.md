# datagram

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 05

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A self-contained unit of data handled by a datagram-oriented protocol.

## Usage and distinctions

The term is also used for IP datagrams. UDP does not itself guarantee delivery or ordering.

## Examples

> The application sends a UDP datagram.

> The packet capture shows a UDP datagram.
