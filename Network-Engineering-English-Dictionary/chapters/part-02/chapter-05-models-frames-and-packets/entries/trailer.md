# protocol trailer

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 05

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Protocol information placed after the associated data.

## Usage and distinctions

Not every protocol has a trailer. A capture may omit the Ethernet frame check sequence depending on the interface and capture method.

## Examples

> An Ethernet frame includes a frame check sequence in its trailer.

> The device checks the received frame for errors.
