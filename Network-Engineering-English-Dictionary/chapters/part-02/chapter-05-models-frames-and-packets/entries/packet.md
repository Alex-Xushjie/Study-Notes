# packet

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 05

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A formatted unit of data transmitted across a packet-switched network.

## Usage and distinctions

Packet is used broadly; an IP packet is a specific network-layer unit.

## Examples

> The router forwards the IP packet.

> Capture the packet to inspect its source and destination addresses.
