# decapsulation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 05

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The process of removing or interpreting protocol encapsulation as data is received or processed.

## Usage and distinctions

A forwarding device may remove and replace link-layer encapsulation at each hop; it does not always process application data.

## Examples

> The receiving host decapsulates the frame and processes the IP packet.

> The device removes the link-layer framing before processing the next layer.
