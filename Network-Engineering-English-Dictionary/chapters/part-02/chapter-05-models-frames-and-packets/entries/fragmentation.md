# IP fragmentation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 05

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The division of an IP packet into smaller fragments to meet transmission constraints.

## Usage and distinctions

IPv4 routers may fragment under specified conditions; IPv6 routers do not fragment packets in transit.

## Examples

> An IPv4 router may fragment a packet when allowed and necessary.

> Check whether fragmentation is affecting the application.
