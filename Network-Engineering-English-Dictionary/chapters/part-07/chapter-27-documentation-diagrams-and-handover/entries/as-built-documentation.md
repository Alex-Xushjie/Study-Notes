**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Records updated to reflect the completed installation.

## Usage and distinctions

As-built records should identify the revision date and unresolved discrepancies.
