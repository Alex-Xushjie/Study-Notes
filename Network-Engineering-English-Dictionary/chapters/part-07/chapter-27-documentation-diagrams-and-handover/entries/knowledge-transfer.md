# knowledge transfer

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The communication of information and skills needed by a receiving team.

## Usage and distinctions

Knowledge transfer is more effective when supported by current documentation and practical exercises.

## Examples

> The implementation team provides knowledge transfer to operations.

> Record the topics and outstanding questions.
