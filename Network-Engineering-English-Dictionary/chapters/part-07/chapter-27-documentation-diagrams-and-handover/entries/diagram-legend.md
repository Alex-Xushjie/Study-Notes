# diagram legend

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An explanation of symbols, colors, or line styles used in a diagram.

## Usage and distinctions

A legend makes conventions explicit and supports accessibility.

## Examples

> The diagram legend explains the dashed lines.

> Do not rely on color alone to distinguish links.
