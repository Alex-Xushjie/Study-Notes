# handover meeting

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A meeting to transfer knowledge, responsibilities, and outstanding information to a receiving team.

## Usage and distinctions

A meeting alone does not ensure that documents, access, and responsibilities have been transferred.

## Examples

> The project team scheduled the handover meeting.

> Review the open issues and support contacts.
