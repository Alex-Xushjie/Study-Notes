# support boundary

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The agreed division of responsibility between support teams or organizations.

## Usage and distinctions

Physical demarcation and operational responsibilities may not be identical.

## Examples

> The support boundary is the provider handoff.

> Clarify which team owns the firewall policy.
