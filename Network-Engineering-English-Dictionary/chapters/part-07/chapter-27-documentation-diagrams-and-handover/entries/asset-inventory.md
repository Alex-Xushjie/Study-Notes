# asset inventory

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A record of equipment and its identifiers, ownership, location, or lifecycle information.

## Usage and distinctions

An inventory should distinguish observed hardware, assigned asset tags, and administrative ownership.

## Examples

> The asset inventory includes the router serial number.

> Confirm the owner and location in the inventory.
