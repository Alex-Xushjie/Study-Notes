# handover open item

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An unresolved task or issue recorded for follow-up during handover.

## Usage and distinctions

The receiving team should understand impact, priority, and acceptance of each open item.

## Examples

> The missing label is an open handover item.

> Assign an owner and target date.
