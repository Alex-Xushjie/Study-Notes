# cabling schedule

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A record of cables, endpoints, identifiers, and related installation details.

## Usage and distinctions

The schedule can include media type and test results where required.

## Examples

> The cabling schedule lists both ends of the fiber link.

> Check the cable label against the schedule.
