# operations runbook

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A documented procedure for performing a defined operational task.

## Usage and distinctions

A runbook should include prerequisites, expected results, escalation, and rollback where relevant.

## Examples

> The runbook describes the gateway failover checks.

> Verify that the runbook matches the current platform version.
