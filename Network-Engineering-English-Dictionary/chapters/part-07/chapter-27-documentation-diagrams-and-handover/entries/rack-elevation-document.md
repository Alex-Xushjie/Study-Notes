# rack elevation diagram

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A drawing of equipment positions within a rack.

## Usage and distinctions

U numbering, device height, and front or rear orientation should be clear.

## Examples

> The rack elevation shows the switch at U18.

> Update the elevation after moving the equipment.
