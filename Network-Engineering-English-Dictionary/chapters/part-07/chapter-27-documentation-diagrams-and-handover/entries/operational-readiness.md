# operational readiness

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The condition in which a service and its support arrangements are prepared for operation.

## Usage and distinctions

Readiness criteria should be agreed and verified for the service.

## Examples

> The readiness review checks monitoring and support ownership.

> Confirm the runbooks, access, and escalation paths.
