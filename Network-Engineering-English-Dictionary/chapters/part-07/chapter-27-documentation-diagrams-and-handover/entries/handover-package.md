# handover package

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A collection of information and records transferred to the receiving team.

## Usage and distinctions

The required contents depend on the project and receiving team.

## Examples

> The handover package includes the topology and test results.

> Confirm that support contacts and outstanding issues are included.
