# document owner

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The person or team accountable for maintaining a document.

## Usage and distinctions

Ownership should remain current when teams or responsibilities change.

## Examples

> The operations team is the document owner.

> Ask the owner to review the updated runbook.
