# high-level design

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 27

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A document or description outlining major architecture choices and relationships.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: HLD. The expected detail and structure depend on the organization.

## Examples

> The high-level design shows the site connectivity.

> Review the design against the business requirements.
