# project risk

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An uncertain event or condition that could affect project objectives.

## Usage and distinctions

A risk is uncertain; an issue is a current problem requiring attention.

## Examples

> The delayed circuit delivery is a project risk.

> Record the likelihood, impact, and owner.
