# requirements stakeholder

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A person or group with an interest in the needs or outcome of a project.

## Usage and distinctions

Different stakeholders may have conflicting needs or decision authority.

## Examples

> The operations team is a design stakeholder.

> Confirm the requirements with each affected stakeholder.
