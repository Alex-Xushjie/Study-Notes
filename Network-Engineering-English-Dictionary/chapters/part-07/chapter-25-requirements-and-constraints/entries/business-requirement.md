# business requirement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A need expressed in terms of an organization’s desired business outcome.

## Usage and distinctions

Business requirements and technical solutions are related but distinct.

## Examples

> The business requirement is to support the new office.

> Clarify the business impact of an outage.
