# out of scope

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Outside the defined boundaries of a project or task.

## Usage and distinctions

An out-of-scope item can still affect project success.

## Examples

> Application remediation is out of scope.

> Document the out-of-scope dependency.
