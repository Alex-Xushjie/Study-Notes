# design assumption

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A condition treated as true for planning but not yet verified.

## Usage and distinctions

An unverified assumption is not a confirmed requirement or fact.

## Examples

> The design assumes two independent power sources.

> Assign an owner to validate the assumption.
