# project dependency

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A condition or deliverable on which another activity or outcome relies.

## Usage and distinctions

Dependencies can be technical, operational, or external.

## Examples

> The installation depends on the carrier handoff.

> Confirm the dependency owner and due date.
