# design input

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Information used to develop or evaluate a design.

## Usage and distinctions

A design input may be an observed fact, a requirement, or an assumption; identify which.

## Examples

> The traffic forecast is a design input.

> Verify the age and source of each input.
