# requirement traceability

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The ability to connect a requirement to its source, design, implementation, and verification.

## Usage and distinctions

Traceability makes gaps visible but does not by itself prove the requirement was met.

## Examples

> The traceability matrix links each requirement to a test.

> Update the traceability record when the design changes.
