# non-functional requirement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A requirement describing a quality or constraint on system behavior.

## Usage and distinctions

Availability, performance, security, and maintainability requirements need measurable context.

## Examples

> The design has a defined availability target.

> Clarify the performance and recovery requirements.
