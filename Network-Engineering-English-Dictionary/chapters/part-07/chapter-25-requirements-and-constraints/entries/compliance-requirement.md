# compliance requirement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A condition imposed by an applicable law, regulation, contract, or organizational policy.

## Usage and distinctions

Applicability and interpretation should be verified with the relevant authority.

## Examples

> The project has a data-retention compliance requirement.

> Confirm the applicable policy with the responsible team.
