# design constraint

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A limitation that restricts the available solution choices.

## Usage and distinctions

Constraints can be technical, physical, organizational, regulatory, or commercial.

## Examples

> The project has a fixed maintenance window.

> Document the budget and site constraints.
