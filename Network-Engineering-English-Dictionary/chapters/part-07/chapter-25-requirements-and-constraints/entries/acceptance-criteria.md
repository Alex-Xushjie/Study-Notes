# acceptance criteria

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Conditions used to decide whether a deliverable meets agreed expectations.

## Usage and distinctions

Criteria should be observable and tied to the requirement they validate.

## Examples

> The acceptance criteria include successful failover testing.

> Agree on the test method and threshold before implementation.
