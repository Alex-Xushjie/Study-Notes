# operational requirement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A condition needed to operate, monitor, maintain, or support a solution.

## Usage and distinctions

Operational needs should be included early in the design.

## Examples

> The operational requirement includes out-of-band access.

> Confirm monitoring and support ownership.
