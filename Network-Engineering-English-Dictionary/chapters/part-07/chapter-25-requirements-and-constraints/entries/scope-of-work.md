# scope of work

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A description of the work and deliverables included in an engagement or project.

## Usage and distinctions

Scope should distinguish included deliverables, assumptions, and exclusions.

## Examples

> The scope of work includes two branch routers.

> Document exclusions and responsibilities.
