# capacity requirement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A defined need for system or network capacity under specified conditions.

## Usage and distinctions

Capacity must include the measurement point, time horizon, and operating assumptions.

## Examples

> The capacity requirement includes peak branch traffic.

> Record expected growth and traffic patterns.
