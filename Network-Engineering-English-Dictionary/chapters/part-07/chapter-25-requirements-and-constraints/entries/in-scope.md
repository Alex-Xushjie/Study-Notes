# in scope

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Included within the defined boundaries of a project or task.

## Usage and distinctions

In scope does not mean automatically approved for execution.

## Examples

> The firewall replacement is in scope.

> Confirm that the requested migration is in scope.
