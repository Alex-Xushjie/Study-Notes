# project issue

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A current problem or unresolved matter that may affect the project.

## Usage and distinctions

An issue exists now; a risk describes an uncertain future event or condition.

## Examples

> The carrier handoff location remains an open issue.

> Assign an owner and target resolution date.
