# availability requirement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 25

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A defined target or condition for service availability.

## Usage and distinctions

A target must be measurable and interpreted with its agreed conditions.

## Examples

> The availability requirement specifies an approved measurement period.

> Confirm exclusions and the service boundary.
