## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [business requirement](entries/business-requirement.md) | A desired business outcome. |
| [technical requirement](entries/technical-requirement.md) | A technical condition or capability. |
| [design constraint](entries/constraint.md) | A limitation on solution choices. |
| [acceptance criteria](entries/acceptance-criteria.md) | Conditions used to judge a deliverable. |

## Examples and usage

> The business requirement is to support the new office.

> The technical requirement specifies redundant uplinks.

> The project has a fixed maintenance window.

> Agree on the test method before implementation.
