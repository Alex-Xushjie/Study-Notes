# network resilience

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The ability of a network service to withstand or recover from disruptions.

## Usage and distinctions

Resilience involves recovery and operations as well as redundant hardware.

## Examples

> The design improves resilience through diverse paths.

> Test resilience against the agreed failure scenarios.
