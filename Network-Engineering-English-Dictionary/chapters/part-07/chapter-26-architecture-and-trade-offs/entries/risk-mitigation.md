# risk mitigation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An action intended to reduce the likelihood or impact of an identified risk.

## Usage and distinctions

Mitigation can reduce a risk without eliminating it.

## Examples

> A second path mitigates the circuit failure risk.

> Check whether the two paths are physically diverse.
