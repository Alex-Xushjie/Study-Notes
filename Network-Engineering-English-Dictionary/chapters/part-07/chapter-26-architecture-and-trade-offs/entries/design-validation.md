# design validation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The evaluation of whether a proposed design meets its intended requirements.

## Usage and distinctions

Validation is scoped to the requirements and conditions examined.

## Examples

> The team validated the design against failure scenarios.

> Record the test evidence and limitations.
