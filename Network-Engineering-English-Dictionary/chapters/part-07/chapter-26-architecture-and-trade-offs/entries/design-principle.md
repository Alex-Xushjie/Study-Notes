# design principle

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A guiding rule used to make consistent design choices.

## Usage and distinctions

A principle guides decisions but can conflict with other requirements or constraints.

## Examples

> The design principle favors clear failure domains.

> State why the principle applies to this project.
