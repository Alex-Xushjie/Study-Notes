# design rationale

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The reasoning and evidence supporting a design choice.

## Usage and distinctions

Rationale should be understandable to a future reviewer without relying on meeting history.

## Examples

> The rationale cites the recovery requirement.

> Explain why the rejected option was less suitable.
