# design criterion

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A factor used to evaluate a design option.

## Usage and distinctions

Criteria can include cost, resilience, performance, security, and operational complexity.

## Examples

> Availability is one of the design criteria.

> Agree on the criteria before scoring the options.
