# operational simplicity

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A design characteristic that reduces unnecessary complexity in operation and support.

## Usage and distinctions

Simplicity is relative to the team’s skills, tools, and requirements.

## Examples

> The team selected a simpler routing design.

> Consider the operational cost of each additional protocol.
