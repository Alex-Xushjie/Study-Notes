# pilot deployment

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A limited deployment used to evaluate a solution under selected real conditions.

## Usage and distinctions

A pilot differs from a laboratory proof of concept, though project usage varies.

## Examples

> The team will pilot the design at one branch.

> Define pilot success criteria and rollback.
