# scalability

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The ability of a system to accommodate growth within defined limits.

## Usage and distinctions

Scalability should be evaluated against traffic, operational, control-plane, and platform limits.

## Examples

> The design supports additional branches.

> State the expected growth and capacity limits.
