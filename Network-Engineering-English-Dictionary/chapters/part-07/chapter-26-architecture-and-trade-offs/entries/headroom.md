# capacity headroom

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Available capacity beyond expected or current demand.

## Usage and distinctions

Headroom is meaningful only relative to a defined resource and workload.

## Examples

> The uplink has headroom for planned growth.

> Define the headroom target and measurement conditions.
