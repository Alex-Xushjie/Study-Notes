# fault isolation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The containment or identification of a fault so its effects can be limited or located.

## Usage and distinctions

Isolation can involve physical, logical, operational, or security boundaries.

## Examples

> The design improves fault isolation between branches.

> Check whether the failure can affect another site.
