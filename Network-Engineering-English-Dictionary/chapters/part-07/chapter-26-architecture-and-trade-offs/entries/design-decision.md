# design decision

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A selected approach made in response to design requirements and constraints.

## Usage and distinctions

A decision should state context, options, trade-offs, and consequences.

## Examples

> The design decision is to use routed access.

> Record the decision and its rationale.
