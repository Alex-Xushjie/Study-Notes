# proof of concept

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A limited exercise used to test whether a proposed idea is feasible.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: PoC. A successful PoC does not prove production scale, security, or operational readiness.

## Examples

> The team built a proof of concept for the overlay.

> Define what the proof of concept must demonstrate.
