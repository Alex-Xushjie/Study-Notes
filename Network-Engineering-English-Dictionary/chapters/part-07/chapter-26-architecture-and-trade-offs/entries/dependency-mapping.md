# dependency mapping

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The identification of components and services on which another service depends.

## Usage and distinctions

Dependencies can be physical, logical, operational, or external.
