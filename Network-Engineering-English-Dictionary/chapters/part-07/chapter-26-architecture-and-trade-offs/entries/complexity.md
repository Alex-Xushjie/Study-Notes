# design complexity

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The degree of interaction and detail that makes a design harder to understand or operate.

## Usage and distinctions

Complexity can be justified by requirements but should be made explicit.

## Examples

> The overlay adds operational complexity.

> Document the additional failure modes and dependencies.
