# design failure domain

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A group of components or services affected by a particular failure.

## Usage and distinctions

A failure domain is defined relative to the failure scenario under review.
