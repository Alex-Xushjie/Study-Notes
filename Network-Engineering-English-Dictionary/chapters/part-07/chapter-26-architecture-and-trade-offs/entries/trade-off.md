# design trade-off

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A compromise between desirable characteristics that cannot all be maximized simultaneously.

## Usage and distinctions

A trade-off should identify the affected objectives and the reasons for the decision.

## Examples

> The design trades additional cost for greater path diversity.

> Explain the trade-off to the stakeholders.
