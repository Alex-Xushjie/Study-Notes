# residual risk

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 26

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The risk remaining after controls or mitigations are applied.

## Usage and distinctions

Residual risk must be evaluated in the context of the service and failure scenario.

## Examples

> The design retains residual risk from shared power.

> Document the residual risk and its owner.
