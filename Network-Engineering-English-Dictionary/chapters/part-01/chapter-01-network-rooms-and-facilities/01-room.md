## Examples and usage

> The equipment rack is in the telecommunications room.

> The cables run along the overhead cable tray.

> The switch is mounted below the patch panel.

> The UPS supplies power during a power interruption.

## Room names

**[telecommunications room](entries/telecommunications-room.md)**: a room housing cable terminations and associated equipment.

**[main distribution frame (MDF)](entries/mdf.md)**: a primary distribution frame. The term *MDF* is also used informally for the room housing it.

**[intermediate distribution frame (IDF)](entries/idf.md)**: an intermediate distribution frame. In site conversations, *IDF* can also mean its room.

> The access switches are in the third-floor IDF room.

> Could you arrange access to the MDF room for the site survey?
