## Picture words

| No. | Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [uninterruptible power supply (UPS)](entries/ups.md) | A system that supplies energy during an interruption. |
| 2 | [power distribution unit (PDU)](entries/pdu.md) | A unit that distributes power to equipment. |
| 3 | [power outlet](entries/power-outlet.md) | A socket that accepts an electrical plug. |
| 4 | [power supply module](entries/power-supply-module.md) | A component that supplies electrical power to a device. |

## Examples and usage

> Connect the power cord to the labeled PDU outlet.

> The switch has two power supply modules.

> The UPS reports its estimated runtime.

> Confirm the upstream power source for each PDU.

## More useful words

**[power cord](entries/power-cord.md)**: a cable that connects equipment to an electrical power source.

**[network switch](entries/network-switch.md)**: a network device that may contain power supplies.

## Useful distinctions

**UPS versus PDU:** A UPS can supply stored energy when normal power is interrupted. A PDU distributes power to connected equipment.

**Redundancy:** Two PDUs or power supplies do not automatically mean independent power paths. Verify the upstream sources and the device design.

> Are the two power supplies connected to independent sources?

> The UPS runtime depends on the load and battery condition.
