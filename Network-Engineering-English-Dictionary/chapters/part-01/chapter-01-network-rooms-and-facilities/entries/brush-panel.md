# cable brush panel

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A panel with flexible bristles that allows cables to pass while limiting an open gap.

## Usage and distinctions

Airflow performance depends on the panel and cabinet design.

## Examples

> The cables enter the cabinet through a brush panel.

> Check that the brush panel does not pinch the cables.
