# inspection checklist

**Technical term** · Chapter 01 · `technical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A list of items to verify during an inspection.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. A checklist records what to inspect; results, abnormal conditions, and ownership should also be recorded.

## Common combinations

site inspection; inspection checklist; record the findings

## Examples

> Complete the inspection checklist before leaving the room.

> Record the alarm and its timestamp in the checklist.
