# trace a cable

**Verb phrase: trace a cable; traced a cable.** · Chapter 01 · `expression`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

To identify a cable’s route or endpoints by checking labels, records, and suitable tracing methods.

## Usage and distinctions

Pattern: “Trace [cable ID] from [endpoint A] to [endpoint B].” Check labels and records and follow the site procedure for live links.

## Common combinations

trace the cable to; verify both ends; cable label

## Examples

> Please trace this cable to the patch panel and verify the port label.

> We have identified one end; the far end still needs to be confirmed.
