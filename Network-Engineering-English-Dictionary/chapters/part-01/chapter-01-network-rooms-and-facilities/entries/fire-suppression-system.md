# fire suppression system

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A system designed to control or extinguish fire in a protected area.

## Usage and distinctions

Different systems use different agents and safety procedures; follow the facility’s instructions.

## Examples

> The equipment room has a fire suppression system.

> Confirm the site’s entry procedure after a suppression alarm.
