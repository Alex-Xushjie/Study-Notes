# meet-me room

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A room where network operators or tenants interconnect their communications facilities.

## Usage and distinctions

A meet-me room may contain multiple providers; procedures and ownership boundaries vary.

## Examples

> The cross-connect terminates in the meet-me room.

> Arrange access to the meet-me room before tracing the link.
