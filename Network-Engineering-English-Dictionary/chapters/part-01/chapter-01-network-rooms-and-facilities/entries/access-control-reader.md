# access control reader

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A device that reads credentials used to request entry to a controlled area.

## Usage and distinctions

A reader validates credentials through a connected system; it does not grant work authorization.

## Examples

> Present the access card at the door reader.

> The access control reader rejected the card.
