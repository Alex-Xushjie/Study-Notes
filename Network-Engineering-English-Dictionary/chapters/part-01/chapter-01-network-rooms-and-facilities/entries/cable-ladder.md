# cable ladder

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An open support structure with side rails and cross members for routing cables.

## Usage and distinctions

A cable ladder is one form of cable support; confirm loading and routing requirements.

## Examples

> The backbone cables run along the overhead cable ladder.

> Check that the cable ladder has spare capacity.
