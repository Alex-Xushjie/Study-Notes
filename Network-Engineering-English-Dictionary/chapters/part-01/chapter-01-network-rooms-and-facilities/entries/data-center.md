# data center

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A facility or space housing computing, storage, networking, and supporting infrastructure.

## Usage and distinctions

A data center is a facility or environment; a data center network is its networking system.

## Examples

> The data center connects to the campus over two links.

> Confirm the data center access procedure before arrival.
