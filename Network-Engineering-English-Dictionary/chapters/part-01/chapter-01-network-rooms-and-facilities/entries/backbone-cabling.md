# backbone cabling

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Cabling that provides connections between telecommunications spaces or distribution points.

## Usage and distinctions

Backbone cabling and horizontal cabling serve different parts of a structured cabling system.

## Examples

> The backbone cabling links the building’s network rooms.
