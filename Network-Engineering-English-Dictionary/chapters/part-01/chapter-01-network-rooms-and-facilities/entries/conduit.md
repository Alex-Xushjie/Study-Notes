# conduit

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A tube or enclosed pathway used to protect and route cables.

## Usage and distinctions

Conduit dimensions, fill, and installation rules are site- and jurisdiction-specific.

## Examples

> The fiber cable passes through a conduit between rooms.
