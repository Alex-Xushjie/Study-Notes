# grounding busbar

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A conductive bar used as a connection point in a grounding or bonding system.

## Usage and distinctions

Grounding and bonding designs must follow applicable standards and local electrical rules.

## Examples

> The cabinet bonding conductor connects to the grounding busbar.
