# airflow

**Technical term** · Chapter 01 · `technical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 04 cooling visual spread](../04-cooling.md).

## Definition

The movement of air through a space or a piece of equipment.

## Usage and distinctions

Usually uncountable in this context. Check the actual airflow direction of the device; it varies by model and fan configuration.

## Common combinations

airflow direction; restrict airflow; airflow path

## Examples

> The cable bundle must not block the airflow.

> Check the airflow direction before installing the switch.
