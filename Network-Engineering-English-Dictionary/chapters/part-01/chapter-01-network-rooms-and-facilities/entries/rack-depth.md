# rack depth

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A front-to-back dimension relevant to equipment mounting or cabinet clearance.

## Usage and distinctions

Overall cabinet depth, mounting depth, and rear clearance are different measurements.

## Examples

> The rack depth is insufficient for the appliance.

> Measure the usable mounting depth before ordering.
