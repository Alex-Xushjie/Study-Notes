# bonding conductor

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A conductor used to connect conductive parts to maintain an electrical bond.

## Usage and distinctions

Selection and installation require the approved electrical design and qualified personnel.
