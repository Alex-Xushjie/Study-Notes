# Could you confirm the exact location of …?

**Polite question for a site contact or colleague.** · Chapter 01 · `expression`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A polite request for a precise equipment location when a room name alone is insufficient.

## Usage and distinctions

Pattern: “Could you confirm the exact location of [device], including [room, rack ID, and U position]?” A location does not itself grant authorization to perform work.

## Common combinations

confirm the location; rack ID; U position

## Examples

> Could you confirm the exact location of SW-03, including the room, rack ID, and U position?

> It is in IDF-03, rack R02, at U18.
