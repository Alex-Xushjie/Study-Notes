# cable tie

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A fastener used to bundle or secure cables.

## Usage and distinctions

Fastener type and tension should follow the cable manufacturer’s guidance.

## Examples

> The installer used a cable tie to organize the patch cords.

> Do not overtighten the cable ties.
