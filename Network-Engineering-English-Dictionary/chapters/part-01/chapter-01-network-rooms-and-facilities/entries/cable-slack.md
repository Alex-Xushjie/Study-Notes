# cable slack

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Extra cable length left to permit routing, movement, or maintenance.

## Usage and distinctions

Excess slack must be managed according to the cable and site requirements.

## Examples

> Leave enough cable slack to service the equipment.

> Secure the slack without exceeding the bend-radius limits.
