# perforated floor tile

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 04 cooling visual spread](../04-cooling.md).

## Definition

A raised-floor tile with openings that allow air to pass through.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. A perforated tile is used only where appropriate for the cooling design; tile placement is site-specific.

## Common combinations

perforated tile; floor opening; supply air

## Examples

> Cool air rises through the perforated floor tile.

> Check the cooling plan before moving a floor tile.
