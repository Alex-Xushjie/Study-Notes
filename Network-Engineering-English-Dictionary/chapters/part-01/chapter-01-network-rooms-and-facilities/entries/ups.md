# uninterruptible power supply

**Countable noun; abbreviation: UPS. Say “a UPS.”** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

![Illustrated detail: uninterruptible power supply.](../../../../assets/visual-ups.svg)

*A system that supplies power during an interruption. This is an illustrative detail; actual equipment varies.*

## Definition

A system that supplies stored energy to a load when its normal input power is interrupted.

## Usage and distinctions

Runtime depends on load, stored-energy condition, and system design. Rated power and runtime are separate specifications.

## Common combinations

UPS runtime; UPS load; battery replacement; on UPS power

## Examples

> The UPS reports an estimated runtime of 18 minutes at the current load.

> The equipment remained online during the brief power interruption.
