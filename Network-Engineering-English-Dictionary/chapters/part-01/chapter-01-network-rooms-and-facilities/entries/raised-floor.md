# raised floor

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 04 cooling visual spread](../04-cooling.md).

## Definition

A floor built above the structural floor to create a space beneath it.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. Some raised-floor designs distribute conditioned air, while others use the underfloor space for services.

## Common combinations

raised-floor tile; underfloor space; lift a floor tile

## Examples

> The data center has a raised floor.

> Cables run in the space below the raised floor.
