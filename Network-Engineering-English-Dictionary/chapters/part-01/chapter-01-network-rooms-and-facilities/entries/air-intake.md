# air intake

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 04 cooling visual spread](../04-cooling.md).

## Definition

An opening through which air enters a piece of equipment or a cooling system.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. The intake location and direction depend on the equipment model.

## Common combinations

air intake opening; intake temperature; clear the intake

## Examples

> Keep the air intake clear of obstructions.

> Measure the temperature near the air intake.
