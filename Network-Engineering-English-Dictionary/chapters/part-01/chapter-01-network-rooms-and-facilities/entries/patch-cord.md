# patch cord

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 02 rack visual spread](../02-rack.md).

## Definition

A short cable used to connect equipment to a patch panel or another connection point.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. A patch cord may be copper or fiber; connector types and cable properties must match the application.

## Common combinations

Ethernet patch cord; fiber patch cord; connect a patch cord

## Examples

> Connect the patch cord to the labeled port.

> The patch cord is too short to reach the switch.
