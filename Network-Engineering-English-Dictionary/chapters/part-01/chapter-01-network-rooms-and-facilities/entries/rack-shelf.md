# rack shelf

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A shelf designed to support equipment within a rack or cabinet.

## Usage and distinctions

The equipment, shelf, and rack must all be suitable for the intended load and mounting.

## Examples

> The small appliance sits on a rack shelf.

> Verify the shelf’s load rating before installation.
