# hook-and-loop cable tie

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A reusable fabric fastener used to bundle cables.

## Usage and distinctions

Reusable ties can simplify maintenance, but cable tension and bend requirements still apply.

## Examples

> The patch cords are secured with hook-and-loop ties.

> Release the tie before adding another cable.
