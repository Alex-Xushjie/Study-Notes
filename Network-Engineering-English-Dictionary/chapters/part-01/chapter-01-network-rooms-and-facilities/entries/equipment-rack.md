# equipment rack

**Countable noun.** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

![Illustrated detail: equipment rack.](../../../../assets/visual-equipment-rack.svg)

*A frame for mounting network equipment. This is an illustrative detail; actual equipment varies.*

## Definition

A supporting frame used to mount networking, computing, or telecommunications equipment.

## Usage and distinctions

A rack is a mounting frame. A cabinet is an enclosure that may contain mounting rails. U18 identifies a position, while 1U or 2U describes equipment height.

## Common combinations

mount equipment in a rack; rack space; a rack-mounted switch

## Examples

> Mount the switch in rack R03 at U18.

> We need to confirm the available rack space before delivery.
