# rack load

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The weight of equipment and accessories supported by a rack or cabinet.

## Usage and distinctions

Static and dynamic load ratings can differ; use the manufacturer’s specifications.

## Examples

> The planned rack load is below the cabinet rating.

> Confirm the load distribution and support requirements.
