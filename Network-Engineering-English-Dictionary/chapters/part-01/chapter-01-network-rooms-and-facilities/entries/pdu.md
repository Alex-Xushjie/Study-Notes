# power distribution unit

**Countable noun; abbreviation: PDU.** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

![Illustrated detail: power distribution unit.](../../../../assets/visual-pdu.svg)

*A unit that distributes power to equipment. This is an illustrative detail; actual equipment varies.*

## Definition

A unit that distributes electrical power to connected equipment.

## Usage and distinctions

A PDU distributes power; a UPS supplies energy during an interruption. Two PDUs are not necessarily independent unless their upstream paths are independent.

## Common combinations

rack PDU; PDU outlet; connect to separate PDUs

## Examples

> Power supply 1 is connected to PDU-A, and power supply 2 is connected to PDU-B.

> Please verify the upstream power sources for both PDUs.
