# rack label

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An identifier attached to a rack or cabinet.

## Usage and distinctions

A rack label identifies the physical location; it is not necessarily a device asset tag.

## Examples

> The rack label reads R02.

> Check the rack label against the elevation drawing.
