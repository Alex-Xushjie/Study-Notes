# exhaust vent

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 04 cooling visual spread](../04-cooling.md).

## Definition

An opening through which air leaves equipment or a space.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. An exhaust vent is different from an air intake; verify the actual direction for the device.

## Common combinations

exhaust vent; exhaust air; keep the vent clear

## Examples

> Warm air leaves through the exhaust vent.

> Do not obstruct the exhaust vent.
