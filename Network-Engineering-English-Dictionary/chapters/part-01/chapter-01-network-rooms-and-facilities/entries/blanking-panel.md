# blanking panel

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 02 rack visual spread](../02-rack.md).

## Definition

A panel that fills an unused opening in a rack or cabinet.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. Blanking panels can help control unwanted airflow through open rack spaces; the effect depends on the design.

## Common combinations

install a blanking panel; unused rack space; airflow management

## Examples

> Install a blanking panel in the unused opening.

> The blanking panels cover the empty rack spaces.
