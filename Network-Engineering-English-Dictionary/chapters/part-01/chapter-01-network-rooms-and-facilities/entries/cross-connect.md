# cross-connect

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A connection between separately managed cable or service termination points.

## Usage and distinctions

A cross-connect can use different media and physical arrangements.

## Examples
