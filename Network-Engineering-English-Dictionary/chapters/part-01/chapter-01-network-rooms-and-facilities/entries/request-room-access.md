# Could you arrange access to …?

**Polite request; access is uncountable in this sense.** · Chapter 01 · `expression`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A polite request for someone to arrange access to a restricted room or facility.

## Usage and distinctions

Pattern: “Could you arrange access to [location] for [purpose] on [date]?” Confirm the access window and any escort requirement.

## Common combinations

arrange access; authorized escort; site contact

## Examples

> Could you arrange access to the MDF room for our site survey on Friday?

> Please confirm the access window and whether an escort is required.
