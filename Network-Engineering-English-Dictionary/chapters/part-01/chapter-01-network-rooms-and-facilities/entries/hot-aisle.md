# hot aisle

**Countable noun.** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An aisle into which equipment exhaust air is directed in a hot-aisle/cold-aisle layout.

## Usage and distinctions

In a hot-aisle/cold-aisle arrangement, the hot aisle receives exhaust air. Verify the actual airflow direction of each device.

## Common combinations

hot-aisle containment; exhaust air; hot-aisle temperature

## Examples

> Do not block the exhaust path into the hot aisle.

> Confirm the airflow direction before installing the switch.
