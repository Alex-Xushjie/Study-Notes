# cabinet door

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 01

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A door providing access to the front or rear of an equipment cabinet.

## Usage and distinctions

Door perforation and clearance can affect airflow and maintenance access.

## Examples

> The cabinet door is locked.

> Leave adequate clearance to open the rear door.
