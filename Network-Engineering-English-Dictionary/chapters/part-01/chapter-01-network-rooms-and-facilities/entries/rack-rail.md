# rack rail

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 02 rack visual spread](../02-rack.md).

## Definition

A rail with mounting positions used to support or secure equipment in a rack.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. Equipment may use front mounting rails, support rails, or both, depending on its mounting kit.

## Common combinations

mounting rail; rack rail holes; attach to the rail

## Examples

> The switch is attached to the front rack rails.

> Check the mounting kit before installing the device.
