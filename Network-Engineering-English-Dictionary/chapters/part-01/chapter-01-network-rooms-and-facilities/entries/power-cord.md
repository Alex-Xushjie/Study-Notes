# power cord

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

[See this term in the 03 power visual spread](../03-power.md).

## Definition

A cable that connects equipment to an electrical power source.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. Confirm connector type, electrical rating, length, and approved routing for the actual equipment.

## Common combinations

power cord connector; secure the power cord; power-cord label

## Examples

> The power cord connects the device to the PDU.

> Label both ends of the power cord.
