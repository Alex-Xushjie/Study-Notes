## Picture words

| No. | Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [rack rail](entries/rack-rail.md) | A mounting rail that helps secure equipment. |
| 2 | [patch panel](entries/patch-panel.md) | A panel of cable connection points. |
| 3 | [network switch](entries/network-switch.md) | A device that forwards traffic between its connections. |
| 4 | [cable manager](entries/cable-manager.md) | A guide that organizes cables. |
| 5 | [power distribution unit (PDU)](entries/pdu.md) | A unit that distributes power to connected equipment. |
| 6 | [patch cord](entries/patch-cord.md) | A short cable connecting equipment or connection points. |

## Examples and usage

> Mount the switch on the rack rails.

> Connect the patch cord to port 12 on the patch panel.

> Route the patch cords through the cable manager.

> Check the label on the switch port.

## Position and space

**[rack unit (U)](entries/rack-unit.md)**: a unit of mounting height. One U equals 1.75 inches (44.45 mm).

**[rack elevation](entries/rack-elevation.md)**: a drawing showing the positions of devices in a rack.

**[rack location](entries/rack-location.md)**: the recorded site, room, rack and mounting position.

**[blanking panel](entries/blanking-panel.md)**: a panel covering an unused rack opening.

**[switch port](entries/switch-port.md)**: a physical interface for a cable or transceiver.

> The 2U device is installed at U18 in rack R02.

> Please update the rack elevation after the installation.
