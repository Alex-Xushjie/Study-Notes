# handover

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The transfer of a completed system and its supporting information to the receiving team.

## Usage and distinctions

As a noun, handover is one word; the related verb phrase is hand over.

## Examples

> The project team completed the operational handover.

> Include the as-built drawings and test records in the handover.
