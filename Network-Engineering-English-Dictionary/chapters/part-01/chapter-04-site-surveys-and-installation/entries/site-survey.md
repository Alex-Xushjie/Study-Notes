# site survey

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An on-site assessment of the conditions relevant to a planned project.

## Usage and distinctions

A site survey records actual conditions and constraints; its scope should be agreed before the visit.

## Examples

> The engineer completed a site survey before installation.

> The survey identified a shortage of rack space.
