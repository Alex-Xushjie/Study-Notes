# cable schedule

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A record listing cable identifiers, endpoints, and other relevant cable information.

## Usage and distinctions

A cable schedule may also include media type, length, and test results according to project needs.

## Examples

> Update the cable schedule after moving the patch cord.

> The cable label does not match the schedule.
