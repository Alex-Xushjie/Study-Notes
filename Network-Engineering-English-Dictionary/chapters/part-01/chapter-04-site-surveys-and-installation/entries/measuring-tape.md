# measuring tape

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A flexible marked strip used to measure length or distance.

## Usage and distinctions

Also called a tape measure. State units and measurement reference points in survey notes.

## Examples

> Use a measuring tape to check the cabinet depth.

> Record the available clearance in millimeters.
