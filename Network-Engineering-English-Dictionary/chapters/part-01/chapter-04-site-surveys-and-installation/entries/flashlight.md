# flashlight

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A portable handheld light used to illuminate a dark area.

## Usage and distinctions

Flashlight is common in US English; torch is common in UK English.

## Examples

> Use a flashlight to read the cable label.

> Bring a flashlight for the rack inspection.
