# clipboard

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A rigid board with a clip used to hold sheets of paper.

## Usage and distinctions

A clipboard is a physical object; the digital clipboard is a different meaning.

## Examples

> The engineer carried the survey notes on a clipboard.

> Place the completed checklist on the clipboard.
