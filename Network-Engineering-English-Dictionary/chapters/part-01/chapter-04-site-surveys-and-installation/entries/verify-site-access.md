# verify site access

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

To confirm that a person has the necessary arrangements to enter the site.

## Usage and distinctions

Access to a room does not by itself authorize changes to production equipment.

## Examples

> Please verify site access before the team travels.

> Confirm the access window and escort requirements.
