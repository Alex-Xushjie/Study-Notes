# snag list

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A list of outstanding defects or incomplete items identified for correction.

## Usage and distinctions

Also called a punch list in many US contexts. Each item should have an owner and a status.

## Examples

> Add the missing cable label to the snag list.

> The contractor will close the remaining items before handover.
