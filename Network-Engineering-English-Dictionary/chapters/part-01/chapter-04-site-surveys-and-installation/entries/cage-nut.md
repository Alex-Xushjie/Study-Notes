# cage nut

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A threaded nut held in a spring-steel cage, commonly used with square rack holes.

## Usage and distinctions

Cage nuts are one mounting method; threaded-hole and other rack designs use different hardware.

## Examples

> Install the cage nuts in the specified rack positions.

> Confirm the screw size matches the cage nut.
