# equipment clearance

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The free space required around equipment for installation, operation, ventilation, or service.

## Usage and distinctions

Clearance requirements depend on the specific equipment and site design.

## Examples

> The rear equipment clearance is insufficient.

> Verify the front and rear clearances against the hardware guide.
