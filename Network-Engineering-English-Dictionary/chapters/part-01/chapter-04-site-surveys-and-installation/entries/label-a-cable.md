# label a cable

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

To attach an identifier to a cable so it can be traced and documented.

## Usage and distinctions

Use the approved labeling scheme and leave labels readable after installation.

## Examples

> Label both ends of the cable.

> Check that the cable label matches the schedule.
