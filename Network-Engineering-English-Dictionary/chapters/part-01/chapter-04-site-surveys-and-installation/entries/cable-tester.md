# cable tester

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A device used to check selected properties of a cable or cabling link.

## Usage and distinctions

Testers have different capabilities. A continuity tester alone does not certify a cabling link.

## Examples

> Use the cable tester to check continuity.

> Record the tester model and result.
