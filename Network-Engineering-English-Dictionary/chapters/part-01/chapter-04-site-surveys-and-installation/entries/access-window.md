# access window

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An agreed period during which a person may enter a site or room.

## Usage and distinctions

An access window is not necessarily an approved maintenance or outage window.

## Examples

> The access window begins at 09:00.

> Confirm the access window with the site contact.
