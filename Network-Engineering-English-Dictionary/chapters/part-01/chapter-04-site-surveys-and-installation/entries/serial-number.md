# serial number

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A manufacturer-assigned identifier for a particular product unit.

## Usage and distinctions

Serial numbers and asset tags are different identifiers; record both when required.

## Examples

> Record the serial number before installing the switch.

> Verify that the serial number matches the delivery record.
