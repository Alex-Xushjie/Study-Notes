# installation checklist

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A list of tasks or checks used to control and record an installation.

## Usage and distinctions

Record actual results, exceptions, and responsible people; a checked box alone may not capture evidence.

## Examples

> Complete the installation checklist before handover.

> The checklist includes power and connectivity checks.
