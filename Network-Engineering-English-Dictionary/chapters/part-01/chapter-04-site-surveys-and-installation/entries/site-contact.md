# site contact

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A person designated to coordinate access or activities at a location.

## Usage and distinctions

A site contact may coordinate access without being authorized to approve a network change.

## Examples

> The site contact will meet the installation team.

> Please confirm the site contact’s phone number.
