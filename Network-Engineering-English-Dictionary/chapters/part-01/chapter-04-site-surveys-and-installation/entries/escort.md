# escort

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An authorized person who accompanies a visitor in a restricted area.

## Usage and distinctions

Use as a noun or verb: an escort accompanies a visitor; to escort someone is to accompany them.

## Examples

> An escort is required in the equipment room.

> Please arrange an escort for the site survey.
