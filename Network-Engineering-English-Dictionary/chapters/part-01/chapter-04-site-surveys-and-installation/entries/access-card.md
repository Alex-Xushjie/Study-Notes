# access card

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A card used to identify a person or grant entry to a controlled area.

## Usage and distinctions

The card does not automatically authorize work on network equipment.

## Examples

> The site contact provided an access card for the visit.

> Return the access card after the site survey.
