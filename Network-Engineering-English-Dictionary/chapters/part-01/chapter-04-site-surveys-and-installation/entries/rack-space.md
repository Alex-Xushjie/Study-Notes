# rack space

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 04

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The available mounting capacity within an equipment rack.

## Usage and distinctions

Available height is only one requirement; depth, weight, power, airflow, and clearance also matter.

## Examples

> The cabinet has 6U of available rack space.

> Check rack space before ordering the appliance.
