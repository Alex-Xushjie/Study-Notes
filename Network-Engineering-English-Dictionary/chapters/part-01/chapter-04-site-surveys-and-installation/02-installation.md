## Picture words

| No. | Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [mounting bracket](entries/mounting-bracket.md) | A component used to attach equipment to a support. |
| 2 | [rack screw](entries/rack-screw.md) | A screw for securing rack equipment. |
| 3 | [cage nut](entries/cage-nut.md) | A nut held in a spring cage for a square rack hole. |
| 4 | [screwdriver](entries/screwdriver.md) | A hand tool used to turn a screw. |
| 5 | [cable tester](entries/cable-tester.md) | A device that checks selected cable properties. |

## Examples and usage

> Check that the mounting bracket is compatible with the rack.

> Use the correct screws and cage nuts for the installation.

> Record the cable tester results in the installation checklist.

> Update the as-built drawing after the work is complete.
