# management interface

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An interface used to administer or monitor a system.

## Usage and distinctions

A management interface may be physical or logical, depending on the system.

## Examples

> The management interface has an address in the management network.

> Check the management interface route and access policy.
