# management port

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An interface intended for managing a device, often separate from its data interfaces.

## Usage and distinctions

A dedicated management port may support out-of-band access when its management path is separate from production traffic.

## Examples

> The management port is connected to the management network.

> Check whether the management port is in the correct subnet.
