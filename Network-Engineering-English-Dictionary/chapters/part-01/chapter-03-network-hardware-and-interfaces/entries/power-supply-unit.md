# power supply unit

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A component that converts or supplies electrical power for equipment.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: PSU. Redundancy depends on capacity, device design, and upstream power paths.
