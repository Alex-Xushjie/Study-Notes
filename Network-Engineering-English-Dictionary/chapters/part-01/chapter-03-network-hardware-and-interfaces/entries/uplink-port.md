# uplink port

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A port used to connect a device toward another network device or upstream part of the topology.

## Usage and distinctions

Uplink describes a role in a topology; it does not imply a universal port type or speed.

## Examples

> The access switch uplink connects to the distribution switch.

> Check both ends of the uplink.
