# access switch

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A switch that connects end devices to a network at the access layer.

## Usage and distinctions

Access describes the device’s role in the design, not a specific hardware model.

## Examples

> The access switch connects the users on the third floor.

> Check the uplink from the access switch.
