# firewall appliance

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A hardware device that enforces network security policies on traffic.

## Usage and distinctions

A firewall may be hardware, virtual, or software. This term refers to the physical appliance.

## Examples

> The firewall appliance filters traffic between the two zones.

> Verify the policy before connecting the new network.
