# downlink port

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A port used to connect toward downstream devices or another layer of the network.

## Usage and distinctions

The terms uplink and downlink are relative to the network topology and point of view.

## Examples

> The downlink connects the distribution switch to an access switch.

> Label the downlink with the remote device name.
