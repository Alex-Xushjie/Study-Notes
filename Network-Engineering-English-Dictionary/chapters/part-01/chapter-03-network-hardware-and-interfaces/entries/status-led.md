# status LED

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A light-emitting indicator that reports a device or interface condition.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: LED. Color and blink patterns are vendor- and model-specific; do not infer a universal meaning.
