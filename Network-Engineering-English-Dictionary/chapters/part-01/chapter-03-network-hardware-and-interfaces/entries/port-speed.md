# port speed

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 03

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The data rate at which a port operates or is configured to operate.

## Usage and distinctions

Supported and negotiated speeds depend on the interface, module, medium, and peer.

## Examples

> The port speed is one gigabit per second.

> Compare the configured and operational speeds.
