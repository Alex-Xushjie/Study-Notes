## Picture words

| No. | Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [network switch](../chapter-01-network-rooms-and-facilities/entries/network-switch.md) | A device that connects ports and forwards traffic. |
| 2 | [router](entries/router.md) | A device that forwards packets between networks. |
| 3 | [firewall appliance](entries/firewall-appliance.md) | A hardware device that applies security policy. |
| 4 | [transceiver module](entries/transceiver-module.md) | A component that transmits and receives signals. |
| 5 | [wireless access point](entries/wireless-access-point.md) | A device that provides wireless connectivity. |

## Examples and usage

> The access switch connects the users on the third floor.

> The router forwards the packet to the next hop.

> Verify the firewall policy before connecting the new network.

> Check the access point’s uplink and power source.
