## Quick review

1. What is the difference between a plug and a jack?
2. Does an LC connector alone identify the fiber type?
3. What information should be checked before selecting an optical transceiver?
4. What does a cable certification result test against?

**Answers:** 1. A plug mates with a jack. 2. No; connector format and fiber type are separate properties. 3. Check device compatibility, fiber type, wavelength, reach, and the optical budget. 4. The selected test limits and applicable cabling specification.
