# LC connector

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A small-form-factor fiber-optic connector commonly used in single-fiber and duplex assemblies.

## Usage and distinctions

LC identifies the connector format; it does not by itself specify fiber mode, polish, wavelength, or link speed.

## Examples

> The transceiver has duplex LC ports.

> Check that the LC connectors match the selected optical interface.
