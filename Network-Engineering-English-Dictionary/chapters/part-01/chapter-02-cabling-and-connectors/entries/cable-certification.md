# cable certification

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Testing a cabling link against the requirements of a specified cabling standard and test configuration.

## Usage and distinctions

Certification is different from basic continuity checking. Record the selected standard, tested link model, and result.

## Examples

> The contractor supplied the cable certification results.

> Review the test limits and the results for each link.
