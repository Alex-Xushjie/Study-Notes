# RJ45 connector

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A common informal name for the eight-position modular connector used on many twisted-pair Ethernet connections.

## Usage and distinctions

In Ethernet work RJ45 commonly refers to an 8P8C modular connector; the terminology is widely used but the naming is not technically exact.

## Examples

> Insert the RJ45 plug into the switch port.

> Check that the connector latch is intact.
