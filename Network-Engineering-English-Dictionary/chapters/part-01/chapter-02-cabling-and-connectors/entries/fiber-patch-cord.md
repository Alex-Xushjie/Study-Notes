# fiber patch cord

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A short fiber-optic cable assembly fitted with connectors at its ends.

## Usage and distinctions

A patch cord is a complete assembly; LC-to-LC describes its connectors but not all of its optical properties.

## Examples

> The fiber patch cord connects the switch to the fiber panel.

> Verify the connector type and fiber grade before connecting it.
