# twisted-pair cable

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A cable containing pairs of insulated copper conductors twisted together to help reduce interference.

## Usage and distinctions

The pairs are twisted to control interference; the cable category and construction affect its performance.

## Examples

> The twisted-pair cable connects the wall outlet to the patch panel.

> Keep the cable within the manufacturer’s specified bend radius.
