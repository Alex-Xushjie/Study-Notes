# optical fiber core

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The central region of an optical fiber through which light is guided.

## Usage and distinctions

Core size and optical properties depend on the fiber specification.

## Examples
