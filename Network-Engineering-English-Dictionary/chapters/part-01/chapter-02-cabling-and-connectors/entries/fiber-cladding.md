# optical fiber cladding

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The layer surrounding a fiber core that helps confine guided light.

## Usage and distinctions

Cladding is part of the fiber structure, not the outer cable jacket.

## Examples

> The cladding surrounds the fiber core.

> Follow the fiber manufacturer’s handling instructions.
