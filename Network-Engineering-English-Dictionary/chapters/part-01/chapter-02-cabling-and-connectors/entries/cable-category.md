# cable category

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A classification of twisted-pair cabling components by specified performance characteristics.

## Usage and distinctions

Examples include Category 6 and Category 6A. Do not infer a supported link length or data rate from the category name alone.

## Examples

> The cable jacket is marked with its category.

> Confirm the category of the cable, jacks, and patch cords.
