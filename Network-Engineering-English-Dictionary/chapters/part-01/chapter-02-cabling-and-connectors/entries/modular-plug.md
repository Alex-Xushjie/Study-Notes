# modular plug

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A plug fitted to the end of a cable for connection to a compatible modular jack.

## Usage and distinctions

Plug is the male part; jack is the mating receptacle. Verify the connector is suitable for the cable construction.

## Examples

> The modular plug clicks into the jack.

> Inspect the plug for damage before connecting it.
