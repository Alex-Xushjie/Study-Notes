# fiber polarity

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The arrangement of optical paths so that transmitting and receiving interfaces connect as intended.

## Usage and distinctions

In a common duplex connection, a transmitter must reach the remote receiver. Polarity methods vary by cabling system.

## Examples

> Verify fiber polarity if the link does not come up.

> Check the polarity plan before changing the patching.
