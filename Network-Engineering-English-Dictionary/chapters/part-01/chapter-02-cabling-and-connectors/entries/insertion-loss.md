# insertion loss

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The reduction in signal power caused by inserting a component or link into a transmission path.

## Usage and distinctions

Loss is commonly expressed in decibels. The applicable limit depends on the component, link design, and test method.

## Examples

> The measured insertion loss is above the project limit.

> Compare the result with the applicable link budget.
