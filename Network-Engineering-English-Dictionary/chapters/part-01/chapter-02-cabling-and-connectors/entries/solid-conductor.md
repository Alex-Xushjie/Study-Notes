# solid conductor

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A conductor formed from a single solid wire.

## Usage and distinctions

Solid and stranded conductors can require different termination hardware.

## Examples

> The permanent link uses solid-conductor cable.

> Verify that the connector supports solid conductors.
