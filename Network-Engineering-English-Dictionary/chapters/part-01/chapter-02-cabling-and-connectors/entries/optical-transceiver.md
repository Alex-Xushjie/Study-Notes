# optical transceiver

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A device that transmits and receives optical signals for a network interface.

## Usage and distinctions

A transceiver is active hardware, not a fiber connector. SFP is a form factor; speed and optical properties vary.

## Examples

> The switch uses an optical transceiver for the uplink.

> Confirm the transceiver, fiber, wavelength, and reach requirements.
