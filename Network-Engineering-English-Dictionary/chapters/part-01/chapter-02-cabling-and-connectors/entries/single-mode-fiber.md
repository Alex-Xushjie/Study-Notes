# single-mode fiber

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An optical fiber designed to support essentially one propagation mode at the intended operating wavelengths.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: SMF. Yellow is common for some single-mode cable jackets, but jacket color alone is not definitive identification.

## Examples

> The backbone uses single-mode fiber.

> Verify that the transceivers are compatible with the installed single-mode fiber.
