# bend radius

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The radius of a bend in a cable, usually discussed in relation to the minimum radius specified by its manufacturer.

## Usage and distinctions

A smaller radius means a tighter bend. Requirements can differ during installation and in the final installed condition.

## Examples

> The cable bend radius is too tight.

> Follow the cable manufacturer’s minimum bend-radius requirements.
