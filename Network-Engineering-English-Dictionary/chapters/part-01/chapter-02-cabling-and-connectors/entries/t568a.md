# T568A wiring scheme

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A standardized arrangement of conductor positions used for balanced twisted-pair terminations.

## Usage and distinctions

T568A and T568B differ in pair position assignments; use the applicable project specification.

## Examples

> The installer terminated the jack using T568A.

> Confirm the approved wiring scheme for the site.
