# keystone jack

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A snap-in jack module designed to fit a compatible keystone opening.

## Usage and distinctions

Keystone describes the mounting form, not the performance category or connector type.

## Examples

> The keystone jack is mounted in the wall plate.

> Label the jack to match the cable schedule.
