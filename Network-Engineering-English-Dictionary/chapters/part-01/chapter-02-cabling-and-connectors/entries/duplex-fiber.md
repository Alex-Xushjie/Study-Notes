# duplex fiber

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A fiber connection or cable assembly using two optical fibers.

## Usage and distinctions

A common duplex link uses separate fibers for transmit and receive; not all optical links are duplex-fiber systems.

## Examples

> The duplex fiber link uses one fiber in each direction.

> Check the transmit and receive connections at both ends.
