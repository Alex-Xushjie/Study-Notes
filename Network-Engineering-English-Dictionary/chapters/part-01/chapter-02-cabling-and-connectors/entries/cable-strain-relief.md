# cable strain relief

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A feature that reduces mechanical stress on a cable or connector.

## Usage and distinctions

Strain relief does not remove the need to meet bend-radius and tension limits.

## Examples

> The plug has a molded strain-relief boot.

> Avoid pulling on the cable near the connector.
