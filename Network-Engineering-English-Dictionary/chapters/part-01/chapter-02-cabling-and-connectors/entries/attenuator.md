# optical attenuator

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A component that reduces optical signal power by a specified amount.

## Usage and distinctions

Attenuator type, connector, wavelength, and required loss must match the application.

## Examples

> The design includes an optical attenuator.

> Verify the required attenuation against the transceiver specifications.
