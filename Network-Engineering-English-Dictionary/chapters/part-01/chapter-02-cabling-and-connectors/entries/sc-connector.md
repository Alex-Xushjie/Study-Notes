# SC connector

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A fiber-optic connector with a push-pull coupling mechanism.

## Usage and distinctions

SC and LC are distinct connector formats. Connector choice and fiber type must both match the application.

## Examples

> The fiber patch panel has SC adapters.

> Use the specified connector type at each end.
