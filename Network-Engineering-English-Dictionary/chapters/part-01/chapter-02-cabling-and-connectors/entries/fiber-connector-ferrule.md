# fiber connector ferrule

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A component of a fiber connector that holds and aligns the optical fiber end.

## Usage and distinctions

Ferrule geometry and connector end-face condition affect optical performance.

## Examples

> The technician inspected the connector ferrule.

> Keep the ferrule protected when the connector is not in use.
