# small form-factor pluggable transceiver

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A compact pluggable transceiver format used by network equipment.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: SFP. SFP-family modules differ in capabilities; form factor alone does not determine optics, speed, or reach.

## Examples

> The switch has an SFP transceiver installed.

> Check that the device supports the selected transceiver.
