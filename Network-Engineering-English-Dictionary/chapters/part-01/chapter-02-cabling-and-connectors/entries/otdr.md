# optical time-domain reflectometer

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An instrument that analyzes a fiber link using reflected and backscattered optical signals.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: OTDR. Trace interpretation depends on test setup, resolution, and fiber characteristics.

## Examples

> The OTDR trace shows an event near the splice.

> Record the test settings and direction.
