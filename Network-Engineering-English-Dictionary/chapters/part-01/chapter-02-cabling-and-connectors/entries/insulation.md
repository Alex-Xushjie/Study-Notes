# conductor insulation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A nonconductive covering that separates and protects a conductor.

## Usage and distinctions

Insulation is distinct from the cable’s outer jacket.

## Examples

> Each copper conductor has insulation.

> Avoid damaging the insulation during termination.
