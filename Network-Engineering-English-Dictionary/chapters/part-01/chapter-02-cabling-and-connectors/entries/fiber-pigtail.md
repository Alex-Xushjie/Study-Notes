# fiber pigtail

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A short fiber assembly with a connector at one end and a free fiber end for termination or splicing.

## Usage and distinctions

A pigtail normally has one connectorized end, unlike a patch cord with connectors at both ends.

## Examples

> The installer spliced the pigtail to the backbone fiber.

> Record the pigtail and splice identifiers.
