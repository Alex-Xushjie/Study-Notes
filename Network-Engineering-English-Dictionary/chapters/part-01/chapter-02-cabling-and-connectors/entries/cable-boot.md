# connector boot

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A protective sleeve or molded part fitted near a cable connector.

## Usage and distinctions

Boot styles vary and do not establish the cable’s category or compatibility.

## Examples

> The patch cord has a snagless connector boot.

> Check that the boot does not obstruct the latch.
