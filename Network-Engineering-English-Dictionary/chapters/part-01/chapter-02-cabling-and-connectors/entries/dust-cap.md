# fiber dust cap

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A protective cover used on an unused optical connector or port.

## Usage and distinctions

A dust cap does not guarantee that a connector is clean; inspection may still be required.

## Examples

> The unused transceiver port has a dust cap.

> Keep the dust cap in place until the connection is made.
