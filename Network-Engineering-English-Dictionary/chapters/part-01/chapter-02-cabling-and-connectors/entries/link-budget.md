# optical link budget

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An accounting of available optical power and expected losses between transmitter and receiver.

## Usage and distinctions

A useful budget considers transmitter power, receiver sensitivity, component losses, and design margin for the actual equipment.

## Examples

> Calculate the optical link budget before selecting the transceivers.

> Include connector and fiber losses in the link budget.
