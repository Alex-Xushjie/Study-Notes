# multimode fiber

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An optical fiber that supports multiple propagation modes at the intended operating wavelengths.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: MMF. Reach depends on the fiber grade, transceiver, data rate, and link loss.

## Examples

> The short link uses multimode fiber.

> Check the fiber grade and transceiver requirements.
