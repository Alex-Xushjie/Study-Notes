# optical power

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The power level of an optical signal at a defined measurement point.

## Usage and distinctions

Transmitted and received power are different values; interpret readings against the specific module’s limits.

## Examples

> The receiver reports low optical power.

> Record the measurement point and units.
