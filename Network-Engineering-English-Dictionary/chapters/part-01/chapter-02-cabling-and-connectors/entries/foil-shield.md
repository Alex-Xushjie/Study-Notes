# foil shield

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A thin metallic layer used as shielding in a cable.

## Usage and distinctions

A foil shield may be applied around pairs, the overall cable, or both.

## Examples

> The cable uses a foil shield around the pairs.

> Follow the connector instructions when terminating the shield.
