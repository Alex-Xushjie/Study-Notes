# twisted pair

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Two insulated conductors twisted together along their length.

## Usage and distinctions

Twisting helps reduce coupling and interference; termination quality also matters.

## Examples

> A four-pair cable contains four twisted pairs.

> Keep the pairs twisted as close to the termination as specified.
