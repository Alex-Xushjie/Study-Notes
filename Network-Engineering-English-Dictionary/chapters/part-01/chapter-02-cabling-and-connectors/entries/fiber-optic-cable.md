# fiber-optic cable

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A cable that carries optical signals through one or more glass or plastic fibers.

## Usage and distinctions

The cable is the complete protected assembly; an optical fiber is the signal-carrying strand inside it.

## Examples

> The fiber-optic cable connects the two network rooms.

> Verify the fiber type and connector before installation.
