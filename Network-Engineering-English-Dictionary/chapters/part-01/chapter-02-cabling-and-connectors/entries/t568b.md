# T568B wiring scheme

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A standardized arrangement of conductor positions used for balanced twisted-pair terminations.

## Usage and distinctions

T568B and T568A use different pair position assignments; the selected scheme must be documented.

## Examples

> The installer terminated the panel using T568B.

> Check that the termination matches the approved plan.
