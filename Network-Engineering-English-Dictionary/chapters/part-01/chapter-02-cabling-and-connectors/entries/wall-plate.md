[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A cover or mounting plate that holds one or more network jacks at an outlet.

## Usage and distinctions

A wall plate can hold different modules; inspect the installed jack type.
