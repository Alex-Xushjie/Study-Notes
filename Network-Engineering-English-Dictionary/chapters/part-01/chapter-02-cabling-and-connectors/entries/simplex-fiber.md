# simplex fiber

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A fiber connection or cable assembly using one optical fiber.

## Usage and distinctions

One fiber can support bidirectional transmission with suitable equipment; simplex does not necessarily mean one-way service.

## Examples

> The technician installed a simplex fiber patch cord.

> Confirm that the optical modules support the planned single-fiber design.
