# copper conductor

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A copper element that carries an electrical signal or current.

## Usage and distinctions

Conductors may be solid or stranded; suitability depends on the cable and connector.

## Examples

> Each twisted pair contains two copper conductors.

> Inspect the conductors before terminating the cable.
