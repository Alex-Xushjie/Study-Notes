# cable termination scheme

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A defined arrangement for connecting conductors to termination positions.

## Usage and distinctions

Different schemes exist; record which is used rather than assuming from appearance.

## Examples

> Both ends follow the approved termination scheme.

> Check the wiring scheme on the patch panel.
