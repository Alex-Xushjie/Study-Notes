# cable label

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An identifier attached to a cable to help trace and document its endpoints or purpose.

## Usage and distinctions

A label supports identification but should be verified against current records and observed endpoints.

## Examples

> The cable label matches the port schedule.

> Update the label after the cable is moved.
