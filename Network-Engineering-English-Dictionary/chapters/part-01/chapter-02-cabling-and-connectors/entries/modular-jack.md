# modular jack

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A receptacle designed to accept a compatible modular plug.

## Usage and distinctions

A jack is a receptacle; a plug is the mating connector.

## Examples

> The modular jack is installed in the wall plate.

> Check the jack’s category and pin assignment.
