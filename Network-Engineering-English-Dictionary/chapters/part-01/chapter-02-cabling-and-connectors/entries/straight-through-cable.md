# straight-through cable

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A cable assembly in which corresponding connector positions are connected end to end.

## Usage and distinctions

Device interface behavior and auto-MDI/MDIX support affect when a crossover is needed.
