# shielded twisted pair

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A twisted-pair cable construction that uses metallic shielding to reduce unwanted electromagnetic effects.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: STP is used broadly; the exact foil and braid construction should be specified rather than inferred from the abbreviation.

## Examples

> The design specifies shielded twisted-pair cable.

> Check the grounding requirements for the selected cable system.
