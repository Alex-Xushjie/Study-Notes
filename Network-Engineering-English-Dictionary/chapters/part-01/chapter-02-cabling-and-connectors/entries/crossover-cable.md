# crossover cable

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 02

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A cable assembly in which specified conductor paths are crossed between its ends.

## Usage and distinctions

Modern interfaces may support auto-MDI/MDIX; do not assume every device requires a crossover cable.

## Examples

> The lab uses a crossover cable for a legacy connection.

> Confirm the interface requirements before selecting the cable.
