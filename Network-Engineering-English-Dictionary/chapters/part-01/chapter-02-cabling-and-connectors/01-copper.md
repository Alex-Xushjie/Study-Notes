| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [twisted-pair cable](entries/twisted-pair-cable.md) | A cable with pairs of twisted copper conductors. |

| 3 | [Ethernet cable](entries/ethernet-cable.md) | A cable used for an Ethernet connection. |
| 4 | [keystone jack](entries/keystone-jack.md) | A snap-in jack module for a compatible opening. |

## Examples and usage

> The patch cord connects the wall jack to the switch.

> Check that the modular plug and jack are securely connected.

> The cable jacket is marked with its category.

> Route the cable without exceeding its specified minimum bend radius.
