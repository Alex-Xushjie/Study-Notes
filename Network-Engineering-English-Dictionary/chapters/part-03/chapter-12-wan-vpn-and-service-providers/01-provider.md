## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [customer edge](entries/customer-edge.md) | The customer-side device or edge connecting to a provider. |
| [network handoff](entries/handoff.md) | The presentation at which connectivity is delivered. |
| [provider edge](entries/provider-edge.md) | The provider network edge connecting to customers. |
| [carrier circuit](entries/carrier-circuit.md) | A connectivity service supplied by a carrier. |

## Examples and usage

> Confirm the handoff speed and connector.

> The customer-edge router connects to the provider handoff.

> Include the circuit ID in the provider ticket.

> Confirm the demarcation point before escalating.
