# network overlay

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical network built on top of an underlying transport network.

## Usage and distinctions

An overlay typically uses encapsulation, but encryption and policy are separate design choices.

## Examples

> The overlay carries traffic across the WAN.

> Verify both the overlay routes and the underlay path.
