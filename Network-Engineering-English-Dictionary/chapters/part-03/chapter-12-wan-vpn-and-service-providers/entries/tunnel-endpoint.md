# tunnel endpoint

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A device or address at one end of a network tunnel.

## Usage and distinctions

Tunnel endpoint addresses and inner network addresses are distinct concepts.

## Examples

> The tunnel endpoint uses the provider-facing address.

> Confirm that the remote tunnel endpoint is reachable.
