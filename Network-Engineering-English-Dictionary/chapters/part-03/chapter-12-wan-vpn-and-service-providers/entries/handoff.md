# network handoff

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The interface or service presentation at which one party delivers connectivity to another.

## Usage and distinctions

Handoff details include medium, encapsulation, physical interface, and responsibility boundary as applicable.

## Examples

> The provider delivers an Ethernet handoff.

> Confirm the handoff speed and connector.
