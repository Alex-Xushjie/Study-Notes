# last mile

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The portion of a connectivity service between the customer location and a nearby provider network point.

## Usage and distinctions

Last mile describes a service segment, not a guaranteed distance or physical technology.

## Examples

> The provider is testing the last-mile connection.

> Confirm who owns the last-mile segment.
