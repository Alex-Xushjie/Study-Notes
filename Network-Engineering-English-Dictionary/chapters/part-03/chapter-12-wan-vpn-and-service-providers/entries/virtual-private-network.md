# virtual private network

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical private network service built over shared or public infrastructure.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: VPN. A VPN does not necessarily provide encryption; properties depend on the technology and configuration.

## Examples

> The branches use a VPN to exchange private traffic.

> Confirm the VPN topology and security requirements.
