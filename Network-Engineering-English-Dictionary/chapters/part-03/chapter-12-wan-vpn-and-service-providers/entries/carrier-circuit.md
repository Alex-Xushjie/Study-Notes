# carrier circuit

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A connectivity service or circuit supplied by a communications provider.

## Usage and distinctions

Circuit can refer to different service types; include the provider, endpoints, bandwidth, and service identifier.

## Examples

> The carrier circuit terminates in the equipment room.

> Open a provider ticket using the circuit ID.
