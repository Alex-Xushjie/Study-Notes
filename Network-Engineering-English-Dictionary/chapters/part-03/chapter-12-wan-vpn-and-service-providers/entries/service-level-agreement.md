# service-level agreement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An agreement defining service commitments and how performance or availability is measured.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: SLA. The contractual terms, measurement period, and exclusions determine the actual commitment.

## Examples

> The service-level agreement defines the availability target.

> Check the SLA measurement and exclusions.
