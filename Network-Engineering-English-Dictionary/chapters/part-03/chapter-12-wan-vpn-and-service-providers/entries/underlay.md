# network underlay

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The underlying network that provides transport for an overlay or tunnel.

## Usage and distinctions

An underlay can use different routing and transport technologies.

## Examples

> The IP underlay reaches both tunnel endpoints.

> Check underlay connectivity before troubleshooting the overlay.
