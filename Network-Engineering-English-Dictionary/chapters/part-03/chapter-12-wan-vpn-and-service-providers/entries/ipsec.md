# Internet Protocol Security

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A suite of protocols used to protect IP traffic through authentication, encryption, or both.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: IPsec. Security properties and tunnel behavior depend on the protocols and configuration used.
