# customer-premises equipment

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Equipment located at the customer site and used as part of a communications service.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: CPE. Equipment location does not by itself establish ownership or management responsibility.

## Examples

> The provider installed customer-premises equipment at the branch.

> Check whether the CPE is managed by the provider.
