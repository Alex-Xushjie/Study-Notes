# site-to-site VPN

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A VPN connecting networks at separate locations through gateway devices.

## Usage and distinctions

A tunnel being up does not guarantee all application traffic is permitted or routed correctly.

## Examples

> The site-to-site VPN connects the branch and headquarters.

> Check the tunnel and the routes to both site subnets.
