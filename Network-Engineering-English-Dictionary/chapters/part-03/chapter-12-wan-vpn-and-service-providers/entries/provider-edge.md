# provider edge

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The part of a service-provider network that connects to customer or external networks.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: PE. The role and protocols depend on the service architecture.

## Examples

> The customer router peers with the provider edge.

> Ask the carrier to check the provider-edge interface.
