# Multiprotocol Label Switching

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A forwarding technology that uses labels to direct packets through a network.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: MPLS. MPLS is a forwarding mechanism; a particular MPLS VPN service has additional control-plane and service characteristics.

## Examples

> The provider uses MPLS in its backbone.

> Confirm the service topology and routing responsibilities.
