# customer edge

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The customer-side device or network edge that connects to a service provider.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: CE. The device may be managed by the customer or by a provider under an agreement.

## Examples

> The customer-edge router connects to the provider handoff.

> Check the interface between the CE and PE.
