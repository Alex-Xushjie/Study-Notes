# encryption

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The transformation of data into a form intended to be readable only by authorized parties with the appropriate key.

## Usage and distinctions

Encryption is distinct from authentication, integrity protection, and encapsulation.

## Examples

> IPsec encryption protects the payload in transit.

> Verify the encryption requirements in the design.
