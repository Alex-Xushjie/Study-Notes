# leased line

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A dedicated connectivity service supplied between locations under a service agreement.

## Usage and distinctions

Leased-line services vary; the service description determines the actual physical and logical characteristics.

## Examples

> The branch uses a leased line to reach the data center.

> Confirm the leased line bandwidth and handoff type.
