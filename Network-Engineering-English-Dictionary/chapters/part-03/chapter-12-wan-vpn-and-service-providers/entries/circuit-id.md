# circuit ID

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An identifier used to distinguish a provider circuit or service.

## Usage and distinctions

The provider may use several identifiers for one service; record the exact reference used for support.

## Examples

> Include the circuit ID in the provider ticket.

> Confirm that the circuit ID matches the affected site.
