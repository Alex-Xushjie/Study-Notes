# wide area network

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A network that connects locations over a broad geographic area.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: WAN. A WAN can use different transport services and technologies.

## Examples

> The wide area network connects the branches to headquarters.

> Check the WAN path to the remote site.
