# demarcation point

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The boundary between the provider’s network responsibility and the customer’s network responsibility.

## Usage and distinctions

Also called demarc. The exact physical location and responsibility split depend on the contract.

## Examples

> The provider will test the circuit up to the demarcation point.

> Confirm the demarcation point before escalating.
