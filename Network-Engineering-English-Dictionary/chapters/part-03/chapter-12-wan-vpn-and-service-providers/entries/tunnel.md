# network tunnel

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 12

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical path that carries one type of packet within another protocol or transport.

## Usage and distinctions

A tunnel provides encapsulation; encryption and authentication depend on the specific technology.

## Examples

> The router sends the packet through the tunnel.

> Verify tunnel endpoints and the underlay path.
