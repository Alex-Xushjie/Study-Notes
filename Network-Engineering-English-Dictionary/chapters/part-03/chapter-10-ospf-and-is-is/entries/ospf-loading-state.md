# OSPF Loading state

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A neighbor state in which a router requests link-state information needed to complete database synchronization.

## Usage and distinctions

The router moves toward Full after the needed link-state information has been received.

## Examples

> The neighbor remains in the OSPF Loading state.

> Inspect retransmissions and link-state requests during Loading.
