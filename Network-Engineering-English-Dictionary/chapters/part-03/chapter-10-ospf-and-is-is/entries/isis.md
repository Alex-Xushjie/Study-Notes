# Intermediate System to Intermediate System

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A link-state interior gateway protocol that distributes routing information between intermediate systems.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: IS-IS. Its terminology and wire behavior differ from OSPF even though both are link-state protocols.

## Examples

> The backbone uses IS-IS for internal routing.

> Check the IS-IS adjacency on the link.
