# OSPF link-state database

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The collection of link-state advertisements maintained by an OSPF router.

## Usage and distinctions

Routers synchronize the relevant area database; the database is distinct from the routing table.

## Examples

> Compare the OSPF link-state database on both routers.

> A changed link-state advertisement triggered an SPF calculation.
