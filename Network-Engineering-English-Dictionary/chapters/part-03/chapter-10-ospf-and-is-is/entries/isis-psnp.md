# IS-IS partial sequence number PDU

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An IS-IS protocol data unit used to acknowledge or request particular link-state database entries.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: PSNP. Its exact use depends on whether the circuit is point-to-point or broadcast.

## Examples

> The router sends a PSNP to request a missing LSP.

> Check PSNP exchanges when database synchronization stalls.
