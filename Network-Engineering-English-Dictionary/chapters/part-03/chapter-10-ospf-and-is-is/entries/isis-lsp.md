# IS-IS link-state PDU

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An IS-IS protocol data unit containing link-state information.

## Usage and distinctions

IS-IS LSP means link-state PDU; it is not the same meaning as an MPLS label-switched path.

## Examples

> The router floods the updated IS-IS LSP.

> Compare the LSP information in the Level 2 database.
