# OSPF Full state

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A neighbor state indicating that the routers have synchronized their link-state databases for the adjacency.

## Usage and distinctions

Full adjacency alone does not prove that every desired route is installed or reachable.

## Examples

> The point-to-point neighbors reached the OSPF Full state.

> Verify that the designated router has Full adjacencies with the other routers.
