# OSPF 2-Way state

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A neighbor state indicating that bidirectional communication has been established through Hello packets.

## Usage and distinctions

Full adjacency is not required between every pair of routers on a broadcast network.

## Examples

> Both routers reached the OSPF 2-Way state.

> On a broadcast network, some neighbors normally remain in 2-Way.
