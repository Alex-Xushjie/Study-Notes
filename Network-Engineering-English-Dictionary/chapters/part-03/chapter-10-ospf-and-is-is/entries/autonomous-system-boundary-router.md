# autonomous system boundary router

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An OSPF router that introduces external routing information into the OSPF domain.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: ASBR. Redistribution and external route behavior depend on protocol version and policy.

## Examples

> The ASBR redistributes the external route into OSPF.

> Check the external route policy on the ASBR.
