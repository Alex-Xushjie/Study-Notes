# IS-IS designated intermediate system

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The router elected to perform designated functions on an IS-IS broadcast circuit.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: DIS. Its election and failover behavior differ from the OSPF designated router.

## Examples

> The IS-IS designated intermediate system originates the pseudonode LSP.

> Check the DIS election on the shared segment.
