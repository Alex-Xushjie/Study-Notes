# LSA flooding

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The distribution of OSPF link-state advertisements to relevant routers.

## Usage and distinctions

Reliable flooding and scope rules are protocol-specific; flooding does not mean sending every LSA to every router.

## Examples

> The router floods the updated LSA to its neighbors.

> Check whether the LSA reaches the appropriate area.
