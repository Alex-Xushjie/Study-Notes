# IS-IS complete sequence number PDU

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An IS-IS protocol data unit that summarizes a set of link-state database entries.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: CSNP. Its use and periodic transmission depend on the circuit type.

## Examples

> The designated intermediate system sends a complete sequence number PDU.

> Compare the CSNP with the local link-state database.
