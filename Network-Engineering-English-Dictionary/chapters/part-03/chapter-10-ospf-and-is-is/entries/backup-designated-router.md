# OSPF backup designated router

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A router elected to take over the designated-router role if needed on certain multi-access networks.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: BDR. DR/BDR roles are not used on every OSPF network type.

## Examples

> The backup designated router is visible in the neighbor output.

> Verify the BDR role on the segment.
