# OSPF hello packet

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An OSPF message used for neighbor discovery and maintaining neighbor relationships.

## Usage and distinctions

Hello parameters must be compatible according to the OSPF version and network type.

## Examples

> The router sends hello packets on the OSPF interface.

> Confirm that hello packets are received from the neighbor.
