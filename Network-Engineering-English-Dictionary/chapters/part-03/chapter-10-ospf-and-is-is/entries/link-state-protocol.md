# link-state routing protocol

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A routing protocol that distributes link-state information and calculates paths from a topology database.

## Usage and distinctions

The exact database, flooding, and path calculation rules depend on the protocol.

## Examples

> OSPF and IS-IS are link-state routing protocols.

> Check whether the routers have consistent topology information.
