# OSPF link-state advertisement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An OSPF data structure describing part of the topology or routing information.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: LSA. Types and flooding scopes vary by OSPF version and area type.

## Examples

> The router floods the LSA within its applicable scope.

> Check whether the expected LSA is present in the database.
