# OSPF area

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical grouping of routers and links used to organize OSPF routing information.

## Usage and distinctions

Area design and LSA scope depend on the OSPF version and area type.

## Examples

> The access routers are in OSPF area 10.

> Verify the area configured on both sides of the link.
