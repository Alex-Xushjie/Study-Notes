# OSPF backbone area

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

OSPF area 0, which forms the backbone of a multi-area OSPF design.

## Usage and distinctions

The precise multi-area design must follow the relevant OSPF specification; special mechanisms such as virtual links have their own constraints.

## Examples

> The area border router connects the site area to area 0.

> Check backbone connectivity when inter-area routes are missing.
