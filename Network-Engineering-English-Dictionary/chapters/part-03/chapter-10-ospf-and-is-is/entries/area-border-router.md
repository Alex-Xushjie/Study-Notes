# area border router

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An OSPF router that connects multiple areas, including the backbone in a standard multi-area design.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: ABR. Platform behavior can include additional rules for when a router acts as an ABR.

## Examples

> The area border router advertises inter-area information.

> Verify the ABR’s area connections.
