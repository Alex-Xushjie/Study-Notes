# IS-IS Level 2

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An IS-IS routing level used for inter-area or backbone routing.

## Usage and distinctions

An IS-IS router can participate in Level 1, Level 2, or both, depending on configuration.
