# link-state database

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A collection of link-state information maintained by a router for a protocol scope.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: LSDB. Database scope and representation vary by protocol and configuration.
