# OSPF external type 2 route

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An OSPF external route whose external metric is the primary metric used when comparing type 2 external paths.

## Usage and distinctions

Often written E2. Other selection rules and tie-breakers also apply.

## Examples

> The redistributed prefix appears as an OSPF external type 2 route.

> Inspect the internal cost when external metrics are equal.
