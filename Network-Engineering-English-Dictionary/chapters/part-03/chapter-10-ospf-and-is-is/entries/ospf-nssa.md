# OSPF not-so-stubby area

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An OSPF area type that limits conventional external advertisements while permitting certain external routes to be introduced within the area.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: NSSA. The treatment of Type 7 advertisements depends on the configured topology and translators.

## Examples

> The branch uses an OSPF not-so-stubby area to redistribute a local route.

> Check the translation of the NSSA external advertisement.
