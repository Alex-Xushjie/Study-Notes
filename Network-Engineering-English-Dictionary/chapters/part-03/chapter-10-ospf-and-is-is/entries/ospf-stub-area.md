# OSPF stub area

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An OSPF area type that limits the flooding of external routes and typically receives a default route from an area border router.

## Usage and distinctions

A stub area is different from an NSSA; exact supported options depend on the implementation.
