# OSPF designated router

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A router elected on certain multi-access networks to coordinate link-state exchange.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: DR. DR elections and adjacency behavior depend on network type and priority.

## Examples

> The designated router is elected on the broadcast segment.

> Check the designated-router and backup roles.
