# IS-IS network entity title

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An ISO network address identifying the IS-IS routing process and its area context.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: NET. NET formatting and uniqueness requirements must follow the deployed IS-IS specification and implementation.

## Examples

> The router’s IS-IS NET includes an area address and system ID.

> Check that the system IDs are unique where required.
