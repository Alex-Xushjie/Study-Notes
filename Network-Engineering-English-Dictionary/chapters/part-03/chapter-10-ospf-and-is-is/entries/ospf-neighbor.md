# OSPF neighbor

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 10

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A router discovered on a shared link through OSPF hello exchanges.

## Usage and distinctions

Being a neighbor does not always mean that a full adjacency is formed, especially on multi-access networks.

## Examples

> The OSPF neighbor is visible on the interface.

> Check the hello and dead timers on both neighbors.
