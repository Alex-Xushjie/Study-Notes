## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [AS_PATH](entries/as-path.md) | An attribute containing autonomous-system path segments. |
| [BGP NEXT_HOP](entries/bgp-next-hop.md) | Next-hop information associated with a route. |
| [BGP LOCAL_PREF](entries/local-preference.md) | An attribute expressing preference within an AS. |
| [BGP community](entries/bgp-community.md) | An attribute used to group or label routes for policy. |

## Examples and usage

> Check the AS_PATH for unexpected entries.

> Verify that the BGP next hop is reachable.

> The policy sets a higher local preference for the primary exit.

> Check how the provider interprets the community.
