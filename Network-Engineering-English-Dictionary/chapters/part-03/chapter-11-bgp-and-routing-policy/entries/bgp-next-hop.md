# BGP NEXT_HOP

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A BGP path attribute or associated next-hop information identifying a forwarding next hop.

## Usage and distinctions

Next-hop behavior depends on the address family, peer relationship, and configuration; reachability must be verified.

## Examples

> The BGP next hop is not reachable.

> Check the route used to resolve the BGP next hop.
