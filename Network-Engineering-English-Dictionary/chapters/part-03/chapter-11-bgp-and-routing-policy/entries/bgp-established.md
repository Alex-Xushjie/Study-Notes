# BGP Established state

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The BGP finite-state-machine state in which peers can exchange UPDATE messages.

## Usage and distinctions

Established indicates session state; it does not guarantee that the intended prefixes are accepted or selected.

## Examples

> The BGP neighbor is in the Established state.

> Check received and advertised prefixes after the session is established.
