# BGP peer

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A router that exchanges BGP messages with another BGP speaker.

## Usage and distinctions

Also called a BGP neighbor. A configured peer does not necessarily have an established session.

## Examples

> The BGP peer is configured with the remote autonomous system number.

> Confirm the peer address and reachability.
