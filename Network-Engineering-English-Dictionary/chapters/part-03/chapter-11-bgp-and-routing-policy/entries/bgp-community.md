# BGP community

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An attribute used to group or label routes for routing policy.

## Usage and distinctions

Standard, extended, and large communities have different formats and semantics. Meaning is defined by the relevant policy or specification.

## Examples

> The policy adds a community to the advertised prefix.

> Check how the provider interprets the community.
