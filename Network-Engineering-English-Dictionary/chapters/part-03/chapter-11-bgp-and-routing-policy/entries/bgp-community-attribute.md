# BGP community attribute

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A BGP attribute used to attach policy labels to a route.

## Usage and distinctions

A community is a policy signal; its effect depends on the receiving network’s configuration.

## Examples

> Tag the route with a BGP community attribute.

> Confirm that the provider recognizes the community before relying on it.
