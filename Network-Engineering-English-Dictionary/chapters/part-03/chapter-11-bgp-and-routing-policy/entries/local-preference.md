# BGP LOCAL_PREF

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A BGP attribute used within an autonomous system to express a preference among routes.

## Usage and distinctions

Higher local preference is commonly preferred within an AS; exact selection behavior depends on implementation and policy.

## Examples

> The policy sets a higher local preference for the primary exit.

> Compare LOCAL_PREF on the candidate paths.
