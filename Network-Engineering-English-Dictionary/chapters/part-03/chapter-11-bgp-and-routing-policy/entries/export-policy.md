# BGP export policy

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Rules applied to routes sent to a BGP peer.

## Usage and distinctions

Also called outbound policy. A locally known route may still be suppressed by policy or protocol rules.

## Examples

> The export policy permits the customer prefix.

> Check the export policy if the peer does not receive the route.
