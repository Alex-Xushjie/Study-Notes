**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A category of network reachability information identified by address-family parameters.

## Usage and distinctions

Address family support and activation are separate from merely establishing a TCP session.

## Examples

> Enable the BGP address family for IPv6 unicast.
