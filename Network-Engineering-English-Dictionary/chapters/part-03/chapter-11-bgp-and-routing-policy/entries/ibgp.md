# internal BGP

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A BGP relationship between peers in the same autonomous system.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: iBGP. Route propagation rules and mechanisms such as route reflectors affect how routes are distributed.

## Examples

> The routers exchange BGP routes through iBGP.

> Check internal next-hop reachability.
