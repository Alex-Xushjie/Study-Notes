# BGP route flap

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Repeated announcement and withdrawal of a route or repeated changes in its availability.

## Usage and distinctions

A route flap can have several causes; record timestamps and evidence before assigning a cause.

## Examples

> The prefix is flapping during the incident.

> Check the peer stability and underlying connectivity.
