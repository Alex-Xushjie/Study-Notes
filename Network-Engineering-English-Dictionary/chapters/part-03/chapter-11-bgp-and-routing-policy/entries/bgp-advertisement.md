# BGP route advertisement

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The announcement of reachability information to a BGP peer.

## Usage and distinctions

A route installed locally is not automatically advertised to every peer; policy and protocol rules apply.

## Examples

> The router advertises the prefix to the provider.

> Check the export policy before changing the peer.
