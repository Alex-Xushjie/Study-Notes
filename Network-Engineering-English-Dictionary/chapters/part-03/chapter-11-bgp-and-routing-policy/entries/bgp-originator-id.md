# BGP ORIGINATOR_ID

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A route-reflection attribute that identifies the originator of a reflected internal BGP route.

## Usage and distinctions

This attribute is distinct from the BGP ORIGIN attribute.

## Examples

> Inspect the BGP ORIGINATOR_ID on the reflected route.

> The router rejects a reflected route that identifies itself as the originator.
