# BGP routing policy

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A set of rules controlling how BGP routes are accepted, modified, selected, or advertised.

## Usage and distinctions

Policy behavior is ordered and platform-specific; a permitted session is not equivalent to permission for every route.

## Examples

> The routing policy rejects unexpected prefixes.

> Review the policy before enabling the session.
