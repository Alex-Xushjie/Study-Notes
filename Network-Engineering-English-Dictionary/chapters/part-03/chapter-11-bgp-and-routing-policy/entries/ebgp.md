# external BGP

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A BGP relationship between peers in different autonomous systems.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: eBGP. Exact next-hop, policy, and hop-limit behavior depends on the configuration and implementation.

## Examples

> The edge router uses eBGP to peer with the provider.

> Verify the local and remote AS numbers.
