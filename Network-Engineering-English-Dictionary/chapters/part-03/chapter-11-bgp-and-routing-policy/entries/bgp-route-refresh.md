# BGP route refresh

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A BGP capability and message mechanism that requests a peer to resend routing information.

## Usage and distinctions

The requested address family and platform behavior determine what is resent.

## Examples

> Request a BGP route refresh after changing the inbound policy.

> Confirm that both peers negotiated route refresh support.
