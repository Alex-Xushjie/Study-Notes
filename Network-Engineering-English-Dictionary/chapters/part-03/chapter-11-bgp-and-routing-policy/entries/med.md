# BGP multi-exit discriminator

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A BGP attribute that can communicate a preferred entry point to a neighboring autonomous system.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: MED. Comparison rules and handling depend on implementation and configuration; lower is commonly preferred when compared.
