# BGP Loc-RIB

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The conceptual collection of BGP routes selected by the local BGP decision process.

## Usage and distinctions

A route selected by BGP may still be subject to installation and forwarding checks.
