# BGP route-reflector client

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An internal BGP peer designated as a client of a route reflector.

## Usage and distinctions

Client status is configured on the reflector; outbound policy still applies.

## Examples

> Configure the new router as a BGP route-reflector client.

> Check whether the route reflector advertises the route to the client.
