# BGP route withdrawal

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 11

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A message or action that removes a previously advertised BGP route from a peer.

## Usage and distinctions

Withdrawal of one path does not necessarily remove all available paths to the destination.

## Examples

> The router withdraws the prefix after the link failure.

> Check whether the provider received the withdrawal.
