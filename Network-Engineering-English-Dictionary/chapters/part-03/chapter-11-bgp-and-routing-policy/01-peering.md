## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [autonomous system](entries/autonomous-system.md) | A routing administration represented by an ASN. |
| [BGP UPDATE](entries/bgp-update.md) | A message carrying reachability information. |
| [BGP peer](entries/bgp-peer.md) | A router exchanging BGP messages with another speaker. |
| [BGP session](entries/bgp-session.md) | A connection used to exchange BGP messages. |

## Examples and usage

> The BGP session is established.

> The router advertises the prefix to its peer.

> Verify the local and remote AS numbers.

> An established session does not prove that the intended routes are accepted.
