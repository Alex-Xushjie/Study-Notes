# destination prefix

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An address prefix identifying the destinations covered by a route.

## Usage and distinctions

A prefix is matched against the destination address during forwarding; the most specific matching route generally wins.

## Examples

> The destination prefix is 192.0.2.0/24.

> Compare the packet destination with the route prefix.
