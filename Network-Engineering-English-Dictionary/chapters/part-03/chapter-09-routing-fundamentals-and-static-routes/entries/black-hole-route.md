# black-hole route

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A route or forwarding condition that causes matching traffic to be discarded.

## Usage and distinctions

A discard route can be deliberate for aggregation or filtering; traffic can also be black-holed unintentionally.

## Examples

> The aggregate route points to a discard interface.

> Confirm whether the discard route is intentional.
