# multicast distribution tree

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The set of forwarding paths used to carry multicast traffic to receivers.

## Usage and distinctions

Distribution trees may be shared or source-specific depending on the service and protocol.
