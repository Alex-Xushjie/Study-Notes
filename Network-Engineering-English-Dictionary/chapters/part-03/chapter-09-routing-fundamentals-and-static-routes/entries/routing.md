# routing

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The selection of paths for forwarding packets between networks.

## Usage and distinctions

Routing decisions depend on the routing information and implementation; routing and packet forwarding are related but distinct.

## Examples

> The router uses routing information to choose a path.

> Check routing in both directions.
