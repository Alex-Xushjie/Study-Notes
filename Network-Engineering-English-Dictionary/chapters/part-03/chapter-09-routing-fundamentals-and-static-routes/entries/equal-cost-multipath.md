# equal-cost multipath

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The use of multiple eligible paths with equal routing cost for the same destination.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: ECMP. Hashing and per-flow or per-packet behavior depend on the platform and configuration.

## Examples

> The router has two equal-cost paths to the destination.

> Check how traffic is distributed across the ECMP paths.
