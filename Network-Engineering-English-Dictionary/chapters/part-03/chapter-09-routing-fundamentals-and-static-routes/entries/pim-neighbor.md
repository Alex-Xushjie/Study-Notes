# PIM neighbor

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A router discovered through PIM messages on a shared network.

## Usage and distinctions

Neighbor presence alone does not establish all required multicast forwarding state.

## Examples

> The router lists a PIM neighbor on the interface.

> Verify that PIM hellos are received on both sides.
