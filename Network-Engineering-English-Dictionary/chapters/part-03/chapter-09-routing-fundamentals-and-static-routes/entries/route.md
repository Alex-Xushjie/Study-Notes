# route

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A routing entry describing a destination prefix and associated forwarding information.

## Usage and distinctions

A route normally includes a destination prefix and may include a next hop, interface, metric, and source.
