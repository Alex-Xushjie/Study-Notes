# (S,G) multicast state

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Multicast forwarding or control state identified by a source and group.

## Usage and distinctions

(S,G) identifies a source-group pair; the state’s behavior depends on the protocol.

## Examples

> The router has (S,G) state for the feed.

> Verify the source, group, and outgoing interfaces.
