# multicast boundary

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A configured point at which multicast forwarding or group scope is restricted.

## Usage and distinctions

Boundary behavior and supported controls depend on protocol and device configuration.
