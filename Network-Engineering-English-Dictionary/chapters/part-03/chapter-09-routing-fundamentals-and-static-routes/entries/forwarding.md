# packet forwarding

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The process of sending a received packet toward its next destination according to forwarding information.

## Usage and distinctions

A route can exist while actual forwarding fails because of an unresolved next hop, policy, or other conditions.

## Examples

> The router forwards the packet to the next hop.

> Verify that the selected forwarding path is usable.
