# next hop

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The next router or directly reachable forwarding destination on a path.

## Usage and distinctions

The next hop is not necessarily the final destination. Some routes use an outgoing interface or platform-specific adjacency.

## Examples

> The route points to next hop 192.0.2.1.

> Verify that the next hop is reachable.
