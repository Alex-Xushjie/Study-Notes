# recursive route lookup

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 09

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The resolution of a route’s next hop through another routing lookup.

## Usage and distinctions

A next-hop route can exist but be unusable because its resolution fails or is restricted.

## Examples

> The router resolves the BGP next hop through an IGP route.

> Check the route used to resolve the next hop.
