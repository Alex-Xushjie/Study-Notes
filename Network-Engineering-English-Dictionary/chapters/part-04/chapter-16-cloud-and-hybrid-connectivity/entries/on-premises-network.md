# on-premises network

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 16

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A network operated at an organization’s own site or facilities.

## Usage and distinctions

On-premises describes location and operational context; ownership and management arrangements may vary.

## Examples

> The on-premises network connects to the cloud through a gateway.

> Check the on-premises route to the cloud prefix.
