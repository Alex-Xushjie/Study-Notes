# load balancer health check

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 16

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A periodic probe used to assess whether a target is eligible to receive traffic.

## Usage and distinctions

A successful health check covers the configured probe; it does not prove every application feature works.

## Examples

> The health check reports the target as unhealthy.

> Verify the probe path, port, and expected response.
