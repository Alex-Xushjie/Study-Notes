# power sourcing equipment

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Equipment that supplies power to a powered device over Ethernet cabling.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: PSE. A PoE switch and a compatible power injector are examples.

## Examples

> The switch acts as power sourcing equipment for the access point.

> Verify the PSE power capacity.
