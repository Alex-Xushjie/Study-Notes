# PoE power budget

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The total power capacity available for powered devices on a PoE system or switch.

## Usage and distinctions

The usable budget depends on power supplies, redundancy mode, temperature, and platform configuration.

## Examples

> The switch has insufficient PoE budget for all access points.

> Compare the requested power with the available budget.
