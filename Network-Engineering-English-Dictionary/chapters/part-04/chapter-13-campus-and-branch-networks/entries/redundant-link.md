# redundant link

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An additional connection intended to provide an alternative path.

## Usage and distinctions

A redundant link does not automatically provide independent failure protection; check shared devices, power, pathways, and protocol behavior.

## Examples

> The access switch has a redundant uplink.

> Test the redundant link during the maintenance window.
