# distribution layer

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The part of a hierarchical design that aggregates access connections and may apply routing or policy.

## Usage and distinctions

Responsibilities differ by design; the distribution layer is not necessarily present as a separate device tier.

## Examples

> The distribution layer aggregates the access switches.

> Check the uplink from the access switch to the distribution layer.
