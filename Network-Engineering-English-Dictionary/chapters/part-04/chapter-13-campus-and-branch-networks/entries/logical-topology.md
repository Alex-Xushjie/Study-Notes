# logical topology

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The arrangement of network relationships as seen by traffic or protocols.

## Usage and distinctions

Logical topology need not correspond one-to-one with the physical cabling.

## Examples

> The logical topology shows the VLAN and routing boundaries.

> Check the logical topology against the current configuration.
