# site resilience

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The ability of a site network to maintain or recover service when components fail.

## Usage and distinctions

Resilience includes dependencies such as power, equipment, physical paths, operations, and recovery processes.

## Examples

> The design improves site resilience with diverse uplinks.

> Check whether the uplinks share the same pathway.
