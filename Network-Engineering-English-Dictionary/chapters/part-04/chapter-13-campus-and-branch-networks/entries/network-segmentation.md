# network segmentation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The division of a network into smaller logical or physical sections.

## Usage and distinctions

Segmentation boundaries and enforcement depend on the design; a VLAN alone does not define every security control.

## Examples

> The design uses segmentation to separate guest and corporate traffic.

> Verify the policy between the segments.
