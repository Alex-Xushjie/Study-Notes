# Power over Ethernet

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A family of technologies that deliver electrical power over suitable Ethernet cabling.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: PoE. Standards, power classes, cable, switch, and device support determine available power.

## Examples

> The switch supplies power to the access point using PoE.

> Check the available PoE budget before connecting the device.
