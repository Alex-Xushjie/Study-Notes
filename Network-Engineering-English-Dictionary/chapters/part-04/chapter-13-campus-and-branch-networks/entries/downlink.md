# downlink

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A connection toward downstream devices or another layer of the topology.

## Usage and distinctions

Downlink is relative to a device’s role and the observer’s perspective.

## Examples

> The distribution switch has downlinks to the access switches.

> Label the downlink with the remote device name.
