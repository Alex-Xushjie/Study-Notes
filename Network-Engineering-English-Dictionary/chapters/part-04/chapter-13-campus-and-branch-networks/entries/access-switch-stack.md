# switch stack

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A group of compatible switches managed or operating together as a logical system, depending on the platform.

## Usage and distinctions

Stacking is vendor- and product-specific; it is not the same as simply connecting independent switches.
