# network topology

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The arrangement of network devices and their connections.

## Usage and distinctions

Physical topology describes actual connections; logical topology describes traffic or protocol relationships.

## Examples

> The topology diagram shows two uplinks from each access block.
