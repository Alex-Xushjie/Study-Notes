# uplink

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A connection toward another network device or a more central part of a topology.

## Usage and distinctions

Uplink is a relative role, not a specific connector or data rate.

## Examples

> The access switch has two uplinks.

> Check both ends of the uplink.
