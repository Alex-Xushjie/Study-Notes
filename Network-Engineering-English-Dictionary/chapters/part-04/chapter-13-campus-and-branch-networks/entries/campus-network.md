# campus network

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A network connecting users, devices, and services across a campus or site.

## Usage and distinctions

A campus network can contain multiple buildings and layers; its design varies by scale and requirements.

## Examples

> The campus network connects the office buildings.

> Review the campus topology before the change.
