# failure domain

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A set of components or services that can be affected by a particular failure.

## Usage and distinctions

Failure-domain boundaries depend on the failure scenario and system design.

## Examples

> The two uplinks share a power failure domain.

> Identify shared dependencies before claiming redundancy.
