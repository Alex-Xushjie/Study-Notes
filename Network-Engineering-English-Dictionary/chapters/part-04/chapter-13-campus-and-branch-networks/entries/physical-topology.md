# physical topology

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The arrangement of physical devices, ports, and cabling.

## Usage and distinctions

Physical and logical topologies can differ, especially when virtual interfaces or overlays are used.

## Examples

> The physical topology shows the cable between the switches.

> Update the physical topology after moving the uplink.
