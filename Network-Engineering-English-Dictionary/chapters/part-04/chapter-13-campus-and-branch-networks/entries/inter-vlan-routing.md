# inter-VLAN routing

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The forwarding of IP traffic between different VLAN-associated subnets.

## Usage and distinctions

Inter-VLAN routing requires a routing function and appropriate interfaces or gateways; policy can restrict traffic.

## Examples

> The distribution switch performs inter-VLAN routing.

> Check the gateway and policy for both VLANs.
