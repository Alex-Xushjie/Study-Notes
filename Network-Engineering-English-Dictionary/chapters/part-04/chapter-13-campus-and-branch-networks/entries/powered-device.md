# powered device

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A device that receives power through a Power over Ethernet connection.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: PD. The required power class and compatibility depend on the device and PoE standard.

## Examples

> The powered device requests power from the switch.

> Check whether the powered device is detected.
