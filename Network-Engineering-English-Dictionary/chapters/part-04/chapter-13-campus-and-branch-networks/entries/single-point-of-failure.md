# single point of failure

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 13

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A component whose failure can interrupt a dependent service.

## Usage and distinctions

A single point of failure is relative to the service and failure scenario being analyzed.

## Examples

> The single power supply is a single point of failure.

> Identify single points of failure in the design review.
