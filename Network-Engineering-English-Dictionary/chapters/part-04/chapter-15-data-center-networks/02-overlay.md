## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [fabric underlay](entries/fabric-underlay.md) | The network providing transport for an overlay. |
| [VXLAN](entries/vxlan.md) | An encapsulation technology carrying Layer 2 frames over IP. |
| [VXLAN network identifier](entries/vni.md) | A 24-bit field identifying a VXLAN segment. |
| [VXLAN tunnel endpoint](entries/vtep.md) | A function that encapsulates and decapsulates VXLAN traffic. |

## Examples and usage

> Verify underlay reachability before checking the overlay.

> The leaf switch acts as a VXLAN tunnel endpoint.

> Check the VNI mapping on the relevant devices.

> An operational underlay alone does not prove correct overlay state.
