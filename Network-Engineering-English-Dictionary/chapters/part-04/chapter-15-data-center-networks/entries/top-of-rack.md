# top-of-rack switching

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A design placing a network switch in or near each server rack to connect local devices.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: ToR. The switch’s literal rack position can vary; the term describes a deployment pattern.

## Examples

> The servers connect to the top-of-rack switch.

> Verify the uplinks from the rack switch.
