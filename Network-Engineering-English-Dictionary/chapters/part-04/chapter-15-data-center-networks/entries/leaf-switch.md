# leaf switch

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A switch in a leaf-spine design that connects endpoints or services to the fabric.

## Usage and distinctions

Leaf is a topology role; its routing and switching features depend on the fabric design.

## Examples

> The server connects to a leaf switch.

> Check the leaf uplinks to the spine switches.
