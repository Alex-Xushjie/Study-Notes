# east-west traffic

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Traffic exchanged between systems within a data center or similar environment.

## Usage and distinctions

The term is relative to the architecture and does not specify a compass direction.

## Examples

> The application generates east-west traffic between services.

> Check the path between the two server racks.
