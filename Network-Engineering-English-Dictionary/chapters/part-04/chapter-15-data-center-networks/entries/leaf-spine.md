# leaf-spine topology

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A network topology in which leaf switches connect to spine switches to provide fabric connectivity.

## Usage and distinctions

A typical two-tier design connects each leaf to each spine; actual designs and exceptions vary.

## Examples

> The data center uses a leaf-spine topology.

> Confirm that each leaf has the intended spine connections.
