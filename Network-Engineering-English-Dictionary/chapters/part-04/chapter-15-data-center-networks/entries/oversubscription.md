# network oversubscription

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A design in which aggregate potential input capacity exceeds available capacity at another point.

## Usage and distinctions

The meaning depends on which capacities are compared and on the actual workload and traffic patterns.

## Examples

> The rack uplinks have a planned oversubscription ratio.

> Check the expected traffic profile before selecting the ratio.
