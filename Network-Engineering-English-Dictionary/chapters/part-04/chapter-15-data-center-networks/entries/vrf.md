# virtual routing and forwarding

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A mechanism that maintains separate routing tables or routing contexts on a network device.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: VRF. Separate routing contexts do not replace all security controls.

## Examples

> The customer routes are installed in a dedicated VRF.

> Check the route in the correct VRF.
