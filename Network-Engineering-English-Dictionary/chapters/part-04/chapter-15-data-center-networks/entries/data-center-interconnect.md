# data center interconnect

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Connectivity that links separate data centers or data center sites.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: DCI. DCI can use multiple technologies and does not imply Layer 2 extension.

## Examples

> The data center interconnect carries traffic between the two sites.

> Check the inter-site path and failure domains.
