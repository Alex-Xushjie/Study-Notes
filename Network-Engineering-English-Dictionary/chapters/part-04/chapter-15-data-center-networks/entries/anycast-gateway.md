**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A gateway design in which multiple devices present a common gateway identity for a network.

Exact forwarding and address behavior depend on the fabric design and implementation.
