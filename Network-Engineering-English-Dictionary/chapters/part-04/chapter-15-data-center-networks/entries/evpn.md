# Ethernet VPN

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A control-plane technology used to advertise information for Ethernet VPN services.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: EVPN. EVPN can be used with different data-plane encapsulations; EVPN and VXLAN are not synonyms.

## Examples

> The fabric uses EVPN to distribute endpoint information.

> Check the EVPN routes and import policy.
