# route distinguisher

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A value used with a prefix to distinguish otherwise overlapping VPN routes.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: RD. A route distinguisher makes route information distinct; route targets govern import and export policy.

## Examples

> Each tenant routing context has a route distinguisher.

> Check whether overlapping prefixes are uniquely represented.
