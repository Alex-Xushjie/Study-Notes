# Virtual Extensible LAN

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An encapsulation technology that carries Layer 2 frames over an IP underlay using VXLAN network identifiers.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: VXLAN. The VNI identifies a VXLAN segment; exact control-plane behavior depends on the deployment.

## Examples

> The fabric carries the tenant segment over VXLAN.

> Check the VNI and tunnel endpoint reachability.
