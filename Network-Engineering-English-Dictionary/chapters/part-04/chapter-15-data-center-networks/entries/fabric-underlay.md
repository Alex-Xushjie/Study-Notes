# fabric underlay

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The physical and IP network that provides transport for a data center overlay.

## Usage and distinctions

The underlay can use different routing protocols and architectures depending on the design.

## Examples

> The underlay routes between the fabric endpoints.

> Verify underlay reachability before checking VXLAN.
