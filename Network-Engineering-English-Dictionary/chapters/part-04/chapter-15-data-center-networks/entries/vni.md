# VXLAN network identifier

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A 24-bit field used to identify a VXLAN segment.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: VNI. The mapping between VLANs, bridge domains, and VNIs is implementation- and design-specific.

## Examples

> The bridge domain is mapped to a VNI.

> Verify that the VNI mapping is consistent.
