# network tenant

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical owner, customer, or administrative context whose network resources are separated in a shared environment.

## Usage and distinctions

Tenant isolation depends on the design and enforcement; names and implementations vary.

## Examples

> Each tenant has a separate routing context.

> Confirm the tenant before changing the policy.
