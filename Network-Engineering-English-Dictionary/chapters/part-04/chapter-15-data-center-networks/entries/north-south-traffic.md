# north-south traffic

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Traffic entering or leaving a data center or a defined network environment.

## Usage and distinctions

The boundary depends on the system being discussed; the term does not imply a physical compass direction.

## Examples

> The firewall inspects north-south traffic.

> Check the external path for client requests.
