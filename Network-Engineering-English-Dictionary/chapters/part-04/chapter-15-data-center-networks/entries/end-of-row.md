# end-of-row switching

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A design placing switches at the end of a row to serve devices across multiple racks.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: EoR. The design trades local rack switching for longer server-to-switch cabling.

## Examples

> The end-of-row switch connects several racks.

> Check cable paths and port capacity.
