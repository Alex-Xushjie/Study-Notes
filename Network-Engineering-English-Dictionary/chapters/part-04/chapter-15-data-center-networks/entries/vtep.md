# VXLAN tunnel endpoint

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A function or device that encapsulates and decapsulates VXLAN traffic.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: VTEP. An established underlay path alone does not prove correct VNI or endpoint state.

## Examples

> The leaf switch acts as a VXLAN tunnel endpoint.

> Check reachability between the VTEP addresses.
