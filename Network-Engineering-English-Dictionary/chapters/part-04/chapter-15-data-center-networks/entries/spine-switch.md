# spine switch

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A switch in a leaf-spine design that provides connectivity between leaf switches.

## Usage and distinctions

Spine is a topology role. Particular designs may include additional tiers or variations.

## Examples

> Each leaf connects to the spine switches.

> Check the routes through both spines.
