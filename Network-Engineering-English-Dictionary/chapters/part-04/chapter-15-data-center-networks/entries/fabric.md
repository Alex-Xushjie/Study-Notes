# network fabric

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An integrated collection of network devices and connections providing connectivity as a system.

## Usage and distinctions

Fabric is a broad architectural term; its meaning and implementation depend on the platform and design.

## Examples

> The fabric connects the server racks.

> Check the fabric control plane and underlay.
