# BGP route target

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 15

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An extended community used to control which VPN routes are imported into a routing or service context.

## Usage and distinctions

A route target controls route distribution policy; it is different from a route distinguisher.

## Examples

> The route target controls import into the tenant context.

> Verify matching import and export route targets.
