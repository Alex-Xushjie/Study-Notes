# radio frequency

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A frequency in the electromagnetic spectrum used for wireless transmission.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: RF. Wireless behavior depends on frequency, environment, power, and modulation.

## Examples

> The survey identified sources of radio-frequency interference.

> Check the radio-frequency environment at the affected location.
