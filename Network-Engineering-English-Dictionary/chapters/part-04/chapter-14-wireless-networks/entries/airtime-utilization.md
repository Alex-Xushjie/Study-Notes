# airtime utilization

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The proportion of observed radio time occupied by activity over a measurement interval.

## Usage and distinctions

Measurement definitions and reporting vary by platform; utilization alone does not identify the source.

## Examples

> High airtime utilization affects the client experience.

> Check airtime utilization and the source of activity.
