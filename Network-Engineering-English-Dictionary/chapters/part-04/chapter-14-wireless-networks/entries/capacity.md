# wireless capacity

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The ability of a wireless network to support traffic and clients under defined conditions.

## Usage and distinctions

Good signal coverage alone does not guarantee sufficient capacity.

## Examples

> The venue needs more wireless capacity during events.

> Evaluate airtime utilization and client load.
