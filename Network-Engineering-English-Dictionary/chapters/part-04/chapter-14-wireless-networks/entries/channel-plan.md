# wireless channel plan

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A planned allocation of radio channels to access points.

## Usage and distinctions

Channel selection must account for spectrum availability, interference, regulatory constraints, and the actual environment.

## Examples

> The channel plan reduces unnecessary overlap.

> Review the channel plan after adding access points.
