# wireless frequency band

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A range of radio frequencies used by wireless equipment.

## Usage and distinctions

Common Wi-Fi bands include 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz where supported and permitted.

## Examples

> The client supports the 5 GHz band.

> Check the available channels and local regulatory requirements.
