# wireless coverage

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The area in which a wireless network can provide usable signal under specified conditions.

## Usage and distinctions

Coverage depends on client capability, data-rate and service requirements, walls, interference, and environment.

## Examples

> The survey found a coverage gap near the stairwell.

> Measure coverage at the locations where clients will operate.
