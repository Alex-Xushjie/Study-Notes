# wireless site survey

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An assessment of radio conditions, coverage, and other factors relevant to wireless network design.

## Usage and distinctions

A predictive design, passive survey, and active survey provide different kinds of evidence.

## Examples

> The wireless site survey identified a coverage gap.

> Perform measurements at the intended client locations.
