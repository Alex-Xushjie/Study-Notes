# wireless channel width

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The bandwidth occupied by a wireless channel configuration.

## Usage and distinctions

Wider channels can increase capacity under suitable conditions but use more spectrum and may face more contention.

## Examples

> The radio uses an 80 MHz channel width.

> Review channel width when troubleshooting interference.
