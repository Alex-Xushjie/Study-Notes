# signal-to-noise ratio

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The difference or ratio between the desired signal level and the background noise level.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: SNR. In decibel terms it is often computed from signal level minus noise level when measurements are comparable.

## Examples

> The client reports a low signal-to-noise ratio.

> Check both the signal and noise readings.
