# wireless LAN controller

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A system that centrally manages or coordinates wireless access points and related services.

## Usage and distinctions

Controller architectures and traffic forwarding modes vary by vendor and deployment.
