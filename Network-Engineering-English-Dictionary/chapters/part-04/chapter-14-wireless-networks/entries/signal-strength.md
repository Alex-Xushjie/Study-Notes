# wireless signal strength

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A measure of received wireless signal power at a receiver.

## Usage and distinctions

A commonly reported value is RSSI, but vendor scales and interpretation can differ.

## Examples

> The client reports a weak signal.

> Measure signal strength at the affected location.
