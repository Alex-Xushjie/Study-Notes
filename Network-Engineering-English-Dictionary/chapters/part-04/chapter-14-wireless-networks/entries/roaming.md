# wireless roaming

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The process by which a wireless client moves its association between access points or basic service sets.

## Usage and distinctions

Roaming decisions involve client behavior and network support; a strong signal alone does not guarantee seamless roaming.

## Examples

> The client roams to another access point.

> Record the time and BSSIDs during the roaming issue.
