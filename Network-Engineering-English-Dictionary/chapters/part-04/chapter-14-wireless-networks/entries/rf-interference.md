# RF interference

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Unwanted radio-frequency energy that degrades wireless reception or communication.

## Usage and distinctions

Interference is a possible cause of poor service; client, coverage, and network issues should also be checked.

## Examples

> RF interference affects the meeting-room network.

> Identify the interference source before changing the channel plan.
