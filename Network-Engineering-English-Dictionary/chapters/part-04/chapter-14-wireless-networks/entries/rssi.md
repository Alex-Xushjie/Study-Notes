# received signal strength indicator

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A reported indication of received radio signal strength.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: RSSI. Some systems report dBm while others use a vendor-specific scale.

## Examples

> The client reports an RSSI of minus 65 dBm.

> Compare the reading with the device’s documented interpretation.
