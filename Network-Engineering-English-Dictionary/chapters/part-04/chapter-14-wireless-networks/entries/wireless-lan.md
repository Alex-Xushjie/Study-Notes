# wireless LAN

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 14

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A local area network that connects devices over wireless radio links.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: WLAN. The actual coverage and capacity depend on the radio environment and design.

## Examples

> The wireless LAN provides access throughout the office.

> Check whether the client can join the wireless LAN.
