# switchover

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A transition of a role or service from one component to another.

## Usage and distinctions

Switchover can be planned or triggered by failure; the term does not imply zero interruption.

## Examples

> The planned switchover completed successfully.

> Confirm the active role after the switchover.
