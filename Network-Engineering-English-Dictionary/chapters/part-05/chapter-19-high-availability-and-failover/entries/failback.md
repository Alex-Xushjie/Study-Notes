# failback

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The return of a service or function to a preferred component or path after recovery.

## Usage and distinctions

Automatic failback may cause another transition; behavior is configuration-specific.

## Examples

> The service fails back to the primary device.

> Confirm whether failback is automatic or manual.
