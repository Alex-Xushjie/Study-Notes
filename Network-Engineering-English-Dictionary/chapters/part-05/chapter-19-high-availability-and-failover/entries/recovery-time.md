# recovery time

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The elapsed time needed to restore a specified service or function after disruption.

## Usage and distinctions

Recovery time depends on the service and measurement method; distinguish observed results from a target.

## Examples

> The measured recovery time was 20 seconds.

> Define the start and end events used for the measurement.
