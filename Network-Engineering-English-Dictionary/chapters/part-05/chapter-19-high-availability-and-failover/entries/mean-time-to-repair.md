# mean time to repair

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A measure of the average time taken to repair or restore a defined component or service.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: MTTR. Definitions vary; some organizations use mean time to restore or recovery instead.

## Examples

> The team tracks mean time to repair for incidents.

> Confirm which events start and stop the measurement.
