**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A redundancy arrangement in which one component serves traffic while another is ready to take over.

State synchronization and failover behavior depend on the product and configuration.
