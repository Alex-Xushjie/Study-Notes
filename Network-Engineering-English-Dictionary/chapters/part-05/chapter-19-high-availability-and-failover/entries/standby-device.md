# standby device

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A device prepared to assume a role when the active device or service fails.

## Usage and distinctions

Standby readiness depends on connectivity, configuration, state, and product-specific checks.

## Examples

> The standby device is synchronized and ready.

> Check the standby health before the maintenance.
