# primary device

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A device designated as preferred or primary in a particular redundancy configuration.

## Usage and distinctions

Primary and active are not always synonyms; terminology varies by product.

## Examples

> The primary device is currently active.

> Confirm whether primary is a priority or the current forwarding role.
