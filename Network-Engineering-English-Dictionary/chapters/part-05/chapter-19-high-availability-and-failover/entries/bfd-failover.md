# BFD for failover

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The use of Bidirectional Forwarding Detection to notify a client protocol of path failure.

## Usage and distinctions

BFD detects a forwarding-path failure; the client protocol and platform determine the resulting action.

## Examples

> The routing protocol uses BFD to detect the failed path.

> Compare the negotiated BFD timers with the design.
