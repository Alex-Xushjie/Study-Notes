**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A design that provides an alternate default-gateway function to hosts.

## Usage and distinctions

Protocols such as VRRP and HSRP have distinct behavior and configuration.
