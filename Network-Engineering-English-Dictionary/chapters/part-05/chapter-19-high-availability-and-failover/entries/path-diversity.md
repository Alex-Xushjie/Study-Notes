# path diversity

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The use of distinct physical or logical paths to reduce shared failure exposure.

## Usage and distinctions

Two logical circuits are not necessarily physically diverse.

## Examples

> The two circuits enter the building through diverse paths.

> Confirm the actual carrier and building pathways.
