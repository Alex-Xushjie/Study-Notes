# high availability

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A design goal of maintaining service availability through failures or planned maintenance.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: HA. Availability depends on the service definition, failure scenarios, and operational design.

## Examples

> The design provides high availability for the gateway.

> Test high availability during an approved maintenance window.
