# service availability

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The proportion of a defined period during which a service meets its agreed availability criteria.

## Usage and distinctions

A percentage is meaningful only with an agreed service definition, measurement method, and exclusions.

## Examples

> The service availability is measured monthly.

> Review the measurement period and exclusions.
