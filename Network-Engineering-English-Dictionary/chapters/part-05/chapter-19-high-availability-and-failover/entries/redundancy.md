# redundancy

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The inclusion of additional components or paths to reduce dependence on a single component.

## Usage and distinctions

Redundancy does not automatically eliminate all single points of failure.

## Examples

> The design includes redundant uplinks.

> Check whether the redundant paths share common dependencies.
