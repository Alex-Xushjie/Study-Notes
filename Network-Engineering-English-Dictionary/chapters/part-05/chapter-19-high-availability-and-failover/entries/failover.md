# failover

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The transition of a service or function to an alternate component or path after a failure or trigger.

## Usage and distinctions

Failover can be automatic or manual and may cause a brief interruption.

## Examples

> The gateway fails over to the standby device.

> Measure the interruption during failover.
