# recovery point objective

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A target for the maximum acceptable loss of data measured in time.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: RPO. RPO concerns acceptable data loss, while RTO concerns restoration time.

## Examples

> The database has a recovery point objective of 15 minutes.

> Check replication behavior against the RPO.
