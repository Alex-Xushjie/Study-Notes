# cluster quorum

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The minimum participation or voting condition required for a cluster to make certain decisions.

## Usage and distinctions

Quorum rules and witness behavior differ among clustering systems.

## Examples

> The cluster loses quorum after multiple members fail.

> Check the voting members and quorum status.
