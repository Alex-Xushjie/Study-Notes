# recovery time objective

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A target for the maximum acceptable time to restore a specified service after disruption.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: RTO. A target is not proof that the system achieved it.

## Examples

> The service has a recovery time objective of one hour.

> Confirm whether the proposed design meets the RTO.
