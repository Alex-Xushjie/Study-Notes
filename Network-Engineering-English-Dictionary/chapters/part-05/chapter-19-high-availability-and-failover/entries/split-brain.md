# split-brain

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 19

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A condition in which redundant components operate with inconsistent or conflicting views of cluster state.

## Usage and distinctions

The symptoms and resolution depend on the clustering technology; do not assume all failovers are split-brain events.

## Examples

> The cluster entered a split-brain condition after the peer link failed.

> Check the quorum and peer connectivity.
