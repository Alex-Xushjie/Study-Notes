# 802.1X supplicant

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The entity that requests network access and participates in 802.1X authentication.

## Usage and distinctions

The supplicant is usually software on the client; behavior depends on the endpoint implementation.

## Examples

> The client supplicant presents its credentials.

> Check the supplicant logs on the endpoint.
