# IEEE 802.1X

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A port-based network access-control framework commonly used to authenticate devices or users.

## Usage and distinctions

Exact authentication methods and resulting authorization depend on the deployed system and configuration.
