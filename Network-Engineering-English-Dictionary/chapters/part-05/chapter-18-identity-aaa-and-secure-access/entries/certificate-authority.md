# certificate authority

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An entity that issues and signs digital certificates.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: CA. Trust and issuance policies determine whether a certificate is accepted for a given purpose.

## Examples

> The device certificate was issued by the trusted certificate authority.

> Verify the certificate authority in the trust chain.
