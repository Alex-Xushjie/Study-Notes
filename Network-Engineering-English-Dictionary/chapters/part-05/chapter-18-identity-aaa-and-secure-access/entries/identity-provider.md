# identity provider

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A service that authenticates identities and provides identity information to relying systems.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: IdP. The protocols and trust relationships depend on the identity architecture.

## Examples

> The identity provider authenticates the administrator.

> Check the identity provider status and logs.
