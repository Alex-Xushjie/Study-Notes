# multi-factor authentication

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Authentication using two or more different categories of factor.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: MFA. Two secrets of the same factor category do not necessarily constitute two different factors.

## Examples

> The administrator uses multi-factor authentication.

> Confirm the MFA enrollment process.
