# read-only access

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Permission to view specified information without making permitted modifications.

## Usage and distinctions

The exact set of visible data and allowed operations depends on the product and role definition.

## Examples

> The auditor has read-only access to the device.

> Confirm that the role cannot change the configuration.
