# out-of-band management

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A management path that is separate from the primary production data path.

## Usage and distinctions

A management port alone does not guarantee a truly independent management path.

## Examples

> The team uses out-of-band management during the outage.

> Verify that the out-of-band path has independent connectivity.
