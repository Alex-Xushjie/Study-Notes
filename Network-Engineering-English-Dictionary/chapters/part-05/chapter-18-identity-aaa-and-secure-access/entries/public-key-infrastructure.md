# public key infrastructure

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The systems and processes used to issue, manage, validate, and revoke digital certificates.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: PKI. Effective trust depends on both technical controls and operational practices.

## Examples

> The organization uses a public key infrastructure for device certificates.

> Check the certificate authority and revocation status.
