# privileged access

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Access that permits elevated administrative or other sensitive actions.

## Usage and distinctions

Privileges should be scoped and audited according to organizational policy.

## Examples

> The change requires privileged access.

> Use the approved privileged account for the maintenance.
