# AAA accounting

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The recording of activities or resource usage associated with access.

## Usage and distinctions

Accounting records depend on protocol, product, configuration, and logging availability.

## Examples

> The AAA server records the administrative session.

> Check the accounting logs for the change.
