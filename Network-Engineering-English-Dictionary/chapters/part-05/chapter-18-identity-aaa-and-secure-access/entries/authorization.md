# authorization

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The process of determining what an authenticated identity is permitted to do.

## Usage and distinctions

Authorization decisions are separate from authentication and depend on policy and context.

## Examples

> The policy authorizes the engineer to view the configuration.

> Check the user’s assigned authorization role.
