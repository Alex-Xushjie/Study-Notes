# digital certificate

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A signed data structure binding identity information to a public key under a certificate system.

## Usage and distinctions

Certificate-based access also depends on identity mapping, key protection, and policy.

## Examples

> The device presents a certificate during authentication.

> Check the certificate validity and trust chain.
