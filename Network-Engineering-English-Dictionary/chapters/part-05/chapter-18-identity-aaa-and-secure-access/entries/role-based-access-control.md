# role-based access control

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An access-control approach assigning permissions through defined roles.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: RBAC. Roles and privilege granularity depend on the system.

## Examples

> The network engineer receives a read-only role.

> Review the permissions associated with the role.
