# credentials

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 18

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Information presented to support an identity claim during authentication.

## Usage and distinctions

Credentials can include passwords, certificates, tokens, or other factors. Avoid including secrets in tickets or logs.

## Examples

> The administrator provides credentials to sign in.

> Verify the authentication method without exposing the credential.
