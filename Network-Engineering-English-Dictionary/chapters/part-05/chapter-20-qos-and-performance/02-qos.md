## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [traffic classification](entries/classification.md) | Identifying traffic for subsequent treatment. |
| [traffic marking](entries/marking.md) | Setting a field used to identify traffic. |
| [traffic policing](entries/policing.md) | Enforcing a traffic rate or profile with configured actions. |
| [traffic shaping](entries/shaping.md) | Controlling transmission over time, commonly using buffering. |

## Examples and usage

> The policy classifies and marks the voice traffic.

> Check whether the provider preserves the DSCP marking.

> The router shapes traffic to the provider’s service rate.

> Check the policer counters for exceeded traffic.
