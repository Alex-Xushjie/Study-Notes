# queue drop

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The discard of a packet associated with queueing or buffer management.

## Usage and distinctions

Drops can arise from different queues or mechanisms; check platform counter definitions.

## Examples

> The output queue reports drops during peak traffic.

> Correlate queue drops with traffic rate and timestamps.
