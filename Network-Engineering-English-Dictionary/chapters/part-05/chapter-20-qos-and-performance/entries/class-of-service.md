# class of service

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A traffic classification or treatment category used in a network QoS design.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: CoS. The term and available marking fields can vary by technology and vendor.

## Examples

> The switch maps the class of service to an output queue.

> Check the mapping at the trunk boundary.
