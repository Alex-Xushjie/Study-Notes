# quality of service

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The management of network traffic treatment to meet defined service objectives.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: QoS. QoS allocates or controls resources; it cannot create capacity beyond the available link.

## Examples

> The QoS policy prioritizes time-sensitive traffic.

> Check classification, marking, and queue behavior.
