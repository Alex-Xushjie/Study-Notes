# network bandwidth

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The capacity of a network link or channel, commonly expressed as a data rate.

## Usage and distinctions

Bandwidth is capacity; throughput is the amount of data delivered over time.

## Examples

> The WAN circuit has a bandwidth of 100 megabits per second.

> Compare the offered traffic with the available bandwidth.
