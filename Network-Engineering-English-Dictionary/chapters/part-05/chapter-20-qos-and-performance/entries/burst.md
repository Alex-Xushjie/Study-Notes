# traffic burst

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A short interval during which traffic arrives or is sent at a higher rate than its longer-term average.

## Usage and distinctions

Burst duration and size matter when analyzing buffers, policers, and application behavior.

## Examples

> A traffic burst fills the output queue.

> Check short-interval counters rather than only the hourly average.
