# traffic shaping

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The control of traffic transmission over time, commonly by buffering traffic to conform to a configured rate.

## Usage and distinctions

Shaping uses buffering and can add delay; the exact behavior depends on the scheduler.

## Examples

> The router shapes traffic to the provider’s service rate.

> Check the shaping rate and queue drops.
