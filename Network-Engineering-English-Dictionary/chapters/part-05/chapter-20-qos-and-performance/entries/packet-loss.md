# packet loss

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The failure of packets to reach a specified observation point or destination.

## Usage and distinctions

A single observation does not identify where or why a packet was lost.

## Examples

> The test shows packet loss between the sites.

> Compare loss in both directions.
