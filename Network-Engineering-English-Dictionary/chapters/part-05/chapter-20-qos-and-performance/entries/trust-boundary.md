# QoS trust boundary

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A point at which a network decides whether to accept or replace traffic markings.

## Usage and distinctions

Trust behavior and supported marking fields depend on the platform and policy.

## Examples

> The access port is configured as a QoS trust boundary.

> Verify whether markings from the endpoint are trusted.
