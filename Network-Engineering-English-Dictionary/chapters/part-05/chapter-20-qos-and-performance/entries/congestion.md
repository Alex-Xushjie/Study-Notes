# network congestion

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A condition in which demand for network resources exceeds available capacity over some interval.

## Usage and distinctions

Congestion can be transient; average utilization alone may hide short bursts.

## Examples

> The uplink experiences congestion during the backup window.

> Check queue drops and interface utilization.
