# round-trip time

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The elapsed time for a signal or message to travel to a destination and return.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: RTT. RTT is not necessarily twice a stable one-way latency because paths and delays can differ.

## Examples

> The round-trip time to the gateway is 2 milliseconds.

> Record the RTT over a representative interval.
