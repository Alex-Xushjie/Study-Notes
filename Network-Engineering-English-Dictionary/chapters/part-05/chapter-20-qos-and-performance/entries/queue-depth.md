# queue depth

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The amount of traffic or number of packets held in a queue at a point in time.

## Usage and distinctions

Units and sampling intervals vary by platform; a single sample may not reveal short-lived peaks.

## Examples

> The queue depth rises during the burst.

> Compare queue depth with drop counters.
