# traffic marking

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The setting or modification of a field used to identify traffic for subsequent treatment.

## Usage and distinctions

A marking only affects behavior when downstream devices are configured to interpret it.

## Examples

> The ingress policy marks the voice packets.

> Verify the marking before and after the network boundary.
