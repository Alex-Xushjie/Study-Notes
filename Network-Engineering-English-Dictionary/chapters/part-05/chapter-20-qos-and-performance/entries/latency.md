# network latency

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The elapsed time for data or a signal to travel between defined measurement points.

## Usage and distinctions

Latency depends on the path, processing, queues, and the measurement method.

## Examples

> The measured latency increased during the incident.

> Specify whether the measurement is one-way or round-trip.
