# traffic policing

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The enforcement of a traffic rate or profile by applying actions to traffic that exceeds it.

## Usage and distinctions

Depending on configuration, out-of-profile traffic may be dropped or remarked.

## Examples

> The provider polices traffic at the contracted rate.

> Check the policer counters for exceeded traffic.
