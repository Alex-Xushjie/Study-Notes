# service-level objective

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A measurable target for a defined service characteristic.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: SLO. A service objective is distinct from a contractual service-level agreement.

## Examples

> The service-level objective specifies a latency target.

> Confirm the percentile and measurement window.
