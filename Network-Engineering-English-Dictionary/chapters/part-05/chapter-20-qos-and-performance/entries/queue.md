# network queue

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 20

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A buffer holding packets while they wait for transmission or processing.

## Usage and distinctions

Queues are finite; their size, assignment, and behavior depend on the platform and configuration.

## Examples

> The interface queue fills during the traffic burst.

> Check the queue counters and scheduling policy.
