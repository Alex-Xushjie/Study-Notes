## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [network bandwidth](entries/bandwidth.md) | Link or channel capacity, commonly expressed as a data rate. |
| [network throughput](entries/throughput.md) | The rate of data successfully transferred over an interval. |
| [network latency](entries/latency.md) | Elapsed time between defined measurement points. |
| [packet loss](entries/packet-loss.md) | Failure of packets to reach an observation point or destination. |

## Examples and usage

> Compare throughput with the available bandwidth.

> Specify whether latency is one-way or round-trip.

> Record packet loss in both directions.

> Average utilization can hide short traffic bursts.
