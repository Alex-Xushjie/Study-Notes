# source address

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The address identifying the origin of a packet or connection in the relevant protocol.

## Usage and distinctions

The observed address may differ at different points when address translation or proxying is used.

## Examples

> The firewall rule matches the source address.

> Verify the source address before and after translation.
