# firewall log

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A record of an event or traffic decision produced by a firewall.

## Usage and distinctions

Logging configuration, time zone, sampling, and translation can affect what appears in the record.

## Examples

> The firewall log shows that the connection was denied.

> Correlate the log with the client timestamp and addresses.
