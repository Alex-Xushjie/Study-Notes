# firewall rule

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An individual policy entry matching traffic and specifying an action.

## Usage and distinctions

A rule can include source, destination, service, application, schedule, and other conditions depending on the product.

## Examples

> The firewall rule allows TCP port 443 from the approved subnet.

> Check the rule order and match conditions.
