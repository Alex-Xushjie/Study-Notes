# firewall

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A system that applies rules to control network traffic between interfaces, networks, or security zones.

## Usage and distinctions

A firewall may be hardware, virtual, host-based, or a managed service; its features depend on the product.

## Examples

> The firewall permits the application traffic.

> Check the policy and session state on the firewall.
