# security segmentation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The division of systems or networks into zones with controlled communication between them.

## Usage and distinctions

A segment is not automatically secure; enforcement and allowed traffic must be evaluated.

## Examples

> The design segments guest and management traffic.

> Verify the policy between the security zones.
