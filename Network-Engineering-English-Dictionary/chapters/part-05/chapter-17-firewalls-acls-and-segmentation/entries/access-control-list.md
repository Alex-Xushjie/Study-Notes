# access control list

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An ordered or structured collection of rules used to control access or traffic.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: ACL. Syntax, ordering, and implicit actions vary by platform and context.

## Examples

> The access control list permits the monitoring subnet.

> Check the ACL applied to the interface.
