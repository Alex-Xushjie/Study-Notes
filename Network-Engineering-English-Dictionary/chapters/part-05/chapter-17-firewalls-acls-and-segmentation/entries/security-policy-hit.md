# security policy hit

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A match between traffic and a security policy rule.

## Usage and distinctions

A rule hit indicates a match; the complete session and application result still require separate verification.

## Examples

> The rule counter shows hits during the test.

> Reset or record the counter only according to the approved procedure.
