# least privilege

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The principle of granting only the access needed for an authorized purpose.

## Usage and distinctions

Least privilege applies to identities and network access; it requires context and periodic review.

## Examples

> The rule follows least privilege by allowing only the required service.

> Review whether the broad source range is necessary.
