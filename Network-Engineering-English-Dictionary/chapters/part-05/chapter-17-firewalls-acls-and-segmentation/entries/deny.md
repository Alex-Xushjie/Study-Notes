# deny traffic

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

To block or reject matching network traffic according to an applicable policy.

## Usage and distinctions

Device actions and logs vary; a deny may discard traffic or return an error depending on configuration.

## Examples

> The policy denies traffic from the guest network to the management subnet.

> Check the deny log for the affected connection.
