# security zone

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A logical grouping of interfaces or resources used as a context for security policy.

## Usage and distinctions

Zone membership and policy evaluation depend on the firewall platform and design.
