# destination address

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The address identifying the intended destination of a packet or connection.

## Usage and distinctions

An IP destination address and a destination MAC address belong to different protocol layers.

## Examples

> The rule matches the destination address of the application.
