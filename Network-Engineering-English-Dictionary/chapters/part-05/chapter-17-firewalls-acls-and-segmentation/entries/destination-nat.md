# destination network address translation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The translation of a packet’s destination address and possibly transport information.

## Usage and distinctions

Abbreviation: DNAT. Rule ordering and interaction with policy vary by platform.

## Examples

> The firewall maps the public address to an internal server.

> Check the destination NAT rule and security policy.
