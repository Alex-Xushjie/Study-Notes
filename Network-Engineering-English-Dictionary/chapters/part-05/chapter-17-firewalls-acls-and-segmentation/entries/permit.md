# permit traffic

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 17

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

To allow matching network traffic according to an applicable policy.

## Usage and distinctions

Permit is a policy action; it does not guarantee routing, service availability, or successful authentication.

## Examples

> The rule permits the client subnet to reach the service.

> Confirm the permitted source, destination, protocol, and port.
