# When can we expect the next update?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request for the next communication time.

## Usage and distinctions

Ask for a date and time zone when parties are in different regions.

## Examples

> When can we expect the next update on the circuit test?

> Please confirm the next update time in UTC.
