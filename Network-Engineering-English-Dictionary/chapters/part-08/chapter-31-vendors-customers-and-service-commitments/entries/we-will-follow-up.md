# We will follow up with …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement identifying a planned subsequent contact or action.

## Usage and distinctions

State an owner and timing when making a commitment.

## Examples

> We will follow up with the provider after the test.

> We will follow up with the vendor using case 12345.
