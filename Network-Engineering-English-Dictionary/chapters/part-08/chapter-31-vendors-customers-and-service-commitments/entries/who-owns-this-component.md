# Who owns this component?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question identifying ownership or responsibility.

## Usage and distinctions

Distinguish ownership, management, support, and approval authority.

## Examples

> Who owns the customer-premises equipment?

> Who is responsible for maintaining the firewall policy?
