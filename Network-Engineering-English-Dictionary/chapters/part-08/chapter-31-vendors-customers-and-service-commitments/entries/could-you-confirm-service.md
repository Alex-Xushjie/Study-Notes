# Could you confirm the service details?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to verify the scope or characteristics of a service.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify the service identifier, endpoint, or feature that needs confirmation.

## Examples

> Could you confirm the circuit bandwidth and handoff type?

> Could you confirm the support hours for this service?
