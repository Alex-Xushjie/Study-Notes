# From our side, …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression introducing the local team’s observations or responsibilities.

## Usage and distinctions

Identify the exact observation point and do not imply visibility beyond it.

## Examples

> From our side, the interface is up and counters are stable.

> From our side, the configuration has not changed since Monday.
