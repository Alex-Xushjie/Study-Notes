# Where is the demarcation point?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question identifying the provider-customer responsibility boundary.

## Usage and distinctions

Physical handoff and contractual support responsibilities may need separate confirmation.

## Examples

> Where is the demarcation point for this circuit?

> Please confirm the demarcation point and the test responsibility.
