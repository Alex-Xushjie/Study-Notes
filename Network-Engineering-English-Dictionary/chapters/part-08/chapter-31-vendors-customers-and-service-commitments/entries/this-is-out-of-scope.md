# This is outside the agreed scope.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that a requested item is not included in the current agreement or plan.

## Usage and distinctions

Describe the boundary factually and identify the process for requesting a change.

## Examples

> Application remediation is outside the agreed scope.

> A second site survey is outside the current scope.
