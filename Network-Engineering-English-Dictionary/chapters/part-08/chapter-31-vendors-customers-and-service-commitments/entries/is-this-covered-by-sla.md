# Is this covered by the SLA?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question about whether a situation falls within a service agreement.

## Usage and distinctions

Contractual interpretation must be checked against the applicable agreement.

## Examples

> Is this outage covered by the service-level agreement?

> Which exclusions apply to this maintenance period?
