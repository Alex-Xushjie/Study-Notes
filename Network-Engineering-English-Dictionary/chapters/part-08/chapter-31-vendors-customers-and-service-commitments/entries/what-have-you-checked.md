# What have you checked so far?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question requesting a summary of completed investigation.

## Usage and distinctions

Use a neutral tone and ask for observations rather than implying fault.

## Examples

> What have you checked on the provider edge so far?

> What has the vendor checked on the affected device?
