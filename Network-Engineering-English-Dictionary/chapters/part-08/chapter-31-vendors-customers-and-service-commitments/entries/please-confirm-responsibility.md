# Please confirm the responsibility boundary.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to establish which party owns a task or issue.

## Usage and distinctions

Record the agreed boundary and escalation path.

## Examples

> Please confirm the responsibility boundary for the handoff.

> Please confirm who will monitor the circuit after installation.
