# We need your approval before …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request identifying a required decision before an action.

## Usage and distinctions

Explain the exact action and its consequences, and follow the relevant approval process.

## Examples

> We need your approval before extending the maintenance window.

> We need your approval before changing the agreed scope.
