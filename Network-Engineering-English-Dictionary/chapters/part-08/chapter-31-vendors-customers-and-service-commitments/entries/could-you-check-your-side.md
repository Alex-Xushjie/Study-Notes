# Could you check your side?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request for the other party to inspect its part of a service.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify the component, expected behavior, and time window.

## Examples

> Could you check the provider-edge interface on your side?

> Could you check the return route on your side?
