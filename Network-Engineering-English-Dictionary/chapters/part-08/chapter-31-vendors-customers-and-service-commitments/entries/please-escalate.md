# Please escalate this issue.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to refer a problem to a person or team with appropriate authority or expertise.

## Usage and distinctions

Include impact, evidence, the requested recipient, and urgency.

## Examples

> Please escalate the circuit outage to the provider’s network team.

> Please escalate the support case if the agreed response time has passed.
