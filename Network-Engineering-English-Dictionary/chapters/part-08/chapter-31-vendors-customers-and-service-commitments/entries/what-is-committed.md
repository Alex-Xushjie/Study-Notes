# What has been committed?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question clarifying an existing agreement or promise.

## Usage and distinctions

Distinguish contractual commitments from informal estimates or goals.

## Examples

> What restoration time has the provider committed to?

> What delivery date was agreed in the contract?
