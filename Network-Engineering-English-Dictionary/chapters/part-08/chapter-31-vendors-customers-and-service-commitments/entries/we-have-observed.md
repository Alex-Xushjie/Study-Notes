# We have observed …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement introducing a specific observation.

## Usage and distinctions

Include where, when, and how the observation was made.

## Examples

> We have observed input errors on the customer-facing interface.

> We have observed packet loss during the test window.
