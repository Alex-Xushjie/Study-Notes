# What is the circuit ID?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question requesting the provider’s service identifier.

## Usage and distinctions

A provider may use multiple references; confirm which one identifies the affected service.

## Examples

> What is the circuit ID for the Shanghai branch?

> Please include the circuit ID in the escalation.
