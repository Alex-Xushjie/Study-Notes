# Please confirm acceptance of …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request for an authorized recipient to confirm a deliverable against agreed criteria.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify the deliverable and criteria; do not assume acceptance from silence.

## Examples

> Please confirm acceptance of the completed installation.

> Please confirm acceptance after reviewing the test results.
