# Can you confirm the estimated time?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request for a current estimated time of completion or restoration.

## Usage and distinctions

Label estimates as estimates and ask what assumptions or dependencies apply.

## Examples

> Can you confirm the estimated restoration time?

> Can you confirm the estimated delivery date for the circuit?
