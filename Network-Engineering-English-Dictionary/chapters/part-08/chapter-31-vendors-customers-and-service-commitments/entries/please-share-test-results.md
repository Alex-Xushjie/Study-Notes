# Please share the test results.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request for documented findings from a test.

## Usage and distinctions

Request the measurement location, units, time, and method when relevant.

## Examples

> Please share the circuit test results and timestamps.

> Please share the optical measurements from both ends.
