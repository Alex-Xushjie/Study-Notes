# We cannot yet confirm …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that a fact or outcome is still uncertain.

## Usage and distinctions

Explain what evidence or action is needed to resolve the uncertainty.

## Examples

> We cannot yet confirm the restoration time.

> We cannot yet confirm whether the carrier path is diverse.
