# This is subject to verification.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that a conclusion or condition has not yet been validated.

## Usage and distinctions

State what evidence or test will verify the claim.

## Examples

> The second path appears diverse, subject to verification.

> The estimated capacity is subject to verification during testing.
