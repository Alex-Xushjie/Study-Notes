# The expected impact is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 31

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement describing the predicted service effect of work.

## Usage and distinctions

Identify uncertainty, affected users, and the agreed maintenance scope.

## Examples

> The expected impact is a brief interruption during failover.

> The expected impact is limited to one branch.
