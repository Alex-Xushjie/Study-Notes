## Say it at work

> Please confirm the circuit ID, endpoints and demarcation point.

> From our side, the interface is up; could you check the provider edge?

> The restoration time is an estimate and remains subject to verification.

> Please confirm the test results and acceptance criteria.

## Quick review

1. Is an estimated restoration time necessarily a contractual commitment?
2. Does equipment location alone establish support ownership?
3. What should accompany a request for testing?
4. Does silence automatically establish acceptance?

**Answers**

1. No; verify the agreement and the assumptions.
2. No; confirm the responsibility boundary.
3. The scope, timing, authorization, and desired evidence.
4. No; use the agreed acceptance process.
