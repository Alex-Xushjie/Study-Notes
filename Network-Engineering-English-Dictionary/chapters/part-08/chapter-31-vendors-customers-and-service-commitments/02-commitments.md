## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [Can you confirm the estimated time?](entries/can-you-confirm-eta.md) | Request an estimate and its assumptions. |
| [Is this covered by the SLA?](entries/is-this-covered-by-sla.md) | Ask about applicable service agreement terms. |
| [This is outside the agreed scope.](entries/this-is-out-of-scope.md) | Identify a factual scope boundary. |
| [Please confirm acceptance of …](entries/please-confirm-acceptance.md) | Request a decision against agreed criteria. |

## Examples and usage

> Can you confirm the estimated restoration time?

> Which exclusions apply to this maintenance period?

> Application remediation is outside the agreed scope.

> Please confirm acceptance after reviewing the test results.
