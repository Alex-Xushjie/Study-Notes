## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [The issue affects …](entries/the-issue-affects.md) | Identify affected users, services, or locations. |
| [The impact is …](entries/the-impact-is.md) | Describe the observed service effect. |
| [We are investigating …](entries/we-are-investigating.md) | State the current investigation. |
| [We will provide an update by …](entries/we-will-update.md) | Give a specific next update time. |

## Examples and usage

> The issue affects access from one branch.

> The impact is intermittent application access.

> We are investigating the return path.

> We will provide an update by 14:00 UTC.
