# Thank you for your patience.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A courteous expression acknowledging that a recipient has waited.

## Usage and distinctions

Use after a meaningful status update; avoid replacing concrete information with courtesy alone.

## Examples

> Thank you for your patience while we investigate.

> Thank you for your patience during the maintenance.
