# Validation is in progress.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that post-action checks are not yet complete.

## Usage and distinctions

Do not report the change or incident as fully resolved until agreed checks are complete.

## Examples

> The configuration is applied and validation is in progress.

> Application validation is in progress with the customer.
