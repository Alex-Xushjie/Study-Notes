# Please provide …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A direct request for information or an artifact.

## Usage and distinctions

Request only the relevant information and avoid asking for secrets.

## Examples

> Please provide the circuit ID and affected site.

> Please provide the interface counters with timestamps.
