# The service is unavailable.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A clear statement that a defined service cannot be used.

## Usage and distinctions

Identify the source, service, and period; avoid extrapolating beyond evidence.

## Examples

> The service is unavailable from the branch.

> The service was unavailable between 09:10 and 09:25 UTC.
