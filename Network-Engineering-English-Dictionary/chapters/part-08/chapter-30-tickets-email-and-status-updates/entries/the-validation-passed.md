# The validation passed.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that defined checks met their expected results.

## Usage and distinctions

Identify the tests, scope, and time; a pass applies only to what was tested.

## Examples

> The agreed connectivity validation passed.

> The application test passed from both branches.
