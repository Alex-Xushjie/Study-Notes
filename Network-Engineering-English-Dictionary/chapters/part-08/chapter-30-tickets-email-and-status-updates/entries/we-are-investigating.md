# We are investigating …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A status statement that an issue is under investigation.

## Usage and distinctions

State the observed issue, scope, and next update time when known.

## Examples

> We are investigating intermittent packet loss at the branch.

> We are investigating why the route is missing.
