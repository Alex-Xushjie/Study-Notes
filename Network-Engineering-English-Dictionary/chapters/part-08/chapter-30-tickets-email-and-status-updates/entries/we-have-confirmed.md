# We have confirmed that …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement introducing an established fact.

## Usage and distinctions

Use only for facts supported by observation or reliable evidence.

## Examples

> We have confirmed that the physical link is up.

> We have confirmed that the route is present in the correct VRF.
