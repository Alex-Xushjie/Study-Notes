# We will share the results by …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A commitment to provide findings by a specified time.

## Usage and distinctions

Confirm the owner, deadline, time zone, and intended recipient.

## Examples

> We will share the packet capture results by 15:00 UTC.

> We will share the provider test results by Friday.
