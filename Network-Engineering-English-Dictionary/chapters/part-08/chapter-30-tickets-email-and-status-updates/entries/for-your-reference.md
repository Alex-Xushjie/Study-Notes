# For your reference, …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression introducing supporting information.

## Usage and distinctions

Ensure the information is relevant and safe to share.

## Examples

> For your reference, the circuit ID is ABC-123.

> For your reference, the test was performed at 09:00 UTC.
