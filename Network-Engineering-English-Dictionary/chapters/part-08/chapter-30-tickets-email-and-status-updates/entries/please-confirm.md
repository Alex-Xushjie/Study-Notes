# Please confirm …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to verify a specific fact or decision.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify the fact and avoid bundling unrelated questions.

## Examples

> Please confirm whether the maintenance window is approved.

> Please confirm the source and destination addresses.
