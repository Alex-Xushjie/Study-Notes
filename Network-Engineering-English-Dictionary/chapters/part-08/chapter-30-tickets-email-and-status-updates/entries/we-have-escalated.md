# We have escalated …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that an issue has been referred for further attention.

## Usage and distinctions

Include the recipient, reference number, and reason when appropriate.

## Examples

> We have escalated the circuit issue to the provider.

> We have escalated the defect to the vendor support team.
