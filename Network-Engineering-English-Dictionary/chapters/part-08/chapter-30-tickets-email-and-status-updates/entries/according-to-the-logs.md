# According to the logs, …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement attributing an observation to log evidence.

## Usage and distinctions

Identify the log source and time; logs can be incomplete or delayed.

## Examples

> According to the logs, the interface changed state at 09:15 UTC.

> According to the logs, the firewall denied the connection.
