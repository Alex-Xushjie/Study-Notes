# We suspect that …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement introducing an unconfirmed explanation.

## Usage and distinctions

Follow with the test needed to confirm or reject the hypothesis.

## Examples

> We suspect that the return path is missing.

> We suspect that the provider circuit is unstable.
