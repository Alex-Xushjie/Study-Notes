# The alert has cleared.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that the monitoring condition is no longer active.

## Usage and distinctions

An alert clearing does not by itself confirm end-to-end service restoration.

## Examples

> The interface alert has cleared.

> The alert cleared after the link recovered.
