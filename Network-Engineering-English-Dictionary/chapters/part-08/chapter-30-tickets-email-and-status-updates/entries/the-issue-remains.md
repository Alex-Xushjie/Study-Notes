# The issue remains under investigation.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that the cause or resolution is not yet established.

## Usage and distinctions

Pair this with known impact, current action, and next update time.

## Examples

> The issue remains under investigation while service is restored.

> The issue remains under investigation with the provider.
