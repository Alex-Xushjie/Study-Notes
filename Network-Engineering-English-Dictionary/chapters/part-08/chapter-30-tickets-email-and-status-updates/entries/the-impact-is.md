# The impact is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement describing the effect of an issue.

## Usage and distinctions

Describe user or service impact separately from the suspected cause.

## Examples

> The impact is intermittent access to the service.

> The impact is limited to one office.
