# We are monitoring …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that the team is watching a defined condition.

## Usage and distinctions

State what is being observed and the relevant monitoring period.

## Examples

> We are monitoring the link for further errors.

> We are monitoring application response time after the change.
