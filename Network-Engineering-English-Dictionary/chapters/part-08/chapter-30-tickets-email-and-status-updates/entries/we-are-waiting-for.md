# We are waiting for …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A status statement identifying a dependency.

## Usage and distinctions

State the owner, request time, and next follow-up when useful.

## Examples

> We are waiting for the provider’s test results.

> We are waiting for access to the equipment room.
