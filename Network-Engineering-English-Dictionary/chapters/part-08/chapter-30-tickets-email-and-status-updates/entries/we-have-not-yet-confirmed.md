# We have not yet confirmed …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement identifying a point that remains uncertain.

## Usage and distinctions

Name what is unknown and the next action to obtain evidence.

## Examples

> We have not yet confirmed the cause of the packet loss.

> We have not yet confirmed whether both circuits share a path.
