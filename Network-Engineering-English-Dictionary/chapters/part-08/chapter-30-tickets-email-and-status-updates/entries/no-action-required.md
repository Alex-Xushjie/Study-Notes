# No action is required at this time.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that the recipient currently has no task to perform.

## Usage and distinctions

Use only when the recipient truly has no requested action.

## Examples

> No action is required at this time; we will provide another update at 14:00 UTC.

> No action is required unless the provider reports a further issue.
