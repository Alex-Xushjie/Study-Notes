# The cause has not yet been confirmed.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that the underlying cause remains unknown.

## Usage and distinctions

Separate service status from cause and state the next investigation step.

## Examples

> The cause has not yet been confirmed; the team is reviewing the logs.

> Service is restored, but the cause has not yet been confirmed.
