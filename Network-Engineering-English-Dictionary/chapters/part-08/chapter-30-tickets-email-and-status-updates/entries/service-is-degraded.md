# The service is degraded.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that service quality is reduced but some function remains.

## Usage and distinctions

Explain the observed degradation and affected scope.

## Examples

> The service is degraded for users on the wireless network.

> The service remains available, but response times are elevated.
