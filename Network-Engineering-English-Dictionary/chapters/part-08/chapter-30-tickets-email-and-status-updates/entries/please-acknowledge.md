# Please acknowledge receipt.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request for the recipient to confirm receiving a message or item.

## Usage and distinctions

Acknowledging receipt is not the same as approving the content.

## Examples

> Please acknowledge receipt of the updated plan.

> Please acknowledge receipt by 17:00 UTC.
