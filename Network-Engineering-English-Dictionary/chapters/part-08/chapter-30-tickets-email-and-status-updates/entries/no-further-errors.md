# No further errors have been observed.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement reporting the absence of observed errors within a period.

## Usage and distinctions

Always identify the observation window and source when possible.

## Examples

> No further errors have been observed since 10:00 UTC.

> No further errors were observed during the 30-minute test.
