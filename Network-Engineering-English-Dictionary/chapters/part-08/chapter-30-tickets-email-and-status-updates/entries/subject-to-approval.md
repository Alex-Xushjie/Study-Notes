# The change is subject to approval.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement that an action cannot proceed until the required authorization is obtained.

## Usage and distinctions

Identify the approval authority and scope; do not imply approval has already been granted.

## Examples

> The maintenance extension is subject to approval.

> The proposed route change is subject to approval.
