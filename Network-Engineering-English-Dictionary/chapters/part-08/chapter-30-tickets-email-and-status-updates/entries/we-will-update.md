# We will provide an update by …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A commitment to communicate by a specified time.

## Usage and distinctions

Include a clear date and time zone when needed; communicate even if there is no new conclusion.

## Examples

> We will provide an update by 14:00 UTC.

> We will provide an update by 10:00 China Standard Time.
