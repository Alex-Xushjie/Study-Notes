# We observed … between … and …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement locating an observation within a defined time interval.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify the measurement point and time zone.

## Examples

> We observed packet loss between 09:10 and 09:20 UTC.

> We observed repeated resets between 14:00 and 14:15 UTC.
