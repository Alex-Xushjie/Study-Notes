# The next step is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement identifying the action that follows.

## Usage and distinctions

Add an owner and timing if the next step is a commitment.

## Examples

> The next step is to check the provider handoff.

> The next step is to compare packet captures at both ends.
