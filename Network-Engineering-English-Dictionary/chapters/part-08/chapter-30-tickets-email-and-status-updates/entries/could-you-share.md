# Could you share …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A polite request for relevant information or a document.

## Usage and distinctions

Name the item and the reason it is needed when context is unclear.

## Examples

> Could you share the latest topology diagram?

> Could you share the provider case number?
