# The affected service is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement identifying the service impacted by an event.

## Usage and distinctions

Describe the service from the recipient’s perspective and state the known scope.

## Examples

> The affected service is branch access to the trading application.

> The affected service is DNS resolution for the guest network.
