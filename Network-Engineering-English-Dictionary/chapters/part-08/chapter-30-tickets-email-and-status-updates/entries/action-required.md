# Action required: …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A label identifying a request that needs action.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify the action, responsible person, and deadline.

## Examples

> Action required: confirm the maintenance window.

> Action required: review the proposed route policy.
