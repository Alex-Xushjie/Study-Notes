# The change was completed at …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 30

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A statement reporting completion time for a defined change.

## Usage and distinctions

State whether post-change validation also passed.

## Examples

> The change was completed at 22:35 UTC.

> The configuration change was completed within the approved window.
