# Could you elaborate on …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A polite request for a fuller explanation of a specific point.

## Usage and distinctions

Name the point that needs more detail; elaborate on is more natural than elaborate about in this construction.

## Examples

> Could you elaborate on the proposed failover sequence?

> Could you elaborate on the dependency with the provider?
