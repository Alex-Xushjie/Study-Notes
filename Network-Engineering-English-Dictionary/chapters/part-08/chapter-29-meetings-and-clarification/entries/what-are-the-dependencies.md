# What are the dependencies?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to identify conditions or work needed before another activity.

## Usage and distinctions

Follow with owners and dates for each dependency.

## Examples

> What are the dependencies for the migration?

> What are the dependencies on the carrier?
