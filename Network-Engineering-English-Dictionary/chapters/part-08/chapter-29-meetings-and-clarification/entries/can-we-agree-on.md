# Can we agree on …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A proposal to establish a shared decision or definition.

## Usage and distinctions

State the precise decision and record the result.

## Examples

> Can we agree on the acceptance criteria?

> Can we agree on the next update time?
