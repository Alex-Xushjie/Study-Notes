# How will we measure …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question asking how an outcome will be assessed.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify the metric, observation points, interval, and success criteria.

## Examples

> How will we measure service recovery time?

> How will we measure the success of the pilot?
