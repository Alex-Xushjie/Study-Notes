# What are we deciding today?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question clarifying the decision expected from a discussion.

## Usage and distinctions

State the choices and decision authority when they are known.

## Examples

> What are we deciding today: the topology or the vendor?

> Before we continue, what are we deciding today?
