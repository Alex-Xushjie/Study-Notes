# Could you distinguish between … and …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to explain the difference between two concepts.

## Usage and distinctions

Use between with two concepts and specify why the distinction matters.

## Examples

> Could you distinguish between the access window and maintenance window?

> Could you distinguish between the measured result and the target?
