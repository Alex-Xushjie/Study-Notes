# What is the desired outcome?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question identifying what a group is trying to achieve.

## Usage and distinctions

An outcome should be distinguished from a proposed action or implementation.

## Examples

> What is the desired outcome of this meeting?

> What is the desired outcome of the design review?
