# Could you clarify …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A polite request for more precise information.

## Usage and distinctions

Pattern: Could you clarify [the point]? Name the specific uncertainty.

## Examples
