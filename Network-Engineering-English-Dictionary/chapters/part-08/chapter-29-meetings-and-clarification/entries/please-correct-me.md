# Please correct me if I am wrong.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An invitation for another person to correct an understanding.

## Usage and distinctions

Use when a statement is tentative; avoid presenting it as a verified fact.

## Examples

> The provider owns the handoff, but please correct me if I am wrong.

> My understanding is that both links share the same path; please correct me if I am wrong.
