# The next step is to …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression identifying the next action.

## Usage and distinctions

Add an owner and time when a specific commitment is needed.

## Examples

> The next step is to verify the route on the branch router.

> The next step is to request the provider test results.
