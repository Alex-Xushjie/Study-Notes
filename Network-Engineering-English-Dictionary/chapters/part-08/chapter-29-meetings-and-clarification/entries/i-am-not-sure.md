# I am not sure whether …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression that explicitly identifies uncertainty.

## Usage and distinctions

State the uncertainty and propose how to verify it.

## Examples

> I am not sure whether both circuits use separate paths.

> I am not sure whether the subnet is already allocated.
