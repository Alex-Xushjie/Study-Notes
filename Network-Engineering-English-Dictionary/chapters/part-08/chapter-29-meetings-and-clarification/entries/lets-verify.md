# Let’s verify …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A proposal to check a fact or condition.

## Usage and distinctions

Use to move the discussion from assumption to an observable check.

## Examples

> Let’s verify the next-hop reachability.

> Let’s verify the provider handoff before closing the issue.
