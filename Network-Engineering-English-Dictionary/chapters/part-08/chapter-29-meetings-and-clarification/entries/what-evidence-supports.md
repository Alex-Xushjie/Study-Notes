# What evidence supports …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question asking what observations justify a claim.

## Usage and distinctions

Use a neutral tone and distinguish observed facts from interpretation.

## Examples

> What evidence supports the proposed capacity estimate?

> What evidence supports the suspected root cause?
