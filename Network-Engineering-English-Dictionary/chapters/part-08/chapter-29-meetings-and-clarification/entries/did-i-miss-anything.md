# Did I miss anything?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question inviting others to add omitted information.

## Usage and distinctions

Useful after a concrete summary; identify the scope of the review.

## Examples

> Those are the agreed actions. Did I miss anything?

> I have listed the affected sites. Did I miss anything?
