# Could you confirm …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A polite request to verify a detail.

## Usage and distinctions

Pattern: Could you confirm [detail]? Use it when a specific fact needs verification.

## Examples

> Could you confirm the interface number?

> Could you confirm the time zone for the meeting?
