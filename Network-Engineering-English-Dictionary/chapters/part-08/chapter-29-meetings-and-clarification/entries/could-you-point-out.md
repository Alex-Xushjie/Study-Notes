# Could you point out …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to identify something in a document, image, or system.

## Usage and distinctions

Use when a visual reference is available and identify the relevant artifact.

## Examples

> Could you point out the provider handoff on the diagram?

> Could you point out where the two paths share power?
