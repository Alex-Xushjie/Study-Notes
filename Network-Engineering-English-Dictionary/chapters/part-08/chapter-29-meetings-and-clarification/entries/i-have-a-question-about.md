# I have a question about …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A neutral way to introduce a question.

## Usage and distinctions

Follow immediately with the specific question.

## Examples

> I have a question about the return path.

> I have a question about the proposed maintenance window.
