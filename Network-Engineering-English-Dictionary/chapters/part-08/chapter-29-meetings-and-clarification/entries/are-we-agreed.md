# Are we agreed on …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question asking whether participants accept a stated point.

## Usage and distinctions

State the exact decision; silence alone may not establish approval.

## Examples

> Are we agreed on the maintenance scope?

> Are we agreed on the acceptance criteria?
