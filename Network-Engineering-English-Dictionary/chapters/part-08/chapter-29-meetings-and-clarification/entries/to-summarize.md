# To summarize, …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An introduction to a concise recap of key points.

## Usage and distinctions

Separate confirmed decisions from open questions in the summary.

## Examples

> To summarize, the primary circuit is restored and the backup remains under test.

> To summarize, the design needs two independent power sources.
