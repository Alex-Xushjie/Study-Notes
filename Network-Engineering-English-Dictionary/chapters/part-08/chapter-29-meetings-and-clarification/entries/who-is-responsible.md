# Who is responsible for …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question identifying the owner of an activity.

## Usage and distinctions

Use to establish accountability; confirm the owner and deadline in the record.

## Examples

> Who is responsible for updating the diagram?

> Who is responsible for contacting the provider?
