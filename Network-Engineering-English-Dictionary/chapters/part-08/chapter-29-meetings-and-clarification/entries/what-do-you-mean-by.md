# What do you mean by …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A direct request for the intended meaning of a term.

## Usage and distinctions

Use a neutral tone and quote the ambiguous term.

## Examples

> What do you mean by “fully redundant” in this design?

> What do you mean by “available” in the report?
