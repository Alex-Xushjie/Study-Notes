# What is the scope of …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to define the boundaries of a task or decision.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify the activity and ask what is included and excluded.

## Examples

> What is the scope of the planned maintenance?

> What is the scope of the acceptance test?
