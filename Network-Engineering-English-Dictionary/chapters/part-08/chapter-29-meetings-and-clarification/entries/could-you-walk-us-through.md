# Could you walk us through …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request for someone to explain a process or sequence.

## Usage and distinctions

Use for a step-by-step explanation; specify the area of interest.

## Examples

> Could you walk us through the failover sequence?

> Could you walk us through the proposed topology?
