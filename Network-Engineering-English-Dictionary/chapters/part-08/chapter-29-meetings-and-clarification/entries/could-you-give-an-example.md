# Could you give an example?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request for a concrete illustration of a point.

## Usage and distinctions

Examples help expose ambiguity in abstract requirements.

## Examples

> Could you give an example of traffic that should be blocked?

> Could you give an example of the expected alert?
