# Let me check my understanding.

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An introduction to a summary that tests whether everyone shares the same understanding.

## Usage and distinctions

Useful after a complex explanation; follow with the exact point to be confirmed.

## Examples

> Let me check my understanding: the service is restored, but monitoring remains incomplete.

> Let me check my understanding: the second circuit is out of scope.
