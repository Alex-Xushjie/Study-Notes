# Can we take this offline?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A suggestion to continue a detailed discussion outside the current meeting.

## Usage and distinctions

The phrase means continuing separately; it does not imply disconnecting the network.

## Examples

> Can we take the configuration details offline after the meeting?

> Can we take this offline and report the decision back to the group?
