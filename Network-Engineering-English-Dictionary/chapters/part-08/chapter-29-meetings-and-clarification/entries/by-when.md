# By when do we need to …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question identifying the deadline for an activity.

## Usage and distinctions

State a date and time zone when the deadline matters across regions.

## Examples

> By when do we need to approve the change?

> By when do we need to provide the circuit details?
