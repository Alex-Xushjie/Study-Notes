# Could you define …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to explain the intended meaning or boundary of a term.

## Usage and distinctions

Ask for a shared definition when a term may be interpreted differently.

## Examples

> Could you define what “critical service” means here?

> Could you define the scope of the availability target?
