# Could you repeat that?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A polite request to hear a statement again.

## Usage and distinctions

Specify which part was unclear rather than asking the speaker to repeat everything.

## Examples

> Could you repeat the interface number?

> Could you repeat the last point about the timing?
