# Is there a deadline for …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A question asking whether an action must be completed by a specific time.

## Usage and distinctions

Ask for the date, time, and time zone when relevant.

## Examples

> Is there a deadline for submitting the change request?

> Is there a deadline for the circuit order?
