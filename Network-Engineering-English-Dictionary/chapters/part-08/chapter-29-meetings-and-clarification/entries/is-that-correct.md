# Is that correct?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A short question asking whether a stated understanding is accurate.

## Usage and distinctions

Place it immediately after the specific statement being checked.

## Examples

> The affected interface is port 12. Is that correct?

> The window starts at 22:00 UTC. Is that correct?
