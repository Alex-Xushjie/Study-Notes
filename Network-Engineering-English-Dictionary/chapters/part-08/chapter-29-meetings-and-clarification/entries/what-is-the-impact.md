# What is the expected impact?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to identify the likely effect on users or services.

## Usage and distinctions

Ask for affected services, duration, and uncertainty when relevant.

## Examples

> What is the expected impact on branch users?

> What is the expected impact if the primary link fails?
