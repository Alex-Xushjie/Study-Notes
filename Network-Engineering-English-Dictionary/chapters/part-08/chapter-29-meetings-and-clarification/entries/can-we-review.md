# Can we review …?

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A request to examine a topic or artifact together.

## Usage and distinctions

State what decision or concern the review should address.

## Examples

> Can we review the rollback criteria?

> Can we review the current topology diagram?
