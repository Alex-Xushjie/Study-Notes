# Let’s separate … from …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 29

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A suggestion to distinguish two issues or concepts.

## Usage and distinctions

Use to organize a discussion without assuming the issues are unrelated.

## Examples

> Let’s separate the physical link issue from the routing issue.

> Let’s separate confirmed impact from the suspected cause.
