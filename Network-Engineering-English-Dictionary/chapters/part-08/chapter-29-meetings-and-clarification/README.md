> There is an open question about the handoff location.

> To summarize, Alex will confirm the circuit details by Friday.

## Quick review

1. What should follow “Could you clarify …?”
2. Does “take this offline” mean disconnect the network?
3. What makes a next step actionable?
4. Should an uncertain statement be presented as confirmed?

**Answers**

1. The specific point that needs explanation.
2. No; it means continue the discussion separately.
3. A clear action, owner, and time when needed.
4. No; state the uncertainty and how it will be checked.
