## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [Are we agreed on …?](entries/are-we-agreed.md) | Ask whether participants accept a stated point. |
| [To summarize, …](entries/to-summarize.md) | Introduce a concise recap. |
| [The next step is to …](entries/the-next-step-is.md) | Identify the next action. |
| [Who is responsible for …?](entries/who-is-responsible.md) | Identify the owner of an activity. |

## Examples and usage

> Are we agreed on the maintenance scope?

> To summarize, the backup remains under test.

> The next step is to verify the branch route.

> Who is responsible for updating the diagram?
