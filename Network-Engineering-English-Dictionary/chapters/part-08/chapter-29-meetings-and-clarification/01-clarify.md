## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [Could you clarify …?](entries/could-you-clarify.md) | Ask for a more precise explanation. |
| [Could you confirm …?](entries/could-you-confirm.md) | Ask someone to verify a specific detail. |
| [If I understand correctly, …](entries/if-i-understand-correctly.md) | Restate an understanding for correction. |
| [Could you give an example?](entries/could-you-give-an-example.md) | Ask for a concrete illustration. |

## Examples and usage

> Could you clarify which site is affected?

> Could you confirm the interface number?

> If I understand correctly, the change affects one branch.

> Could you give an example of the traffic that should be blocked?
