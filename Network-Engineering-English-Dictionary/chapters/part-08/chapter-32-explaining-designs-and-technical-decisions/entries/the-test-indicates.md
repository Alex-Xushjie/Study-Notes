# The test indicates …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression reporting a test-supported interpretation.

## Usage and distinctions

State the test conditions and remaining uncertainty.

## Examples

> The test indicates that the new path supports the required throughput.

> The test indicates that the route is accepted under the tested policy.
