# The decision needed is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression identifying a pending decision for stakeholders.

## Usage and distinctions

State who decides, the options, and the deadline.

## Examples

> The decision needed is whether to fund the second circuit.

> The decision needed is which maintenance window to use.
