# The trade-off is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression explaining a compromise between competing objectives.

## Usage and distinctions

Identify what improves, what is sacrificed, and the relevant conditions.

## Examples

> The trade-off is higher cost for greater path diversity.

> The trade-off is additional complexity for more flexible routing.
