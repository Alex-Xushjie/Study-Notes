# For the business, this means …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression translating technical behavior into a relevant business effect.

## Usage and distinctions

Avoid unsupported guarantees and explain the conditions of the outcome.

## Examples

> For the business, this means the branch can continue operating after a single circuit failure.

> For the business, this means a longer maintenance interruption.
