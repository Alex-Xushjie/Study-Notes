# The limitation is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression describing a constraint or weakness of an option.

## Usage and distinctions

Be specific about scope and avoid implying that every use case has the same limitation.

## Examples

> The limitation is that both links share the same power source.

> The limitation is the available rack capacity.
