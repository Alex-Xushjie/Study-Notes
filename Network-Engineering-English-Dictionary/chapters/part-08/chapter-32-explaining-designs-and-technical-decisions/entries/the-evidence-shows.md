# The evidence shows …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression introducing a conclusion supported by observations.

## Usage and distinctions

Describe the test scope and avoid claiming more than the evidence supports.

## Examples

> The evidence shows that the backup link carried traffic during the test.

> The evidence shows that the gateway failed over within the measured interval.
