# The assumption is that …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression identifying a condition treated as true but not yet verified.

## Usage and distinctions

Assign an owner to validate the assumption.

## Examples

> The assumption is that the two circuits use separate pathways.

> The assumption is that rack power will be available before installation.
