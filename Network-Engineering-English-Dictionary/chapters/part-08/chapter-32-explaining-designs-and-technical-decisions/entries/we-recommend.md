# We recommend …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression stating a proposed choice.

## Usage and distinctions

Support the recommendation with requirements, evidence, trade-offs, and residual risk.

## Examples

> We recommend the dual-provider design for the critical site.

> We recommend testing the failover in a maintenance window.
