# The goal is to …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression describing the intended outcome.

## Usage and distinctions

A goal may need measurable criteria before it can be tested.

## Examples

> The goal is to reduce recovery time.

> The goal is to simplify operations across the branches.
