# The requirement is to …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression stating the need a design must satisfy.

## Usage and distinctions

State a verifiable need and distinguish it from the proposed solution.

## Examples

> The requirement is to maintain branch connectivity during a link failure.

> The requirement is to support the expected peak traffic.
