# The remaining risk is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression stating a risk that persists after mitigation.

## Usage and distinctions

Identify the risk owner and any proposed follow-up.

## Examples

> The remaining risk is the shared building power supply.

> The remaining risk is that both carriers use the same conduit.
