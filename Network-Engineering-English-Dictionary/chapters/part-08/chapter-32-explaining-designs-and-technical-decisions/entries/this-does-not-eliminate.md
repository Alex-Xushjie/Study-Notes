# This does not eliminate …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression identifying a remaining risk or limitation.

## Usage and distinctions

Use to make residual risks and assumptions clear.

## Examples

> This does not eliminate the shared power dependency.

> This does not eliminate the need for monitoring.
