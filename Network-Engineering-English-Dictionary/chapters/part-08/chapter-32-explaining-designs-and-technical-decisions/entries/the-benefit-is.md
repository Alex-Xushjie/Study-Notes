# The benefit is …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression explaining a positive effect of an option.

## Usage and distinctions

State the conditions under which the benefit is expected.

## Examples

> The benefit is a shorter recovery time.

> The benefit is simpler operations.
