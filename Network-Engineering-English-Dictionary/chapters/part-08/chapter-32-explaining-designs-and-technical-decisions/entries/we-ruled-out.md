# We ruled out … because …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression explaining why an option was rejected.

## Usage and distinctions

Avoid dismissive language; identify the relevant constraint or evidence.

## Examples

> We ruled out the single-circuit option because it does not meet the availability requirement.

> We ruled out that device because its interface capacity is insufficient.
