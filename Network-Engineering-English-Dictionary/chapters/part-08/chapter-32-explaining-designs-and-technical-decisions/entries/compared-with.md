# Compared with …, …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression comparing two approaches or states.

## Usage and distinctions

Compare like-for-like conditions and identify the measurement or criterion.

## Examples

> Compared with the existing design, the proposal has two independent uplinks.

> Compared with the first option, this design has higher operational complexity.
