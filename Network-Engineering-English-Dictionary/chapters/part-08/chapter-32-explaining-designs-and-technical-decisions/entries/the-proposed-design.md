# The proposed design uses …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression introducing a proposed architectural approach.

## Usage and distinctions

Explain how the proposal addresses the relevant requirements.

## Examples

> The proposed design uses diverse provider circuits.

> The proposed design uses a leaf-spine topology.
