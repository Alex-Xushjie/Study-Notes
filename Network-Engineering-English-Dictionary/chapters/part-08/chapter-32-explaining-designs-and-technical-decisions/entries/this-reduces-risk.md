# This reduces the risk of …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression describing how a design may mitigate a specific risk.

## Usage and distinctions

Avoid claiming that a control eliminates a risk unless evidence supports that conclusion.

## Examples

> This reduces the risk of a single circuit failure interrupting the branch.

> This reduces the risk of address conflicts.
