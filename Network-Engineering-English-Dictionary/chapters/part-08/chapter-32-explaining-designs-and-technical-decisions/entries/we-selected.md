# We selected … because …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression explaining a decision and its rationale.

## Usage and distinctions

Connect the reason to agreed criteria and documented evidence.

## Examples

> We selected the routed design because it meets the recovery target.

> We selected the smaller option because the site has limited capacity needs.
