# We have not tested …

**Professional expression** · Chapter 32

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An expression explicitly identifying an unverified condition.

## Usage and distinctions

Use to prevent a limited test result from being mistaken for complete validation.

## Examples

> We have not tested failover during a provider outage.

> We have not tested the design at peak load.
