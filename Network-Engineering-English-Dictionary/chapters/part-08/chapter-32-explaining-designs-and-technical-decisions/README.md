## Say it at work

> The requirement is to maintain service during a single circuit failure.

> We recommend the dual-provider option because it addresses that requirement.

> The trade-off is additional cost and operational complexity.

> The remaining risk is the shared building power supply.

## Quick review

1. Is a proposed design the same as a requirement?
2. Does a successful test prove every failure scenario?
3. What should support a recommendation?
4. Should an assumption be presented as verified?

**Answers**

1. No; the requirement is the need, while the design is a proposed solution.
2. No; state the scope and untested conditions.
3. Requirements, evidence, trade-offs, and residual risks.
4. No; identify it and assign a verification action.
