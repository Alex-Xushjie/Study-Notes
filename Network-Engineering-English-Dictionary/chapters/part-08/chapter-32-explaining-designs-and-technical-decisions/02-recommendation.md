## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [We recommend …](entries/we-recommend.md) | State a proposed choice. |
| [The evidence shows …](entries/the-evidence-shows.md) | Introduce an evidence-supported conclusion. |
| [We have not tested …](entries/we-have-not-tested.md) | Identify an unverified condition. |
| [The remaining risk is …](entries/the-residual-risk.md) | State a risk that persists after mitigation. |

## Examples and usage

> We recommend the dual-provider design for the critical site.

> The evidence shows that the backup link carried traffic during the test.

> We have not tested failover during a provider outage.

> The remaining risk is the shared building power supply.
