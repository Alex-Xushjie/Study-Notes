# incident follow-up action

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A task assigned after or during an incident to address a finding or improve resilience.

## Usage and distinctions

An action should be specific and trackable; a proposed action is not complete until verified.

## Examples

> The team assigned a follow-up action to improve monitoring.

> Record the owner and due date.
