# service outage

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A period during which a service is unavailable.

## Usage and distinctions

An outage differs from degraded service, although incident classifications vary by organization.

## Examples

> The branch experienced a network outage.

> Confirm the outage start and restoration times.
