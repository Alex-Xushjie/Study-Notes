# next update time

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The agreed or communicated time for the next status report.

## Usage and distinctions

Include a time zone or UTC offset when communicating across regions.

## Examples

> The next update will be at 14:00 UTC.

> Provide an update even if the investigation is still ongoing.
