# incident mitigation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An action that reduces the impact or likelihood of further harm during an incident.

## Usage and distinctions

A mitigation may be temporary and does not necessarily resolve the underlying cause.

## Examples

> The team mitigated the issue by moving traffic to the alternate path.

> Record the mitigation and remaining risks.
