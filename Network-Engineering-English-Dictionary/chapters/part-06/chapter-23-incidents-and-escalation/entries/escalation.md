# incident escalation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The referral of an incident to another person or team for attention, expertise, or authority.

## Usage and distinctions

Escalation should identify the recipient, reason, urgency, and expected next action.

## Examples

> The team escalated the circuit issue to the provider.

> Include the impact and evidence in the escalation.
