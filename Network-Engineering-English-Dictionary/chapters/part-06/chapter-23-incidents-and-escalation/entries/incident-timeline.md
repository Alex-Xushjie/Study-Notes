# incident timeline

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A chronological record of incident observations, actions, and decisions.

## Usage and distinctions

A timeline should distinguish observed event times from reporting or logging times when known.

## Examples

> The timeline records the alert and the failover.

> Include timestamps and time zones in the timeline.
