# incident priority

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A ranking used to decide the order or urgency of incident response.

## Usage and distinctions

Priority may consider severity, urgency, and business context according to local policy.

## Examples

> The ticket has a high incident priority.

> Review priority if the scope or impact changes.
