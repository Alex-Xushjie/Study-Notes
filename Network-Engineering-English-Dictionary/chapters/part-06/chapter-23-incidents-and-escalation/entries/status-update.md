# status update

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A concise report of current progress or condition.

## Usage and distinctions

Use precise wording and avoid implying completion before validation.

## Examples

> The status update states that service is partially restored.

> Explain what is known and what the team is doing next.
