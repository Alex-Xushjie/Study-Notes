# technical escalation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An escalation to obtain additional technical expertise or resources.

## Usage and distinctions

Share diagnostic data according to organizational policy and remove secrets where appropriate.
