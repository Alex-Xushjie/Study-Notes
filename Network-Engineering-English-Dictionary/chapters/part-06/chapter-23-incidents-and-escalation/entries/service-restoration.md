**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The return of a service to an agreed usable condition.

## Usage and distinctions

A workaround can restore service even while root-cause investigation continues.
