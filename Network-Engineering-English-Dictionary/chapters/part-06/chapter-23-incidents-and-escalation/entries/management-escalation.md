# management escalation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An escalation to obtain management attention, decisions, or resources.

## Usage and distinctions

Management escalation is distinct from escalation for technical expertise.

## Examples

> The prolonged outage triggered management escalation.

> Explain the business impact and decision needed.
