# incident update

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A communication describing current incident impact, confirmed facts, actions, and expected next update.

## Usage and distinctions

Avoid presenting an unverified hypothesis as a confirmed cause.

## Examples

> The incident update identifies the affected site.

> Include the next update time and time zone.
