# on-call engineer

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A person assigned to respond to operational issues during a specified period.

## Usage and distinctions

Schedules, response expectations, and escalation rules are organization-specific.

## Examples

> The on-call engineer acknowledged the alert.

> Check the on-call schedule before escalating.
