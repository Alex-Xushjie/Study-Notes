# time of restoration

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The time when the agreed service condition was restored.

## Usage and distinctions

Service restoration and permanent root-cause correction may occur at different times.

## Examples

> The time of restoration was confirmed after the application test.

> Record which validation established restoration.
