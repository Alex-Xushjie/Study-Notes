# service incident

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An unplanned interruption or degradation of a service.

## Usage and distinctions

An incident can be caused by different technical conditions; impact and cause should be documented separately.

## Examples

> The team opened an incident for the connectivity outage.

> Record the incident start time and affected service.
