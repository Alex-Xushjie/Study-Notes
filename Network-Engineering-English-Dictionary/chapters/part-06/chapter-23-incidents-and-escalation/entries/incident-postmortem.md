# incident postmortem

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 23

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A structured review of an incident, its impact, response, causes, and improvements.

## Usage and distinctions

A useful review distinguishes facts, hypotheses, contributing factors, and assigned actions.

## Examples

> The team scheduled a postmortem after restoration.

> Document evidence and follow-up actions in the postmortem.
