## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [incident mitigation](entries/mitigation.md) | An action that reduces incident impact. |
| [service restoration](entries/service-restoration.md) | Return of a service to an agreed usable condition. |
| [incident escalation](entries/escalation.md) | Referral for attention, expertise, or authority. |
| [incident update](entries/incident-update.md) | A report of impact, facts, actions and next update. |

## Examples and usage

> The team mitigated the issue using an alternate path.

> Validate service from the affected location.

> Include the impact and evidence in the escalation.

> Provide the next update time and time zone.
