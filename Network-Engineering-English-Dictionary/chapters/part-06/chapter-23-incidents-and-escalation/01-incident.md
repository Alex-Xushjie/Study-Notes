## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [service incident](entries/incident.md) | An unplanned interruption or degradation of service. |
| [service outage](entries/outage.md) | A period of service unavailability. |
| [service degradation](entries/service-degradation.md) | Reduced quality or performance while some service remains available. |
| [incident severity](entries/incident-severity.md) | A classification based on defined impact or urgency criteria. |

## Examples and usage

> The incident affects two branches.

> Users report degraded application performance.

> Record the detection time and affected service.

> Review severity if the impact changes.
