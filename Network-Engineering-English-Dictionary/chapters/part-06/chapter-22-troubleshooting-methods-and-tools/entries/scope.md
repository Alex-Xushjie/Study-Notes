# incident scope

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The boundaries of the affected users, services, locations, or time period.

## Usage and distinctions

Scope can change as more evidence becomes available.

## Examples

> The issue affects one branch and two applications.

> Confirm the incident scope before changing the network.
