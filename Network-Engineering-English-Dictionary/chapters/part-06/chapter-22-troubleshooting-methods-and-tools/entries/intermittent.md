# intermittent issue

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A problem that occurs occasionally rather than continuously.

## Usage and distinctions

An intermittent symptom may be difficult to reproduce and requires time-correlated evidence.

## Examples

> The client reports intermittent disconnections.

> Record precise timestamps and conditions for each occurrence.
