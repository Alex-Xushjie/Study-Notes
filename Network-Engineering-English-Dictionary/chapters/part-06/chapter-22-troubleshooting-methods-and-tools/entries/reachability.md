# network reachability

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The ability of a source to communicate with a destination under specified conditions.

## Usage and distinctions

Reachability should identify the protocol, direction, source, destination, and time.

## Examples

> The router has IP reachability to the gateway.

> Test reachability from the affected source.
