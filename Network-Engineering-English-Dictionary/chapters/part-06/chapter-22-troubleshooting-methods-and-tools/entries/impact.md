# service impact

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The effect of a problem or change on users or services.

## Usage and distinctions

Impact is different from the suspected technical cause.

## Examples

> The impact is intermittent access to the application.

> Quantify the affected users and duration where possible.
