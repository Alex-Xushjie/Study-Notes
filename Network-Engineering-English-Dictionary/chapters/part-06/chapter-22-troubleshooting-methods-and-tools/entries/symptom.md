# incident symptom

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An observable indication that a service or system is not behaving as expected.

## Usage and distinctions

A symptom is not necessarily the underlying cause.

## Examples

> The symptom is intermittent packet loss.

> Describe when and where the symptom occurs.
