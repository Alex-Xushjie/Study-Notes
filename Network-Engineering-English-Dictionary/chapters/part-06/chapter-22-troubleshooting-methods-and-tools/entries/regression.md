# service regression

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A previously working behavior that fails or degrades after a change.

## Usage and distinctions

Temporal correlation alone does not prove the change caused the regression.

## Examples

> The connection failed after the software upgrade.

> Compare the behavior before and after the change.
