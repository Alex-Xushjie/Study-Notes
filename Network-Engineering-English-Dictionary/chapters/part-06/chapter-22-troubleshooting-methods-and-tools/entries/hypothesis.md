# troubleshooting hypothesis

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A proposed explanation that can be tested against evidence.

## Usage and distinctions

A hypothesis remains unconfirmed until evidence supports it and alternatives are considered.

## Examples

> One hypothesis is an MTU mismatch.

> State the expected observation before running the test.
