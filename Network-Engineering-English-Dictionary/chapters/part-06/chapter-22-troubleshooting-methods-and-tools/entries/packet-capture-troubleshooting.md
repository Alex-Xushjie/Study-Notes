# diagnostic packet capture

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A packet recording collected to investigate a specific communication path or behavior.

## Usage and distinctions

A capture shows traffic at its collection point and may omit packets because of filtering, offload, or capture loss.

## Examples

> Capture traffic at the client and server.

> Record the capture filter and observation points.
