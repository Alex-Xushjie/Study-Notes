# known-good reference

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A component, path, or state verified to work under relevant conditions.

## Usage and distinctions

A known-good reference is useful only if its conditions are sufficiently similar.

## Examples

> Compare the affected port with a known-good port.

> Confirm that the two paths are comparable.
