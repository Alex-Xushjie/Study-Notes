# workaround

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An action that reduces the impact of a problem without necessarily removing its underlying cause.

## Usage and distinctions

A workaround may be suitable for restoration while the underlying cause remains under investigation.
