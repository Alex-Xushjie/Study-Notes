# diagnostic evidence

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Observations or records used to support or test an explanation.

## Usage and distinctions

Protect sensitive diagnostic data and distinguish direct observation from interpretation.

## Examples

> The packet captures provide evidence of the reset.

> Record the source, timestamp, and collection point.
