# traceroute

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A diagnostic method that probes the sequence of network hops toward a destination.

## Usage and distinctions

Probe protocols, load balancing, and filtering can affect displayed hops; missing replies do not necessarily mean forwarding stops there.
