# reproduce an issue

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

To make a reported problem occur again under observed conditions.

## Usage and distinctions

Failure to reproduce an intermittent issue does not prove it no longer exists.

## Examples

> The team reproduced the issue in the test environment.

> Record the steps and conditions needed to reproduce it.
