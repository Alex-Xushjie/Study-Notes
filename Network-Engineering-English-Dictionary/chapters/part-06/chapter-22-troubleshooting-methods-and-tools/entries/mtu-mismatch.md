# MTU mismatch

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A difference in supported or configured packet-size limits that can affect a path.

## Usage and distinctions

Interpret results in light of IP version, fragmentation behavior, encapsulation, and filtering.

## Examples

> The path may have an MTU mismatch.

> Test packet sizes and verify the relevant interfaces.
