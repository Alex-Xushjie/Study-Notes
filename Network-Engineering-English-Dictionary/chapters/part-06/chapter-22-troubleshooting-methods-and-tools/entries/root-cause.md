# root cause

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 22

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An underlying condition whose correction prevents or reduces recurrence of the defined problem.

## Usage and distinctions

Root-cause conclusions depend on the defined incident and available evidence.

## Examples

> The investigation identified the root cause.

> Document the evidence supporting the root cause.
