## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [network telemetry](entries/telemetry.md) | Measurements or state collected from network systems. |
| [telemetry collector](entries/collector.md) | A service that receives telemetry. |
| [monitoring metric](entries/metric.md) | A numeric measurement or derived value. |
| [monitoring dashboard](entries/dashboard.md) | A visual display of selected monitoring data. |

## Examples and usage

> The device sends telemetry to the collector.

> Check the metric’s unit and sampling interval.

> Verify that the dashboard data is current.

> A collector receiving data does not guarantee that every metric is present.
