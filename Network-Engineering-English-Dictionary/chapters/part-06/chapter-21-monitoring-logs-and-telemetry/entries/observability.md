# network observability

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 21

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The ability to understand a network’s internal behavior from its external outputs and collected data.

## Usage and distinctions

Observability is a capability, not a single tool or protocol.

## Examples

> Telemetry and logs improve network observability.

> Use multiple evidence sources to investigate the failure.
