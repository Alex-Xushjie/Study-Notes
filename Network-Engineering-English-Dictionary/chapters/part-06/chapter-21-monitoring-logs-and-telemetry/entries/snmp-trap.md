# SNMP trap

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 21

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An unsolicited SNMP notification sent by an agent to a manager.

## Usage and distinctions

Trap delivery characteristics depend on the protocol and transport; an inform has different acknowledgment behavior.

## Examples

> The switch sends an SNMP trap after the interface changes state.

> Check whether the collector received the trap.
