# network telemetry

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 21

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Measurements or state information collected from network systems for observation or analysis.

## Usage and distinctions

Telemetry can be collected by polling or streaming; models and transport methods vary.

## Examples

> The devices stream telemetry to the collector.

> Check whether telemetry is arriving from the affected router.
