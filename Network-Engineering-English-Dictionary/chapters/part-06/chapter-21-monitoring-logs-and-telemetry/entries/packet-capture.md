# packet capture

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 21

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The recording of packets observed at a specified network point.

## Usage and distinctions

A capture reflects what was observed at its location; filtering, offload, and dropped capture packets can affect interpretation.

## Examples

> The packet capture shows the client’s DNS query.

> Record the capture location and time.
