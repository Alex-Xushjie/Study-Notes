# streaming telemetry

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 21

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Telemetry delivered through a continuing or repeated subscription from a source to a collector.

## Usage and distinctions

Encoding, transport, and update behavior depend on the implementation and subscription.

## Examples

> The router streams interface statistics to the collector.

> Verify the subscription and collector reachability.
