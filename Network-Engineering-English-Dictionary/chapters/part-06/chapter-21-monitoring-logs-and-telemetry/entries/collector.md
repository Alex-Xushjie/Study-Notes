# telemetry collector

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 21

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A service that receives and processes telemetry data.

## Usage and distinctions

A collector receiving data does not guarantee that every expected metric is present or current.

> The telemetry collector receives updates from the switches.
