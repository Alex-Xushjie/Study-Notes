## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [change request](entries/change-request.md) | A documented proposal to modify a system or service. |
| [change approval](entries/change-approval.md) | Authorization to proceed under specified conditions. |
| [change plan](entries/change-plan.md) | The actions, checks, and responsibilities for the change. |
| [maintenance window](entries/maintenance-window.md) | An agreed period for specified maintenance work. |

## Examples and usage

> Document the scope and expected impact.

> Confirm the required approvals and prerequisites.

> Record the baseline before making changes.

> The maintenance window includes a time zone and UTC offset.
