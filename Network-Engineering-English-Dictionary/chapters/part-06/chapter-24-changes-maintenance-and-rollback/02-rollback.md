## Key terms

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [post-change validation](entries/post-change-validation.md) | Checks that confirm expected behavior and identify regressions. |
| [rollback criteria](entries/rollback-criteria.md) | Observable conditions supporting a rollback decision. |
| [rollback plan](entries/rollback-plan.md) | A procedure for returning to an agreed earlier state. |
| [change closure](entries/change-closure.md) | Formal completion after required work and records. |

## Examples and usage

> Validate the application after the change.

> Agree on the rollback criteria and decision deadline.

> Leave enough time for rollback and verification.

> Attach the test results before closing the change.
