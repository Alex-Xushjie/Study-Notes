# change freeze

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A period during which specified changes are restricted.

## Usage and distinctions

Freeze scope and exception authority are defined by the organization.

## Examples

> The organization has a change freeze during the event.

> Check whether the change is exempt from the freeze.
