# change closure

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The formal completion of a change after required work, validation, and records are complete.

## Usage and distinctions

Closure criteria vary by organization and may include documentation and communication.

## Examples

> The change owner closes the request after validation.

> Attach the test results before change closure.
