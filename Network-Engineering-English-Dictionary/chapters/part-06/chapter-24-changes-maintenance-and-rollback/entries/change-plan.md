# change plan

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A documented sequence of actions, checks, and responsibilities for a change.

## Usage and distinctions

A useful plan states scope, dependencies, timing, owners, decision points, and communication.

## Examples

> The change plan includes validation and rollback steps.

> Review the plan with the affected teams.
