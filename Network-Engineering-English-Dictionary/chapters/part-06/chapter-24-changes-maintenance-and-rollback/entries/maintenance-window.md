# maintenance window

**Countable noun.** · Chapter 24 · `technical`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

An agreed period during which specified maintenance work may be performed.

## Usage and distinctions

A maintenance window specifies an agreed period and scope of work. Include a time zone and UTC offset to avoid ambiguity.

## Common combinations

schedule a maintenance window; within the maintenance window; planned service impact

## Examples

> The maintenance window is from 22:00 to 23:00 China Standard Time (UTC+08:00).

> We need a separate approval if the work exceeds the agreed scope.
