# change approval

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Authorization to proceed with a defined change under specified conditions.

## Usage and distinctions

Approval does not replace technical readiness or verification of prerequisites.

## Examples

> The change approval covers the maintenance window and scope.

> Confirm all required approvals before implementation.
