# rollback criteria

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Observable conditions that trigger or support a decision to reverse a change.

## Usage and distinctions

Criteria should specify measurements, thresholds, owner, and timing where applicable.

## Examples

> The rollback criteria include failed connectivity checks.

> Agree on the decision deadline before implementation.
