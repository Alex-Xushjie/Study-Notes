# post-implementation review

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A review of a completed change and its outcome.

## Usage and distinctions

A review should compare the approved plan, actual actions, results, and follow-up ownership.

## Examples

> The team scheduled a post-implementation review.

> Document unexpected impact and improvement actions.
