# rollback decision

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A decision to execute the approved rollback procedure.

## Usage and distinctions

The decision maker and deadline should be specified in the plan.

## Examples

> The change owner made the rollback decision at 22:40.

> Leave enough time to complete rollback and validation.
