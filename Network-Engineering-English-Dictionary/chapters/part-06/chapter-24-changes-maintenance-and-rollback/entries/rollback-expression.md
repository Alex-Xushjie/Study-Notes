# We will roll back if …

**Professional expression for a change plan.** · Chapter 24 · `expression`

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A conditional statement that names the trigger for returning to an agreed previous state.

## Usage and distinctions

Pattern: “We will roll back if [observable trigger].” Use roll back as a verb and rollback as a noun or adjective, as in rollback plan.

## Common combinations

roll back the change; rollback plan; rollback criteria

## Examples

> We will roll back if the agreed connectivity checks fail after the change.

> The rollback decision must be made by 22:40 to leave time for recovery.
