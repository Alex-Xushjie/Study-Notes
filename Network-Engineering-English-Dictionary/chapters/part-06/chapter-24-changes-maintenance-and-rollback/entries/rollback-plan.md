# rollback plan

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A documented procedure for returning to an agreed earlier state after a change.

## Usage and distinctions

A rollback may not reverse every external effect; define its scope and dependencies.

## Examples

> The rollback plan restores the previous routing policy.

> Verify that the rollback can be completed within the window.
