# pre-change check

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A verification performed before a change to establish readiness or baseline state.

## Usage and distinctions

Checks should be specific and include the expected result and evidence.

## Examples

> The pre-change check confirms the backup and neighbor state.

> Record the baseline before making changes.
