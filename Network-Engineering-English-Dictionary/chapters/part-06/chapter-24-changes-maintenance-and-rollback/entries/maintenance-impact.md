# planned service impact

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

The expected effect of planned work on users or services.

## Usage and distinctions

Expected impact is an estimate; actual impact should be monitored and reported.

## Examples

> The planned impact is a brief interruption at the branch.

> Notify affected users according to the change process.
