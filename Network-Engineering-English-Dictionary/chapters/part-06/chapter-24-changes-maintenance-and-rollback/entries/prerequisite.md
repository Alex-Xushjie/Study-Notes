# change prerequisite

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

A condition that must be satisfied before a change proceeds.

## Usage and distinctions

Prerequisites should be observable and assigned to an owner.

## Examples

> A verified configuration backup is a prerequisite.

> Confirm the prerequisites before the maintenance window.
