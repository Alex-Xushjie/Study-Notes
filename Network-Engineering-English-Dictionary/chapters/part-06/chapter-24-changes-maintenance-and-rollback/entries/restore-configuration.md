# restore a configuration

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

To apply an earlier saved configuration or relevant portions of it.

## Usage and distinctions

Restoration procedures differ by device; a backup must be suitable and accessible.

## Examples

> The team restored the previous configuration.

> Validate the service after restoring the configuration.
