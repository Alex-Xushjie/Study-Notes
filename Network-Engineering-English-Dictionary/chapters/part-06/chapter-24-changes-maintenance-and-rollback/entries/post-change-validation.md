# post-change validation

**Network engineering term** · Chapter 24

[Chapter](../README.md) · [English index](../../../../indexes/ENGLISH-INDEX.md)

## Definition

Checks performed after a change to confirm expected behavior and identify regressions.

## Usage and distinctions

Validation should test the intended change and relevant unaffected services.

## Examples

> The post-change validation includes application connectivity.

> Record the results and any exceptions.
